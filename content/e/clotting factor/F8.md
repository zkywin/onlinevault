[[intrinsic clotting pathway]]
> [!tip] Function

> [!info] Location
> made by [[endothelium]] (not by liver)

> [!warning] Structure

> [!example] Relationships
> circulates with [[vWF]] (increases half life)
> activated by [[thrombin]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> give for [[hemophilia A]]
> develops inhibitors over time
> normal in [[cirrhosis]]
