[[pancreatic insufficiency]]
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[EtOH]] [[CF]]
> rare

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[pancreatic insufficiency]]
> → [[splenic vein thrombosis]] → [[gastric varices]]
> "pancreatic ascites" with leakage into [[peritoneum]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> chronic abdominal pain
> [[postprandial pain]]
> improved by leaning forward

> [!question] Objective Findings
> dystrophic [[calcification]] of parenchyma
> ↓[[fecal elastase]]
> ↑[[sudan stain]]
> ↑[[A1C]] ([[pancreatic islet]] damage)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[pancrelipase]]

> [!example] Relationships
> surgery ⚠ contraindicated
> may present similar to [[somatostatinoma]]
