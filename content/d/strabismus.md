
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[eye]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> intermittent is normal in first 4 months

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[amblyopia]] if uncorrected

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[test of skew]]
> eso (turning inward)
> exo (turning outward)
> tropia (obvious, constant deviation)
> phoria (a latent tendency for eye deviation)

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[occlusion therapy]]
> [[cyclopentolate]]

> [!example] Relationships
> differentiated from [[retinoblastoma]] with red light reflex
