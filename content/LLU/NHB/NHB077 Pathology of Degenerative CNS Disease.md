> [!info] Definitions
> dementia: literal translation "madness" or "insanity", colloquial term for Major Cognitive Disorders
> dementing disorder classifications: systems based vs. proteinopathy based. Dr. Deisch will teach proteinopathies-based
> gliosis: glial cell "scarring" proliferation

3R/4R [[tau]] accumulation causes memory loss in [[alzheimer's]] and 4R causes parkinsonisms in [[PSP]].
Uniquely, 3R [[tau]] accumulation causes behavioral issues in [[frontotemporal dementia]] and [[non-pick's frontotemporal lobar degeneration]]

[[alpha synuclein]] accumulation causes memory loss in [[lewy body dementia]] and classical [[parkinson's]] disease, 
Uniquely, [[alpha synuclein]] can cause really severe [[MSA]] that includes autonomic dysfunction.

Infectious [[prions]] are responsible for [[creutzfeld-jacob disease]]
