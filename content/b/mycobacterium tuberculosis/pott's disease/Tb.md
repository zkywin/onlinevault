[[intracellular]] [[gram positive]] [[rods]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> suspect if [[Abx]] refractory

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> inhalation
> children/[[immunocompromised]]
> [[TNFα]] inhibition risks reactivation

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> cord factor → ⦻ lysosome fusion → granuloma
> grows within [[macrophage]] → [[IL-12]] [[IFN-γ]] 
> 
> basilar gelatinous exudate → [[hydrocephalus]]
> 
> PRIMARY [[RLL]] [[calcification]], [[ghon complex]]
> MILIARY disseminated infection (blood, all over)
> SECONDARY [[lung apex]] [[caseous necrosis]] 


> [!danger] Symptoms
> primary [[Tb]] is asymptomatic
> secondary [[Tb]] via [[HIV]] and aging
> [[d/miliary tuberculosis]]
> 
> night sweats, [[hemoptysis]]
> spread to [[bacterial meningitis]], [[kidney]], sterile [[pyuria]]
> disk-sparing [[osteomyelitis]] of [[vertebrae]] "pott's disease" aka tuberculous spondylitis

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[tuberculin skin test]] [[quant gold]] [[IGRA]]
> [[CXR]] to differentiate latent from active
> 
> hilar adenopathy
> [[bronchopneumonia]] on [[XR]]
> [[pleural effusion]] transudate
> [[acid fast]] [[lowenstein jensen agar]]
> fibrous capsule with granulation tissue
> [[caseating granuloma]] with cavitary lesion

> [!abstract] Treatment
> latent: [[isoniazid]] [[rifapentine]]
> active: [[RIPE therapy]]
> 
> HIV: [[isoniazid]] + [[vB6]]
> [[BCG vaccine]]


> [!example] Relationships
> [[fungi]] on the differential
> [[CKD]] can increase reactivation rate mildly
