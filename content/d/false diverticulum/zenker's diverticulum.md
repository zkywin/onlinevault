false diverticulum, pulsion diverticulum
> [!tip] In Short
> throat pouch

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[CN X]] issues
> unrelaxed cricopharyngeal muscle (swallowing)
> at "killian's triangle" between thyropharyngeus and cricopharyngeus

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> high pressure at esophagus pharynx junction
> breakdown of [[muscularis externa]]
> herniation
> [[aspiration pneumonia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[dysphagia]]
> [[bad breath]]
> gurgling throat

> [!question] Objective Findings
> video swallowing study

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[cricopharyngeal myotomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[diverticulosis]]
> [[meckel's diverticulum]]
