acute respiratory distress syndrome
[[restrictive lung]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> stiff lungs + poor O2

> [!info] Etiology
> #1 [[sepsis]]
> [[DIC]], [[aspiration pneumonia]], [[acute pancreatitis]], [[uremia]], trauma, [[drowning]]



> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> damage to the alveolar-[[capillary]] interface
> [[neutrophil]] damage of [[pneumocyte 1]] and [[pneumocyte 2]]
> diffuse "hyaline membrane"
> collapse of alveoli from sticky membranes
> [[hypoxia]] from thickened boundary [[cyanosis]]
> [[atelectasis]] throughout lung
> 
> long-term [[idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis]] due to [[pneumocyte 2]] knockout
> loss of [[alveolar surfactant]]
> pulmonary vasoconstriction

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> bilateral infiltrates
> [[PaO2 FiO2 ratio]] < 300
> [[berlin criteria]]
> [[crackles]]
> [[PCWP]] is normal


> [!abstract] Treatment
> keep lungs dry
> ventilation with ↓[[Vt]] ↑[[RR]]
> use low [[PEEP]] if at all

> [!example] Relationships
> [[proBNP]], [[JVP]], [[s3 sound]] in [[CHF]] only
> [[pulmonary edema]], especially at the [[lung base]]
> [[NRDS]]
> [[pulmonary contusion]] < 1 day. ARDS later
