[[GI stenosis]] [[memes]]
> [!tip] In Short
> [[pylorus]] hypertrophy

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> firstborn males 6 weeks old
> ↓ risk if [[breastfeeding]], possibly through [[VIP]]
> [[Fe toxicity]] 2-8 weeks after
> ∝ [[macrolides]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[vomiting]]
> [[metabolic alkalosis]] [[hypochloremia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> still hungry
> projectile non-bilious [[vomiting]]
> "visible peristalsis"
> "epigastric mass"

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[US]] with thickened pylorus "target/donut"

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[IV fluids]] pre-op (lower apnea risk)
> [[ramstedt pyloromyotomy]]
