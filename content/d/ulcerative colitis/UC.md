ulcerative collitis
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[salmonella]] [[campylobacter jejuni]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[Th2 cell]] response
> partial thickness inflammation
> starts at rectum and goes up
> crypt abscesses [[neutrophil]] in [[mucosal crypt]]
> [[PSC]]
> [[ankylosing spondylitis]]
> [[uveitis]]
> [[toxic megacolon]]
> [[adenocarcinoma]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[LLQ pain]]
> [[pyoderma gangrenosum]]
> always bloody stools


> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[p-ANCA]]
> [[PUCAI score]]
> pseudopolyps (like [[keloid]])
> lead-pipe loss of [[haustra]]
> crypt abscesses


> [!abstract] Treatment
> < 4-6 stools/day [[5-ASA]] + [[corticosteroids]]
> 6+ stools/day [[corticosteroids]] + [[upadacitinib]]
> [[mAb]]
> biologics
> [[smoking]] made better...
> [[proctocolectomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[crohn's]] [[Th1 cell]] mouth to anus, granuloma. also not always [[dysentery]]
