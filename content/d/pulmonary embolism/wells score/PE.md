> [!tip] Condensed Description
> sudden dyspnea!!!

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> above-knee [[DVT]] travels
> [[hypercoagulability]]
> septic [[embolus]] from [[endocarditis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> small embolus asymptomatic
> big embolus [[hypoxia]]
>  saddle emboli [[sudden cardiac death]]
> chronic emboli [[pulmonary hypertension]] 
> systemic [[embolus]] from left heart
> ↑[[HR]] → [[hyperventilation]] → [[respiratory alkalosis]]
> ↑[[A-a]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> calf swelling [[DVT]]
> sudden [[dyspnea]] chest pain
> [[tachypnea]]
> [[hemoptysis]]
> [[pleural effusion]]
> [[pleurisy]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> if stable [[CTA]]
> if unstable [[echo]] of [[RV]]
> 
> wells score > 4
> R [[axis deviation]], [[S1Q3T3]] or [[RBBB]] on [[EKG]]
> spiral CT finds vascular filling problem
> [[doppler US]]
> [[D-dimer]] elevation
> [[CXR]] wedge shaped infarct "hampton's hump"
> "westermark sign" engorged with other fluid

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[heparin]] → [[warfarin]] if [[ESRD]]
> otherwise [[F10 inhibitors]]
> [[tissue plasminogen activator]] if low BP
