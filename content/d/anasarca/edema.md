[[pitting edema]] [[hypervolemia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ↑ [[free water]] [[Na+]] in [[interstitium]]

> [!info] Etiology
> #1 is venous valvular incompetence for lower extremity
> 
> [[hypervolemic hypernatremia]] OR [[hypervolemic hyponatremia]]
> [[heart]] problem
> [[liver]] problem
> [[hypothyroidism]]
> [[nephrotic syndrome]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑ [[hydrostatic]] from [[HTN]], [[DVT]], [[right heart failure]], [[COPD]], [[JVP]]
> ↓ [[oncotic]] [[kwashiorkor]], [[nephrotic syndrome]] [[proteinuria]]
> ↑ [[osmotic pressure]] [[myxedema]], low [[lymph flow]]
> ↓ [[Na+]] secretion from [[NE]], [[AT]], [[eNaC]], [[Na-K pump]], [[ANP]] resist in [[nephrotic syndrome]], [[AKI]], [[CKD]], [[ascites]], [[NSAID]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> eyelids and ankles are the first to show signs
> [[pitting edema]] generally in [[hydrostatic]] related instances
> unilateral suggests [[DVT]] or [[paralysis]]
> generalized pattern implies low [[albumin]]
> worsening with upright posture implies [[right heart failure]] or [[ascites]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> elevated [[JVP]] suggests [[HTN]] over [[cirrhosis]]
> [[hepatojugular reflux]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[idiopathic edema]]
> [[pitting edema]] caused by [[mucopolysaccharidoses]]
> other causes [[non-DHP CCB]], [[TZDs]]
