countercurrent multiplication
> [!tip] Function
> passive [[free water]] absorption

> [!info] Definition
> difference of [[interstitium]] [[osmotic pressure]] between [[renal cortex]] and [[renal medulla]]

> [!warning] Mechanism (largely dependent on [[Na+]] and [[urea]])
> [[henle 1]] active ion reabsorption causes countercurrent multiplication passive [[free water]] absorption
> [[collecting tubule]] concentrates [[urea]] in the medulla


> [!example] Relationships
> regulated by [[ADH]]
> [[vasa recta]] maintains the gradient by increasing its own [[Sosm]] at the [[distal tubule]] and then picking up [[free water]] at the [[henle 1]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
