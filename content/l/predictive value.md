[[diagnostics]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> PPV: TP / TP  + FP
> NPV: TN / TN + FN

> [!question] Mechanism
> lowering cutoff will lower PPV, increase NPV

> [!danger] Diseases

> [!example] Relationships
> [[likelihood ratio]] separate
> highly dependent on [[prevalence]]
> high prevalence = high PPV
> low prevalence = high NPV
> 
> [[sensitivity]] [[specificity]] is more test-inherent
