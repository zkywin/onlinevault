[[eclampsia]] [[HELLP syndrome]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[gestational hypertension]] + [[proteinuria]] after 20wk
> [[trophoblast]] fails to invade into myometrium

> [!info] Etiology
> nulliparity, [[3rd trimester]] (most always > 20wks)
> 
> [[molar pregnancy]] [[1st trimester ]]
> [[DM]] [[CKD]] [[SLE]] [[antiphospholipid syndrome]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ⦻ [[spiral arteries]] →  [[placenta]] [[ischemia]]
> ↓release of [[VEGF]] and placental growth factor
> [[vasospasm]]/coagulation
> ↓[[GFR]] → ↓[[UOP]]
> [[vasospasm]] of carotid artery branches
> [[vasospasm]] → [[central retinal artery]] → [[scotoma]]
> [[pulmonary edema]], [[IUGR]],  etc.
> endothelial damage → [[ischemic stroke]], [[DIC]]
> 
> [[preeclampsia with severe features]]
> [[HELLP syndrome]] (hemolysis)
> [[eclampsia]] (seizure)

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[HTN]] [[proteinuria]] 
> [[preeclampsia with severe features]]
> [[myoclonus]] [[hyperreflexia]]


> [!question] Objective Findings
> 24-hr urine for protein only done if high risk
> [[proteinuria]] > 300mg/day
> [[fibrinoid necrosis]] of [[placenta]]


> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[antihypertensives]] [[MgSO4]]
> 🜲 IV [[hydralazine]], [[labetalol]], [[nifedipine]]
> 
> [[delivery]] without severe 37 weeks
> with severe 34 weeks
> [[delivery]] + [[corticosteroids]] if < 34 weeks
> 
> [[aspirin]] 150 mg daily ppx if [[multiple gestations]], etc


> [!example] Relationships
> [[HELLP syndrome]]
> ≈ [[renal artery stenosis]]
> [[SLE]] has rbc casts
> [[diabetic nephropathy]] more likely at < 20 wks
