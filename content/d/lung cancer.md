#2 [[cancer]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[asbestosis]]
> [[radiation exposure]] radon in basements, uranium
> 
> [[lung adenocarcinoma]] 32%
> [[SCC]] 24%
> [[small cell carcinoma]] 15%
> [[large cell carcinoma]] 12%
> [[adenocarcinoma in situ]] 8%
> [[carcinoid tumor]] 4% #1 in the young
> 
> EML4-ALK fusion in non small-cell [[crizotinib]]
> ∝ Ur, As, Cr, Ni

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[SVC syndrome]]
> [[hoarse voice]]
> [[pancoast tumor]]
> 
> [[mets]] mostly breast and colon (cannonball nodules)
> lung metastasizes to [[adrenal gland]]
> [[brain mets]] [[bone mets]] [[adrenal mets]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> nonspecific [[cough]] [[hemoptysis]]
> [[pneumonia]] past the obstruction

> [!question] Objective Findings
> low dose [[CT]]
> "coin lesion" solitary nodule [[XR]] (identify change over time)
> biopsy
> [[TNM staging]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> poor survival due to late presentation (15% 5 year survival)
> [[chemotherapy]] if [[small cell carcinoma]]
> otherwise [[resection]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[breast cancer]] [[prostate cancer]] higher incidence
> [[granuloma]] and [[hamartoma]] are benign differential
> [[mesothelioma]]
