[[galactokinase deficiency]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[corticosteroids]]
> sun exposure
> [[smoking]]
> [](smoking.md)!warning] Pathogenesis
> in small children can cause [[amblyopia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> halos around lights
> decreased vision
> very bad night vision

> [!question] Objective Findings
> normal red reflex

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> may present like a [[retinoblastoma]]
> [[postoperative endophthalmitis]]
