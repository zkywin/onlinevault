[[restrictive lung]] AND [[obstructive lung]]

> [!tip] Condensed Description
> non-caseating [[granuloma]] multiple organs

> [!info] Etiology
> african american females
> more common in non-smokers

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[HSR 4]] [[Th cell]]
> at [[liver]], [[lung]], [[salivary gland]], [[lacrimal gland]]
> ↑[[1α-hydroxylase]] → [[vD]] 
> ↑[[ACE]]
> [[uveitis]]
> [[hypercalcemia]] → [[frequency]]
> [[heerfordt syndrome]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> Lofgren syndrome triad
> [[dyspnea]] [[cough]]
> [[arthralgia]]
> cutaneous nodules
> [[erythema nodosum]]
> [[lupus pernio]]
> [[bell's palsy]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> big lymph nodes = better prognosis
> [[non-necrotizing granuloma]] 
> [[asteroid body]]
> [[schaumann body]]
> [[fibrosis]]
> [[polyclonal hypergammaglobulinemia]]
> [[hypercalcemia]]
> elevated [[ACE]]
> [[lavage]] with high [[Th cell]] [[Tc cell]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]]
> often self-limiting

> [!example] Relationships
> looks like [[rheumatoid arthritis]]
> [[sjogren syndrome]]
> may even look like [[bell's palsy]] but with parotid enlargement
