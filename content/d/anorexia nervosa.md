
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> primarily trying to lose weight

> [!info] Types
> restricting type (starving in 3 months)
> eating/purging type (episodes in 3 months)
> atypical type has normal BMI

> [!warning] Mechanism
> typically starts in adolescence
> ↓dietary [[Trp]] → ↓[[5HT]] in the brain
> substances may be used for weight loss
> [[QTc prolongation]], myocardial atrophy
> [[mitral prolapse]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> intense fear of gaining weight
> yellow skin
> [[lanugo]] 
> [[russel's sign]] 
> [[metatarsal]], [[tibia]] [[stress fracture]]
> [[parotid gland]] enlargement
> "hypercarotenemia" yellowing from carrots

> [!question] Tests
> BMI < 18.5
> atypical anorexia: BMI > 18.5 + psych symptoms
> [[hypercholesterolemia]] reactive

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[hospitalize]] if BMI < 15
> [[IV fluids]]
> hematogical stabilization
> antidepressants
> [[CBT]] [[SSRI]] or [[olanzapine]]
> be careful of [[re-feeding syndrome]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[cachexia]]
> [[QTc prolongation]]
> [[SI]] rate up by 30-60x
> unlike [[bulimia nervosa]] the goal is to lose weight, not to hide the effects of binging
