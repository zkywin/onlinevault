[[autosomal recessive]] [[newborn hypoglycemia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[aldolase B]] deficiency

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> fruit/juice consumption
> baby weaned from milk (later than [[galactose]])

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[F1P]] accumulation
> [[PO4-]]/[[ATP]] starvation
> inhibited [[glycolysis]] [[gluconeogenesis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[hypoglycemia]] [[vomiting]] [[seizure]]
> [[hepatomegaly]] [[jaundice]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[dipstick]] negative
> [[urine reducing sugars]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> screening mandatory
> avoid [[fructose]], [[sucrose]]
> avoid [[sorbitol]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[classic galactosemia]] analogous
