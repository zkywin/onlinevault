
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> blood tears within [[vessel media]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> pre-existing [[vessel media]] weakness
> [[HTN]] [[vaso vasorum]] [[hyaline arteriolosclerosis]]
> [[marfan syndrome]]
> [[ehlers-danlos]]
> [[bicuspid aortic valve]]
> [[smoking]] men 60-80

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> dissection downward compresses organs
> dissection towards heart [[cardiac tamponade]]
> toward [[internal carotid]] → [[horner syndrome]]
> toward [[renal artery]] [[AKI]]
> rupture
> [[aortic regurg]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> tearing chest pain radiates to back
> unequal [[pulse]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[CTA]] if stable
> [[echo]] if unstable
> widened mediastinum on [[CXR]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> Stanford A (proximal) surgery
> Stanford B (distal) [[β1 blockers]] [[direct vasodilator]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[d/blunt aortic trauma]]
