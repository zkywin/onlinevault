[[hypoadrenalism]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> failed [[adrenal cortex]] ALL 3 LAYERS

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[autoimmune adrenalitis]]
> [[Tb]] [[fungi]] [[CMV]] spreading
> [[lung mets]]
> [[waterhouse friderichsen syndrome]]
> [[adrenal mets]] from lung

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> high [[ACTH]] expression
> [[melanocyte]] stimulation [[hyperpigmentation]] 
> inability to inhibit [[ADH]] causes [[hyponatremia]]
> [[hyperkalemia]]
> [[metabolic acidosis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[hyperpigmentation]]
> [[orthostatic hypotension]]
> [[vomiting]] [[diarrhea]]
> "burning feet syndrome"
> salt, sugar craving


> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↓[[cortisol]] → ↑[[eosinophil]] trapped in blood
> low [[cortisol]] [[aldosterone]]
> high [[ACTH]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]] + [[fludrocortisone]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[hypercortisolism]] is too much cortisol
