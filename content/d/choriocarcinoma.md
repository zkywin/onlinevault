[[gestational tumors]]
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> complete [[molar pregnancy]] (not partial)
> can follow normal [[pregnancy]]
> VERY rarely spontaneously in [[testis]] [[ovary]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[trophoblast]] migrate out (no villi)
> hematogenous [[lung mets]] 80%, [[brain mets]]
> [[PCOS]] [[hyperthyroidism]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> dark vaginal bleeding
> cough [[hemoptysis]]
> pulmonary infiltrates

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[hCG]] remains high after treatment/delivery
> no chorionic villi

> [!abstract] Treatment
> gestational type [[chemotherapy]]
> [[MTX]] [[dactinomycin]]
> non-gestational type difficult to treat

> [!example] Relationships
> [[d/placental site trophoblastic tumor]]
