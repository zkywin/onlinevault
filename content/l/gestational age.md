
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> embryonic age: date of fertilization
> gestational age: age to last [[menstrual cycle]]
> gestational age = embryonic age + 2 weeks

> [!question] Mechanism
> ovulation 2 weeks after last period
> [[LnMP]] - 3 months + 7 days "neagle's rule" due date
> [[fundal height]] = weeks ± 3 cm
