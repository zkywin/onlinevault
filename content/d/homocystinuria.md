[[autosomal recessive]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[homocysteine]] stuck unable to become either [[Cys]] or [[Met]]

> [!info] Causes (all [[autosomal recessive]])
> [[cystathionine synthase]] deficiency, [[vB6]] problem
> [[MTHFR]] deficiency
> [[methionine synthase]] deficiency
> [[vB12 deficiency]]
> [[EtOH]]-related deficiency

> [!danger] Symptoms
> fair skin
> hypermobile joints
> long fingers and toes
> chest wall in or out
> ==downward== lens dislocation
> [[atherosclerosis]]
> [[ischemic stroke]]
> [[intellectual disability]]
> [[hypercoagulability]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[Met]] or [[Cys]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> dietary [[Cys]] or [[Met]]
> [[vB6]] [[vB9]] [[vB12]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[marfan syndrome]] has upward lens dislocation
> [[cystinuria]] is a kidney defect
