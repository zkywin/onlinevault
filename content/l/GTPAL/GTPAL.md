[[pregnancy]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> if G#P#, para = T+P

> [!question] Mechanism
> Gravida # (pregnancies) = (sum of lower 3) +1
> 
> Para 
> T# (full term birth > 36 weeks)
> P# (preterm birth 20-36 weeks)
> A# (aborta loss < 20 weeks)
> 
> L# (living children)

> [!danger] Special Cases
> if pregnant, G = term + preterm + aborta + 1
> "living" has no mathematical bearing on anything
> 
> G6P2124: twins counts as one delivery
> G1P0010 = G1P0


> [!example] Relationships
> GPA: gravida, para, aborta
> TPAL: term, preterm, aborta, living
> birth weights, complications
