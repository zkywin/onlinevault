
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> visible blood in urine

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[AKI]]
> exercise in running with movement of bladder

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> initial [[AKI]] causes [[heme]] accumulation
> oxidative stress pathway and inflammation [[MCP-1]] [[TNFα]] [[IL-6]] [[NF-kB]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[nephritic syndrome]]
> without casts → [[nephrolithiasis]] or [[ADPKD]]
> at the end of the stream → [[bladder cancer]]
> [[prostate cancer]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[dipstick]] for [[HbA]]
> [[microhematuria]]
> [[urine dysmorphic RBC]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> bladder irrigation to prevent clots

> [!example] Relationships
> [[renal AV malformation]]
> [[dark urine]] is darker
