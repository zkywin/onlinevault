
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> pesticides
> contaminated water

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> binds [[lipoic acid]]
> shuts down [[PDC]] [[TCA cycle]]
> prevents [[ATP]] formation

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[diarrhea]]
> garlic breath (arsenous oxide)
> [[QTc prolongation]]
> pigmented skin
> stocking-glove [[neuropathy]]
> [[mee's lines]] light spots on the nails

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> chelating agents [[penicillamine]]
> [[dimercaprol]] [[succimer]]

> [!example] Relationships
> looks like [[guillain-barre]] with additional GI symptoms
> [[lead]] is more motor and less neuropathy
