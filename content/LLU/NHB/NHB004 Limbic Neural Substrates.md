Subcortically, the [[amygdala]] connects to [[hypothalamus]] via [[bed nucleus stria terminalis]]. Depending on what part of the [[hypothalamus]] we get hot/cold aggression.

The cortical players seem to all be nestled between the hemispheres. In fear, [[amygdala]] alarm is clamed by [[vmPFC]] and associations built in [[hippocampus]]. Cravings involve the [[VTA]] and [[nucleus accumbens]]. Add the [[vmPFC]] and you get pleasure.
