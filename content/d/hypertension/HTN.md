essential hypertension
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[BP]] > 130 or > 80 persistent
> (2 measurements over 4 weeks)

> [!info] Etiology
> [[BP]] = [[CO]] x [[TPR]]
> 95% essential hypertension: unidentified etiology
> 5% [[2° HTN]], [[renal artery stenosis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> Grade 1 HTN: < 159 and/or < 99
> Grade 2 HTN: > 160 and/or > 100
> 180/120+ [[HTN crisis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[malignant HTN]]
> [[hypertensive nephrosclerosis]]
> [[edema]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> at home [[BP]] readings
> [[s4 sound]] at [[apex]] could also be normal athlete
> ![[Pasted image 20231213163414.png]]
> [[hyaline arteriolosclerosis]]
> 
> H [[A1C]]
> E [[EKG]]
> Lp [[lipids]]
> C chemistry panel
> U [[UA]]
> T [[TSH]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> 1 [[lifestyle modification]] (esp. weight loss, [[DASH diet]])
> 2 [[antihypertensives]]
> [[biofeedback]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[preeclampsia]]
> [[pulmonary hypertension]]
> also check for [[DM]], [[HLD]], [[CKD]]
