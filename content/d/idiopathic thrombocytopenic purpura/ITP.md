[[platelet disorders]]
> [!tip] In Short
> [[plt]] drop-out
> form of [[immune thrombocytopenia]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[IgG]] [[autoantibody]] [[GpIIb-IIIa]]
> trigger by [[virus]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[extravascular hemolysis]]
> [[splenomegaly]]
> [[thrombocytopenia]]
> acute in children
> chronic in adults
> can cross [[placenta]] → fetal [[anemia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[thrombocytopenia]]
> enlarged [[plt]]
> [[PT-INR]] [[PTT]] normal
> [[megakaryocyte]] increase

> [!abstract] Treatment
> check [[HIV]] [[HCV]]
> if just skin, observe
> if [[epistaxis]] → [[corticosteroids]]
> [[IVIg]] or [[RhIG]] if refractory
> [[splenectomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[glanzman thrombasthenia]] is less [[GpIIb-IIIa]]
> [[TTP]] causes more organ damage
> [[SLE]] association
> [[immune thrombocytopenia]] is the umbrella term
