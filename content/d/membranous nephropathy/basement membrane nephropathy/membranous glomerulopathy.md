[[lupus nephritis]] [[nephrotic syndrome]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> subepithelial [[lupus nephritis]]

> [!info] Etiology
> 1° Caucasian adults
> 2° [[HBV]], [[HCV]], [[SLE]], drugs, [[cancer]] solid tumors ie. [[multiple myeloma]]
> [[penicillamine]] [[NSAID]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[autoantibody]] → [[PLA2R]] on [[podocyte]]
> [[immune complex]] → subepithelial → thickening
> no inflammation → lack of cellular proliferation
> ↓[[AT3]] → [[renal vein thrombosis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings "GBM spikes"
> [[PLA2R]] if primary
> thick granular [[IF]]
> dense deposits [[electron microscopy]]
> "dome spikes" [[IgG]] [[C3a]] on capillary walls only. if also [[mesangium]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> good prognosis in children

> [!example] Relationships
> [[DPGN]] is subepithelial version of lupus nephritis
> [[MPGN]] is mesangial and basement membrane
