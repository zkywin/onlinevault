[[furosemide]], [[ethacrynic acid]]
[[bumetanide]], [[torsemide]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> powerful [[diuretic]]

> [!info] Mechanism
> inhibits [[NKCC]] and [[Na+]] reabsorption at [[henle 1]]
> indirectly inhibit [[Ca2+]] and [[Mg2+]] reuptake

> [!danger] Uses
> [[CHF]] [[pulmonary edema]] drug of choice
> [[edema]] [[nephrotic syndrome]]
> [[hypercalcemia]]
> [[hyperkalemia]] [[translocational hyperkalemia]]

> [!abstract] Formulations

> [!warning] Metabolism
> short half life. lasts 2-3 hours

> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> ototoxicity
> [[hypokalemia]] but not as bad as [[thiazide]]
> [[metabolic alkalosis]]
> [[uric acid]] reuptake [[gout]]
> [[hypovolemic hypernatremia]]
> also lower [[Ca2+]] and [[Mg2+]] [[nephrolithiasis]]

> [[NSAID]] may inhibit protective functions
