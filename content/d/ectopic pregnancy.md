[[maternal complications]]
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> tubal scarring [[PID]], tubal ligation, surgeries
> prior [[pregnancy]]
> [[kartagener syndrome]]
> 10% recurrent

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> 90% in ampulla of [[fallopian tubes]]
> 1-3% [[c-section]] scar
> rupture → [[hemoperitoneum]] → abdominal pain (usually 1 mo after [[LMP]])


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[appendicitis]]-type pain
> fluid filled uterus with no gestational sac

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[TVUS]] finds no "double sac" uterine gestation
> [[hCG]] > 1000 (below normal)
> [[progesterone]] < 5

> [!abstract] Treatment
> early/stable [[MTX]]
> [[laparascopy]]
> advanced [[salpingostomy]]
> ruptured [[salpingectomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[molar pregnancy]] [[hCG]] > 100k
> [[pregnancy]] [[hCG]] > 32k
