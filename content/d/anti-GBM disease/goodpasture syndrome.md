[[small vessel vasculitis]] [[nephritic syndrome]] [[HSR 2]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[basement membrane]] autoimmunity


> [!info] Etiology
> HLA-DRW2 predisposition
> adult males, elderly females

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[autoantibody]] α-chain [[collagen 4]]
> [[complement]] activation → [[GBM]]
> [[glomerulus]] destruction → [[bowman's crescent]]
> [[alveoli]] destruction → [[hemoptysis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[hematuria]]
> [[hemoptysis]]
> [[restrictive lung]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> focal [[bowman's crescent]] with [[glomerulus]] necrosis
> IgG on [[GBM]] on [[IF]] (very linear/uniform look)

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[pulmonary-renal syndrome]]
> [[small vessel vasculitis]] ANCAs have no [[IF]] staining
> [[GPA]] has [[rhinosinusitis]] as well
> [[epidermolysis bullosa]] is skin
> [[alport syndrome]] is mechanical failure
