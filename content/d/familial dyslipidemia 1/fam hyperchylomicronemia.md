[[autosomal recessive]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> inability to break down [[TG]] at periphery

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[LPL]] deficiency
> [[apoC2]] deficiency

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[chylomicron]] do not shrink to remnant size

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[acute pancreatitis]] (high [[TG]])
> pruritic xanthoma
> [[hepatomegaly]] [[splenomegaly]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[chylomicron]]
> "creamy" blood supernatant

> [!abstract] Treatment
> low fat diet

> [!example] Relationships
> unrelated to [[atherosclerosis]]
