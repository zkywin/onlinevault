[[HSR 2]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[Rh]] more rare/severe
> [[ABO]] more common/benign

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> Rh - mom with Rh + past exposure with new Rh + baby

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[ABO]] triggers on first pregnancy (less severe)
> [[Rh]] DOES NOT always trigger on first pregnancy
> second pregnancy for sure triggers

> [!danger] Symptoms
> baby range from [[anemia]]/[[jaundice]] to [[hydrops fetalis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[coombs test]]
> [[total bilirubin]] in [[amniotic fluid]]
> anti-D > 1:8 dilution
> [[kleihauer-betke test]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[RhIG]] prevention
> [[non-UV phototherapy]]
> [[exchange transfusion]]
