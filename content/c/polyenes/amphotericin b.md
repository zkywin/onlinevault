lab "amphibian"
[[antifungals]] [[anti-parasitics]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> big gun of [[antifungals]]

> [!info] Mechanism
> [[ergosterol]] membrane disruptor

> [!danger] Uses
> for very bad infections
> [[cryptococcal meningitis]] (with [[flucytosine]])
> [[candida]]
> [[valley fever]]
> [[mucor]]
> [[brain-eating amoeba]]
> [[leishmaniasis]]

> [!abstract] Formulations
> [[IV]]
> intrathecally for CNS infections
> liposomal formulation lowers [[cholesterol]] binding
> give with [[isotonic fluids]] to protect [[nephrotoxicity]]

> [!warning] Metabolism

> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> [[hypotension]] and [[headache]]
> fever and chills
> [[thrombophlebitis]]
> [[seizure]] if intrathecal
> [[RTA 1]] [[hypokalemia]] [[Mg2+]] wasting
> low [[EPO]] causes [[anemia]]
> [[arrhythmia]]

> [!example] Relationships
> resistance via decreasing [[ergosterol]] expression
> [[nystatin]] also polyene [[ergosterol]] disruptor
