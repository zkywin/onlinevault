
> [!tip] In Short
> low [[T3-T4]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> later [[thyroiditis]]
> [[amiodarone]] [[lithium]]
> [[iodine deficiency]]
> [[wolff-chaikoff effect]]
> [[iatrogenic hypothyroidism]]
> 
> [[euthyroid sick syndrome]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑[[TRH]] → ↑[[TSH]] [[prolactin]] → ↓ [[FSH]] [[LH]]
> ↓ [[β1]] ↓ [[Ca2+]] flux → ↓[[contractility]] [[CO]] [[HR]]
> failure to relax vessels → [[HTN]]
> [[myopathy]]
> [[cretinism]]
> [[hypercholesterolemia]] (lowers [[LDL receptor]])
> [[myxedema coma]]
> mirrors [[SIADH]] if severe

> [!danger] Symptoms
> subclinical = asymptomatic
> weight gain
> [[myxedema]]
> [[alopecia]]
> [[constipation]]
> cold intolerance
> [[neuropathy]] slow [[kinesin]]
> [[goiter]]
> sluggish [[reflex]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑ [[TSH]], [[goiter]]
> ↑ [[CK]] in 90% (not seen in hyperthyroid)
> normal [[CRP]]
> [[hyponatremia]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> subclinical: if [[TSH]] > 10, start [[levothyroxine]] till [[TSH]] normal
> [[liothyronine]]
> increase dose in [[pregnancy]] 30% due to ↑[[TBG]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[hyperthyroidism]]
> [[cirrhosis]] also cold body
> 1°  [[hyperprolactinemia]] has much higher [[prolactin]]
