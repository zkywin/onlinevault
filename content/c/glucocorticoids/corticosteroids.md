[[immunosuppressants]]
> [!info] Mechanism
> [[cortisol]] mimic


> [!danger] Uses
> used as a bridge or for severe acute disease
> vasoconstriction
> immunosuppressive

> [!abstract] Formulations
> [[prednisone]]
> [[triamcinolone]]
> [[dexamethasone]]
> [[methylprednisolone]]
> [[beclamethasone]]
> 
> used 2 weeks at a time, twice a day
> [[topical]] measured by ability to vasoconstrict [[skin]] class I-V

> [!warning] Metabolism

> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> tachyphylaxis (skin tolerance to steroid)
> [[immunocompromised]]
> [[hypercortisolism]] [[hyperglycemia]]
> tertiary adrenal insufficiency from withdrawal
> [[psychosis]]
> [[glaucoma]] [[cataracts]] if used in face
> [[PUD]]
> [[avascular necrosis]]
> [[osteoporosis]]
> [[rheumatoid arthritis]]
> [[myopathy]]
> [[insomnia]]
> [[psychosis]] (apparently)
> [[candida]] if inhaled
> [[PJP]] ([[TPM-SMX]] ppx if high dose > 4wks)



> [!example] Interactions
[[curettage]]
[[mohs surgery]]
[[cryosurgery]]
