[[adrenal adenoma]] [[2° hyperaldosteronism]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology
> 60% bilateral adrenal hyperplasia
> 30% aldosteronoma

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑↑↑[[aldosterone]] production
> screening more common for [[2° HTN]] < 30 years old
> may be triggered by [[thiazide]]
> [[aldosterone escape]]: ↑[[Na+]] → ↑[[ANP]] → ↔ [[Na+]]
> ↓[[K+]] ↓[[H+]]
> [[hypokalemia]] [[metabolic alkalosis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> combination of [[2° HTN]], [[hypokalemia]], [[metabolic alkalosis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[salt suppression test]]
> ↑↑↑[[aldosterone]]÷[[renin]] ratio


> [!abstract] Treatment
> remove adenoma
> if bilateral give [[K-sparing diuretic]] [[MRA]]

> [!example] Relationships
> adrenal venous sampling differs in [[2° hyperaldosteronism]]
