[[dementia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> "uncomplicated" dementia course

> [!info] Etiology
> "early onset" if < 65 yo [[PSEN1]] [[down syndrome]]
> "sporadic form" late onset [[ApoE4]]
> [[ApoE2]] lower risk
> [[mild cognitive impairment]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[Aβ amyloid]], [[tau]]
> begins cortically and moves subcortical
> [[basal ganglia]] and [[CA1 hippocampus]] destruction
> ↓[[ACh]]
> [[cerebral amyloid angiopathy]]

> [!danger] Early Symptoms
> no early focal deficits
> [[episodic memory]]
> [[anterograde amnesia]] [[association cortex]]
> [[working memory]]
> [[perseveration]]
> [[visuospatial impairment]]

> [!danger] Late Symptoms
> spatial disorientation and [[apraxic gait]]
> [[semantic memory]]
> [[parkinsonism]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> low [[ACh]]
> [[neurofibrillary tangles]]
> [[senile plaques]]
> [[hydrocephalus ex vacuo]]
> [[hirano bodies]]
> [[granovascular degeneration]]
> Mini-mental State Examination (MMSE)
> Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA)


> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[ACEi]]
> [[memantine]] [[rivastigmine]] [[donepezil]] [[galantamine]]
> [[antidepressants]]
> [[atypical antipsychotics]]
> 
> avoid anticholinergics, 
> [[TCA]] 
> [[benztropine]]
> [[oxycodone]] 
> [[benzos]]
> several [[parkinson's]] drugs will cause some encephalopathy

> [!example] Relationships
> [[non-pick's frontotemporal lobar degeneration]] would be non-uniform atrophy and affect social behavior
> rule out  [[MDD]], [[vB12]] deficiency, [[hypothyroidism]]
