[[vaginitis]]
"venus fly trap garden" [[anaerobe]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> fishy smell

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[sexually active]]
> [[vaginal douching]]
> [[pregnancy]]
> [[Cu IUD]] 

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑anaerobes ↓[[lactobacilli]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[pruritis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> 3+ amsel criteria:
> gray thin discharge
> [[vaginal pH]] > 4.5
> pos [[whiff test]]
> [[clue cell]] covered with many bacteria
> [[gram variable]] staining

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[pregnancy]] 7 days [[metronidazole]]
> [[metronidazole]] or [[clindamycin]]
> asymptomatic (no discharge) requires no treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[trichomonas]] green trichomonads
> [[candida]] hyhae
