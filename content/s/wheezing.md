
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> Polyphonic wheeze is characterized by multiple pitches and is typical of asthma; 
> 
> monophonic wheeze is characterized by only a single pitch and is typical of focal airway obstruction

> [!question] Mechanism
> airway narrowing below the thoracic inlet
> mild airway obstruction, wheezing is usually heard only in expiration

> [!danger] Diseases
> [[lung]] [[cancer]]
