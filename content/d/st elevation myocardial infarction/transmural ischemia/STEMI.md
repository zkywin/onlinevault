[[ACS]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> 100% occlusion, transmural ischemia

> [!info] Etiology
> [[lateral STEMI]]
> [[anterior STEMI]]
> [[inferior STEMI]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> full wall depolarized, propagates waves at rest
> waves point away from sensor (lowered baseline)
> ST looks elevated

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[st elevation]]
> [[deep q wave]]
> [[t wave inversion]] [[hyperacute t wave]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[cardiac catheterization|cardiac catheterization]]
> [[fibrinolysis]] if not reached in 2 hours

> [!example] Relationships
> [[NSTEMI]]
