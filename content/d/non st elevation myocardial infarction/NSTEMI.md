[[ACS]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> 90% occlusion
> subendocardial ischemia

> [!info] Etiology

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> endocardium depolarized, propagates waves at rest
> waves point toward sensor (elevated baseline)
> ST looks depressed

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[EKG]] [[st depression]]
> [[t wave inversion]]
> ↑[[troponin I]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[aspirin]] + [[P2Y12 inhibitor]] "dual therapy"
> [[clopidogrel]] for 12 months 

> [!example] Relationships
> [[STEMI]] different [[EKG]]
> [[unstable angina]] normal biomarkers
