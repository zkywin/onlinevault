[[NM junction]] "grave" [[HSR 2]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> postsynaptic
> pure [[Nm]] disorder
> fatiguable

> [!info] Etiology
> younger women, older men
> post-partum after [[pregnancy]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[thymoma]] [[autoantibody]] competes for [[Nm]] 
> ↑[[Nm]] activation threshold
> resting will ↑[[ACh]] → reach threshold
> [[ACh]] will outcompete [[autoantibody]]
> [[myasthenic crisis]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
>  [[ptosis]] without affecting pupil
>  [[diplopia]]
>  proximal muscle weakness

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ★ [[anti-AChR]]
> [[EMG]] decremental response
> improved ptosis with [[edrophonium]] or ice pack
> [[anti-MuSK]]


> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[AChE inhibitors]] 
> [[pyridostigmine]] 
> [[prednisone]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[lambert-eaton syndrome]] affects [[ANS]] and [[Nm]] (more proximal)
> ⚠ [[aminoglycosides]]
> reminiscent of [[grave's disease]]
