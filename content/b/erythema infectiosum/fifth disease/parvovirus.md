
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> schoolteacher, daycare worker
> children adolescents
> [[hemolysis]] "aplastic crisis"

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[miscarriage]] [[hydrops fetalis]]
> [[aplastic anemia]]
> [[arthralgia]]
> kills [[RBC]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> "slapped face" rash
> [[fever]]
> lacy reticulated erythema

> [!question] Objective Findings
> parvovirus [[IgM]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> relapse may occur, subsides in 1-2 weeks
> if < 20 wks [[pregnancy]] is ok
> if > 20 wks give weekly [[US]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[rheumatoid arthritis]] no patchy redness
> [[rubella]] [[measles]] similar rash progression but RNA
