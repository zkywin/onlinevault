[[benign neoplasm]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> LOCAL accelerated bone resorption and formation

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> old age
> [[measles]] [[croup]]
> SQSTM1[[Δ]] increases [[NF-kB]]
> [[radiation exposure]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[osteoclast]] escape hyperactivity
> [[osteoblast]] compensatory filling
> sclerotic bone as [[osteoblast]] win
> [[high output heart failure]]
> [[osteosarcoma]]
> polyostotic (multibone) mono-ostotic (single)
> [[CN VIII]] impingement → [[sensorineural deafness]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[fracture]]
> bone pain (microfracture)
> increased [[skull]] size 
> [[deafness]]
> lion-like faces

> [!question] Objective Findings
> isolated [[alk phos]] increase
> [[osteoclast]] with 100+ nuclei

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[calcitonin]]
> [[bisphosphonate]]
> bone scintigraphy

> [!example] Relationships
> [[osteopetrosis]] is only bone formation
> [[osteoporosis]] also normal labs
