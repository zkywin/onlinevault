[[urinary retention]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> congenital anomaly

> [!info] Etiology
> either a strange [[ureter]] insertion into [[bladder]]
> or [[urinary obstruction]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> chronic [[pyelonephritis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> major risk factor for [[UTI]], especially [[pyelonephritis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[VCUG]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> grade 1-2 Ppx [[Abx]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[posterior urethral valve]] is at base of bladder
