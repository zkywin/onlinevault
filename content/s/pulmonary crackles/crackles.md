
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> Finer breath sounds heard on inspiration
> fluid in the alveoli or with opening and closing of stiff alveoli

> [!question] Mechanism
> Coarse crackles are usually thought to be associated with purulent secretions in the alveoli as with pneumonia; 
> fine crackles are often associated with pulmonary edema or interstitial lung disease
