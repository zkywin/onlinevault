
> [!tip] Function
> protect fetus from [[amniotic fluid]]
> lubricate for delivery

> [!info] Location
> "periderm" = first layer above [[ectoderm]] at 2 mo
> [[melanocyte]] migration at 3 mo
> complete epidermis at 4 mo
> vernix caseosa = dead skin + [[sebum]]

> [!warning] Structure
> paste of desquamated skin/sebum

> [!example] Relationships
> [[epidermal ridge]] for [[skin appendages]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[harlequin ichthyosis]]
> [[ectodermal dysplasia]]
> [[hemangioma]]
> [[alopecia]]
