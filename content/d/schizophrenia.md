[[psychotic disorders]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> continuous 6mo+

> [!info] Etiology
> ∝ [[neural plate]] migration due to insult not apparent until adolescent synaptic pruning
> 
> 1% of people; men 18+, women 25+
> before 13 is exceptionally rare
> ∝ obstetric complication, [[cannabis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑[[DA]] at [[mesolimbic]] → positive symptoms
> ↓[[DA]] at [[mesocortical]] → negative
> later onset, more acute has better prognosis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> positive: [[delusions]] [[hallucinations]] [[disorganized thought]] disorganized behavior > 1 mo
> [[psychosis]]
> [[negative symptoms]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[lateral ventricles]] enlargement
> cortical atrophy
> dendritic spine abnormality

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[typical antipsychotics]]
> [[atypical antipsychotics]]
> family therapy to minimize home stress

> [!example] Relationships
> [[schizophreniform disorder]]
> [[paranoid personality]]
> [[d/delusional disorder]]
