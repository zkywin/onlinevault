[[vaginitis]]
"Canada with white snow"
[[fungi]] dimorphic
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> parenteral nutrition
> [[immunocompromised]]
> [[Abx]] [[corticosteroids]] [[SGLT2 blocker]]
> [[pregnancy]] [[DM2]] [[obese]] [[contraceptive]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> 20C pseudohyphae/budding yeast
> 37C hyphae germ tubes


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[complicated UTI]] related to catheter
> [[fungal skin infection]] [[diaper rash]]
> [[bacterial meningitis]]
> chronic mucocutaneous candidiasis [[immunocompromised]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> budding yeast pseudohyphae [[KOH]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[amphotericin b]] [[echinocandin]] for systemic
> [[nystatin]] [[fluconazole]] for [[vaginitis]]
> if azoles fail, it's Candida glabrata (needs [[boric acid]])


> [!example] Relationships
> [[HIV]]
> [[irritant contact dermatitis]] also cause of diaper rash
