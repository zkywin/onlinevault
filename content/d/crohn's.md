[[IBD]]
> [!tip] In Short
> mouth to bottom skip lesions

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[smoking]] worsens it
> family history
> 30% before 20yrs
> 5% before 10yrs

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[Th1 cell]] response
> transmural and [[granuloma]]
> [[fistula]] all over
> creeping fat, pulling fat around entire intestine
> [[stricture]] with string sign
> [[calcium oxalate]] stones
> 
> ⦻ [[ileum]] [[bile salts]] wasting  [[vB12 deficiency]]
> ↑[[cholesterol synthesis]] → [[cholelithiasis]]
> ↑luminal fat → ↓[[Ca2+]] → ↓[[calcium oxalate]] excretion → [[calcium stones]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> sometimes bloody stools
> [[RLQ pain]]
> spares rectum
> migratory polyarthritis
> [[erythema nodosum]]
> pain awakes in night
> sleepiness after pain

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[erythema nodosum]] rash on leg
> [[PDCAI score]]
> [[anti-ASCA]]
> [[stool guaiac]]
> cobblestone mucosa with fissures
> "string sign" strictures [[CT]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[UC]] [[Th2 cell]] only colon, no granuloma
