
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[celiac sprue]] of skin

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> gluten [[autoantibody]] to dermal papillae

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[IgA]] deposition at [[dermis]] papillae
> [[neutrophil]] attraction disrupts basement membrane

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[vesicle]] grouped together

> [!question] Objective Findings
> tiny blisters at the tips of the papillae
> micro abscesses
> [[IF]] linear [[IgA]] deposition

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[dapsone]]
> dietary control of [[celiac plexus]] (low gluten)

> [!example] Relationships
> unrelated but similar appearance to [[herpes virus]]
> [[IgG]] is [[pemphigus vulgaris]] [[bullous pemphigoid]]
