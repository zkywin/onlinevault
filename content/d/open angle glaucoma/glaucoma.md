
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ↑intraocular pressure

> [!info] Etiology
> blockage of either "uveoscleral flow" or "trabecular meshwork flow"
> obstruction of [[canal of schlemm]]
> [[sturge-weber syndrome]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> buildup of [[aqueous]]
> damage to [[CN II]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> painless peripheral vision loss

> [!question] Objective Findings
> increased cup to disc ratio > 0.5

> [!abstract] Treatment
> ★ [[PGF analogues]]
> [[β1 blockers]]
> [[α2 agonists]] [[carbonic anhydrase inhibitors]]
> [[cholinomimetics]] [[AChE inhibitors]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[closed angle glaucoma]]
> mechanistically a [[compartment syndrome]]
> do not give [[anticholinergics]]
