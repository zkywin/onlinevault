[[glycolysis]] [[cytoplasm]]
> [!tip] Function
> regenerate [[NAD]]+
> first minute of exercise

> [!info] Location
> muscle uses [[glucose]] to [[lactate]] at +2[[ATP]]
> liver converts [[lactate]] to [[glucose]] at -6[[ATP]]

> [!warning] Structure
> [[LDH]] [[pyruvate]] -> [[lactate]]
> [[G6Pase]] to release [[glucose]] back to muscle

> [!example] Relationships
> [[EtOH]] in [[fungi]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[von gierke disease]]
> [[lactic acidosis]]
> [[muscle cramp]]
