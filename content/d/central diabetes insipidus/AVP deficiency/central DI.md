central diabetes insipidus
> [!tip] In Short
> ⦻ [[ADH]] production

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> ⦻ [[supraoptic nuclei]] [[paraventricular nucleus]] of [[hypothalamus]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> dilute urine → [[polyuria]]
> [[polydipsia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[Uosm]] < 150 mOsm
> water deprivation test finds urine still dilute

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[desmopressin]] intranasal > oral
> synthetic [[ADH]] helps

> [!example] Relationships
> [[primary polydipsia]] urine concentrated on water deprivation
> more severe than [[nephrogenic DI]] in general
