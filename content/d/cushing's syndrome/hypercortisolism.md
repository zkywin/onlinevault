[[obesity]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> high [[cortisol]]

> [!info] Etiology
> low [[ACTH]]: [[corticosteroids]] [[adrenal adenoma]]
> high [[ACTH]]: [[cushing's disease]], [[small cell carcinoma]] [[paraneoplastic]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑ [[NE]] [[α1]] ↓ [[NO]] -> [[HTN]]
> ↓ [[FSH]] [[LH]]
> ↑[[RANKL]] → ↑[[osteoclast]] → [[osteoporosis]]
> 
> saturates [[11b-hydroxysteroid hydrogenase 2]]
> activation of [[ROMK]], [[H-ATPase]], [[eNaC]]
> ↓ [[collagen]] → abdominal striae

> [!danger] Symptoms
> thin skin, bruising, stretch marks, osteopenia
> [[obesity]] + [[DM2]] + [[2° HTN]]
> [[metabolic alkalosis]] + [[hypokalemia]]
> [[immunocompromised]]
> [[hirsuitism]] [[menstrual irregularity]] [[ED]]
> "moon facies", "buffalo hump"
> [[fat cell]] growth

> [!question] Objective Findings
> 1 [[24hr urine cortisol]]
> 2 night salivary [[cortisol]] (morning = useless)
> 3 [[dex suppression]]
> 
> [[ACTH]]
> ↑ [[AT2]] [[cortisol]]
> ↓ [[renin]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> taper steroids
> [[ketoconazole]]
> resection of tumors

> [!example] Relationships
> [[SAME]] and [[2° hyperaldosteronism]] do not have [[obesity]] and [[DM2]] association, skin findings
> often confused with [[MDD]]
