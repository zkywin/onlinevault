> [!info] Definitions
> demyelinating CNS diseases: oligodendrocyte myelin destruction at brain, spinal cord, cranial nerves 1 and 2
> demyelinating CNS diseases: schwann myelin destruction at cranial nerves 3-12 and peripheral nerves
> dysmyelinating diseases: myelin was always malformed, termed "leukodystrophy", unrelated to WBC

Demyelinating CNS diseases [[MS]] and [[neuromyelitis optica]] are both autoimmune attacks on oligodendrocytes. However, [[neuromyelitis optica]] targets astrocyte [[e/aquaporin 4]] specifically, causes eye and severe cervical spine problems, and can cause [[respiratory failure]] #done e
