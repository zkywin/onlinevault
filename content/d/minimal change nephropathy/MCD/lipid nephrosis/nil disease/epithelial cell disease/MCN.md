[[nephrotic syndrome]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> podocyte effacement (flattening)

> [!info] Etiology
> children
> [[URI]], [[allergic contact dermatitis]] [[vaccine]]
> [[classical hodgkin lymphoma]] rarely

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[Th cell]] or [[reed sternberg]] → ↑↑↑[[cytokine]]
> [[podocyte]] irritation
> [[GBM]] charge loss → [[hypoalbuminemia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[proteinuria]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[Ab]] are not lost
> [[podocyte]] effacement/flattening
> [[IF]] negative
> otherwise normal findings


> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[FSGS]] is progressive
> [[transient proteinuria]]
