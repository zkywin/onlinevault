calcium [[nephrolithiasis]], nephrocalcinosis
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[calcium oxalate]]: hard brown
> [[calcium phosphate]]: soft pale

> [!info] Etiology
> idiopathic hypercalcuria
> [[hyperparathyroidism]]
> [[ethylene glycol]]
> [[nephrotoxicity]]
> [[hypercalcemia]]
> [[crohn's]] increases [[calcium oxalate]] absorption

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> mostly at [[renal medulla]] instead of [[renal cortex]]
> high [[PTH]] or [[vD]]
> increased [[Ca2+]] from bone
> increased [[Ca2+]] absorption [[sarcoidosis]], [[vD]]

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[CT]] finds calcifications bilaterally at [[renal medulla]]
> [[US]] bright with shadows

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[hydrochlorothiazide]] decreases urine calcium
> [[potassium citrate]]
> [[low sodium diet]]
> low oxalate (spinach, beans)
