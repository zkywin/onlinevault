> neck mass

> hard nontender submandibular lymph node
> [[keratin pearls]]
> [[intercellular bridges]]
> cavitations
> centrally growing tumor
> [[EGD]] if cervical [[lymphadenopathy]] seen

> [!abstract] Treatment
> excision
> good prognosis

> [!example] Relationships
> [[basal cell carcinoma]] is upper lip, more common, fewer metastases
