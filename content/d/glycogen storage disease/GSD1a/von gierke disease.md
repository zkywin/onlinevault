[[GSD]] [[newborn hypoglycemia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[G6Pase]] deficiency

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> 2-6 months old

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[G6P]] buildup 
> [[glycogen]] accumulates at [[liver]]
> failed [[glycogenolysis]]
> failed [[lactate fermentation]] 
> failed [[gluconeogenesis]]
> severe [[hypoglycemia]] 

> [!danger] Symptoms
> "doll facies"
> [[seizure]] 
> [[lactic acidosis]] [[gout]]
> [[hepatomegaly]] 
> [[hepatocellular disease]] and large kidney


> [!question] Objective Findings
> DNA test
> high [[lactate]]
> high [[uric acid]] 
> high [[TG]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[starch]] between meals
> avoid [[sucrose]] [[fructose]] [[lactose]] [[galactose]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[cori disease]] more mild
