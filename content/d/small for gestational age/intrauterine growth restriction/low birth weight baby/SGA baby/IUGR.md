[[prematurity]] (SGA at birth, IUGR before)
> [!tip] In Short
> smol babby 
> weight <10% or <3% for gestational age
> <2500g or 5.5 lbs

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[smoking]]
> chromosomal abnormality
> [[TORCH infection]]
> [[placenta]] infarcts
> maternal drug abuse, amphetamines
> [[preeclampsia]]
> [[diabetic embryopathy]]
> [[atenolol]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> symmetric in [[1st trimester]]
> asymmetric in [[2nd trimester]] [[3rd trimester]]
> 
> [[fetal demise]] predicted by [[umbilical artery doppler]]
> perinatal hypoperfusion asphyxia during [[labor]]
> [[polycythemia]]
> [[hypocalcemia]]
> [[hypothermia]]
> [[hypoglycemia]]
> [[hyperbilirubinemia]]
> [[NRDS]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> thin loose skin
> wide anterior fontanelle
> thin umbilical cord

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[fundal height]]
> [[doppler US]] of [[umbilical artery]] can tell risk of [[fetal demise]]
> [[placenta inspection]] for thrombosis

> [!abstract] Treatment
> 1-2 [[NST]] weekly + [[BPP]], [[AFI]], umbilical [[doppler US]]
> [[induced labor]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[prematurity]]
> [[LGA baby]]
