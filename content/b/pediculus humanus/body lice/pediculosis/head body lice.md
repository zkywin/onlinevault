[[louse]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> children (head lice)
> homeless (body lice)

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> pediculosis corporis (vagabond's disease)
> drink human blood

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[acanthosis]]
> discoloration
> [[pruritis]] worsens 3-4 weeks later

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> allow school, but not head/head contact

> [!example] Relationships
> [[pubic lice]]
> [[trench fever]]
> [[tick-borne relapsing fever]]
