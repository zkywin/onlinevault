drug-induced hypersensitivity syndrome [[HSR 4]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> bad [[maculopapular rash]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[anticonvulsants]]
> [[vancomycin]] [[dapsone]] [[TPM-SMX]]
> [[allopurinol]]
> arene oxide metabolites

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> 2-8 weeks after drug exposure
> [[IL-5]] [[Th2 cell]] [[eosinophil]]
> [[roseola infantum]] [[HHV-7]] reactivation
> related to [[hepatitis]] [[AIN]] [[myocarditis]]
> fatality 10%

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[fever]]
> facial [[edema]]
> [[maculopapular rash]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[ALT]] elevation
> 75% have high [[eosinophil]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[IV]] [[corticosteroids]] tapered over months
> risk of resurgence if taken off too fast

> [!example] Relationships
> [[vancomycin]] infusion reaction is immediate
