> [!info] Definitions
> Demyelinating disorders: later injury
> Dysmyelinating disorders: AKA **leukodystrophies**, malformation in the cerebral hemispheres with sparing of subcortical "U-fibers"

[[myelin]]

Demyelinating diseases [[MS]] affects all over [[neuromyelitis optica]] prefers spinal cord and eyes, and [[perivenular encephalomyelitis]] prefers the hemispheres and spinal cord. Perivenular is unique because it has an infectious origin, and can be separated into [[ADEM]] on the order of weeks and [[AHLE]] on the order of days.

"Leukodystrophies" are synonymous with dysmyelinating disorders. [[metachromatic leukodystrophy]] and [[krabbe disease]] are fatal in neonates while [[adrenoleukodystrophy]] is progressive and does not take effect until children are a couple years old already. #done
