


Given estimated due date → "you are _ weeks"

HPI: caution, preE
ROS: social, breastfeeding, birth control
O: immunizations, labs, imaging
A/P: recommendations/plan



Four Questions

	kicks (>28wks 10x/hr)
	leaking fluid
	bleeding
	contractions (>3x/hr)
Pre-E Questions

	headache
	blurry vision
	swelling hands/face
	right upper quadrant pain
Social History

	Smoking, alcohol, drugs
	Marijuana → risk of preterm labor, not recommended for breastfeeding
Breastfeeding

	Baby: nutrition, immune, allergy, obesity
	Mother: ↓ breast/ovarian cancer, savings
Birth Control

	No estrogen (clots, milk supply)
	Progestins (IUD, implant, minipill), Condoms
Immunizations

	Tdap 27-36
	RSV 27-36
	Rhogam 28
	Flu/COVID always when in season
Labs

	CBC 24
	Glucola 1hr 24 (3hr if 3hr earlier on)
	RPR retest 28
	Indirect coombs if Rh-
Imaging/US indications

	oligo/poly
	multiple
	IUGR/macrosomia
	DM, PreE
	previa, choroid plexus cyst, pyelectasis
Recommendations

	birth education class
	breastfeeding class
Plan

	4wk return visit
	unless 4 questions/preE
