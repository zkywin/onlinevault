
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[bone marrow]] infection
> <1 yr [[metaphysis]] + [[joint]]
> 1-17 yr [[metaphysis]]
> 17+ [[vertebrae]] x 2

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> hematogenous seed, adjacent joint, trauma
> [[IV drugs]]
> [[MSSA]] #1 
> [[GAS]] #2 
> [[GBS]] [[kingella kingae]] in infants
> [[salmonella]] in [[sickle cell anemia]]
> [[pseudomonas]] in punctured shoes [[IV drugs]]
> [[Tb]] "potts disease"
> [[vibrio vulnificus]] in [[cirrhosis]]
> [[UTI]] → hematogenous spread


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> pressure within bone collapses vessels [[ischemia]]
> "sequestrum" central [[necrosis]]
> "involucrum" chronic bone cavity
> "sinus tract" rupture laterally through skin [[SCC]] risk
> [[septic arthritis]] infants < 1 yo

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[fever]] [[sepsis]]
> when hip/vertebra/pelvis, dull pain only
> swelling, erythema

> [!question] Objective Findings
> acute bacterial form: [[osteoblast]] [[liquefactive necrosis]]
> [[Tb]] and [[fungi]]: [[caseating granuloma]]
> chronic form:  [[granulation tissue]]
> [[MRI]] patchy bright/dark "mixed sclerosis"
> [[blood culture]] positivity
> [[bone marrow biopsy]]
> ↑[[plt]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> bone-biopsy to identify organism
> surgical debridement, packing, revascularization
> [[vancomycin]] + [[cefepime]] + [[metronidazole]]
> [[vancomycin]] +  [[meropenem]]
> [[IV]] [[Abx]] [[fluoroquinolones]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[avascular necrosis]]
> related to [[abscess]]
> [[bone mets]] may be on differential
