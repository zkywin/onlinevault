> [!quote] Residency
> [[urgent dialysis]]

> [!tip] Condensed Description
> more reliable [[kidney]] control than [[peritoneal dialysis]]

> [!info] Mechanism
> artery access, [[vein access]], [[dialysis fluid]]
> dialysis = diffusion-driven removal of [[BUN]]
> ultrafiltration = hydrostatic-driven removal of [[H2O]]

> [!danger] Uses
> Acute: refractory [[metabolic acidosis]], [[hyperkalemia]], [[hypervolemia]], [[uremia]], intoxication
> Chronic: [[ESRD]] bridge to [[kidney transplant]] 2-3 times a week

> [!abstract] Settings
> [[HCO3-]] can be set
> typically 3x a week

> [!warning] Metabolism

> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> [[dialysis disequilibrium syndrome]]
> aquired [[renal cyst]] with risk of [[RCC]]
> [[dialysis-associated amyloidosis]]
> [[hypotension]]
> [[pyelonephritis]]
> [[arrhythmia]]
> [[sudden cardiac death]]

> pts get [[hydromorphone]]
