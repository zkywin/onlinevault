[[EMERGENT]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> intestine telescopes (near inside far)

> [!info] Lead Points
> <2 lymphoid hyperplasia ([[peyer's patch]] [[AAV]]) 
> 2+ [[meckel's diverticulum]] ileocolic
> adults: tumors
> 
> [[HSP]] ileo-ileal

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> often at ileocecal junction

> [!danger] Symptoms
> legs drawn to abdomen from the pain
> thick red currant jelly bleeding stools
> [[hematochezia]] + mucus
> 15 min episodes severe abdominal pain
> [[fatigue]]
> sausage-shaped RUQ mass

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[US]] "bulls-eye" finding

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[NG tube]] decompression
> ileo-colic → air or saline enema
> ileo-ileal → surgical
> ⦻ [[RV1 RVS]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[volvulus]] is totally different
