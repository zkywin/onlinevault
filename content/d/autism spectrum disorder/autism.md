> [!tip] Condensed Description
> mostly disability, very rarely savants

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> must start in early childhood

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> seen younger than 2 years
> in very rare circumstances caused by [[fragile x syndrome]], [[tuberous sclerosis]], [[rett syndrome]], [[down syndrome]], etc.


> [!danger] Symptoms
> emotional reciprocity problems
> poor body language control
> poor understanding of relationships
> repetitive motor movements
> insistence on routine
> strange choices of indifference and interest

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[MCHAT-RF]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> behavioral intervention

> [!example] Relationships
> ∝ [[seizure]], [[hypothyroidism]], [[DM1]]
> differential [[lead]]
> [[reactive attachment disorder]] has normal speech
