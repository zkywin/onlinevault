
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> ∝ [[polyhydramnios]]
> [[subchorionic hemorrhage]]
> [[cocaine]] [[smoking]] [[trauma]] [[HTN]]
> rupture of maternal vessels

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> "dissection type hemorrhage"
> myometerium separates from decidualized layer
> blood accumulation between layers → firm uterus
> [[placenta]] detached
> [[hemorrhagic shock]]
> [[tissue factor]] → [[DIC]] [[miscarriage]]
> [[prerenal AKI]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> focal pain at placenta
> 20% no other symptoms
> 80% have abrupt painful vaginal bleeding 
> may not be as much as you might expect
> uterine contraction (high frequency low intensity)

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[IV fluids]]
> [[EMERGENT]] [[c-section]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[placenta previa]] does not have a firm uterus
> so bad can be [[d/couvelaire uterus]]
