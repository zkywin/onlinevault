choking
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology
> [[epiglottitis]]
> variable extrathoracic: vocal cord paralysis, laryngeal tumor
> variable intrathoracic: tracheomalacia, bronchogenic cyst
> fixed obstruction: tracheal stenosis, [[goiter]], mediastinal mass

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> removal of air in the lung
> complete collapse of the [[lung]] will occur

> [!danger] Symptoms
> silent cough
> inability to talk
> turning blue

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> avoid hot dogs/grapes/vegetables till age 4
> 
> remove only if you can see it
> do chest thrust if waist too wide
> 5 back blows 5 chest thrusts for infants
