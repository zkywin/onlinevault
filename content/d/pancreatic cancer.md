
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> age > 50 [[smoking]], african americans
> [[BRCA]] in ashkenazi jews
> [[chronic pancreatitis]] > 20 yrs
> often at the head
> [[MDD]] [[anxiety disorder]] may be prodromal in 1/3

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[KRAS]] [[SMAD4]] 
> compression [[cholestasis]] at [[common bile duct]]
> → biliary duct dilation
> [[hypercoagulability]]
> [[process/cachexia|cachexia]]
> migratory superficial thrombophlebitis "trousseau syndrome"

> [!danger] Symptoms
> gnawing pain worse at night
> painless [[jaundice]] if at [[pancreas head]]
> [[fat malabsorption]]
> [[l/courvoisier's sign]]: big non-tender gallbladder + jaundice

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[US]] for head
> [[CT]] for tail
> [[CA 19-9]] marker
>[[l/carcinoembryonic antigen/CEA|CEA]] marker

> [!abstract] Treatment
> poor prognosis
> proximal: [[whipple procedure]]
> distal: pancreatectomy, [[splenectomy]]
