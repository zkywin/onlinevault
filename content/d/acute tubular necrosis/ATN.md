

intrinsic [[AKI]], structural acute kidney injury/acute tubular necrosis, acute tubular injury, renal ischemia, 
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ischemic OR toxic damage to [[nephron]] tubules, not [[glomerulus]]

> [!info] Etiology
> #1 Ischemia ([[prerenal AKI]])
> [[hypoxia]] of [[proximal tubule]] and [[henle 1]]
> 
> #2 Nephrotoxicity
> [[aminoglycosides]], [[vancomycin]]
> [[myoglobin]]
> [[lead]]
> [[ethylene glycol]]
> [[radiocontrast]]
> [[rhabdomyolysis]]
> [[uric acid]] [[tumor lysis syndrome]]
> 
> [[RPGN]], [[AIN]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[necrosis]] starts at late [[proximal tubule]] and ascending [[henle 1]]
> dead tissue plugs [[nephron]]
> [[GFR]] drops ([[JG cell]] [[adenosine]], [[necrosis]])
> increased [[RBF]] also increases [[VO2]]
> eventual healing

> [!danger] Symptoms
> sudden [[oliguria]] for 10-12 days
> [[polyuria]] for 12-14 days
> gradual return over weeks to months

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[BUN]] and [[Cr]]
> [[granular cast]]
> immediate [[BUN Cr ratio]] < 15, [[FENa]] and [[Uosm]] problems
> [[hyperkalemia]] with [[HAGMA]]
> 
> [[serum creatinine]] and [[BUN]] high
> [[isosthenuria]]
> [[dark urine]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> supportive [[HD]]

> [!example] Relationships
> most common cause of [[AKI]]
> [[AIN]] is drug-induced
> 
> does not damage [[glomerulus]] unlike [[renal cortical necrosis]]
> unlike [[prerenal AKI]], giving [[isotonic fluids]] does not help [[UOP]]
> 
> [[atherosclerosis]], [[FMD]], [[renal infarct]], [[renal artery stenosis]], [[hypertensive nephrosclerosis]], [[HTN]]
