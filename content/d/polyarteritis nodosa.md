[[HSR 3]] [[neuropathy]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> everywhere except lung
> microaneurysms
> multiple artery "nodes"

> [!info] Etiology
> [[muscular artery]] [[vasculitis]]
> associated with [[HBV]] ([[HBsAg]] in 30%)

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[immune complex]] deposition
> damage to any organ except [[lung]]
> [[mesenteric ischemia]]
> [[renal artery stenosis]] → [[2° HTN]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> asymmetric [[neuropathy]]
> [[ischemic stroke]]
> 
> [[IMA]]  [[melena]]
> [[skin]] [[purpura]] in 1/3 of patients
> subcutaneous nodules
> [[livedo reticularis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[HBsAg]]
> [[fibrinoid necrosis]]
> "string of pearls" appearance from [[aneurysm]]


> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]]
> [[cyclophosphamide]]

> [!example] Relationships
> looks like [[FMD]]
> many reclassified to [[MPA]]
> [[neuropathy]] and [[charcot-marie tooth]] are uniform
