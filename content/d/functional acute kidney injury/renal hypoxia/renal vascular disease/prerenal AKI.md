[[AKI]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ⦻ [[renal artery]] flow
> the kidney functions relatively normally

> [!info] Etiology
> elderly
> severe [[dehydration]]
> sudden [[HTN]]
> [[NSAID]] prevent afferent dilation
> [[ACEi]] [[ARB]] inhibit efferent constriction
> [[compartment syndrome]] abdominal
> [[hypovolemic shock]]
> [[cardiogenic shock]]
> [[warm shock]]
> [[renal artery stenosis]]
> [[hypercalcemia]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[BUN Cr ratio]] > 20
> ⦻ [[renal artery]] → ↓[[RPF]]
> compensatory [[PGE2]], [[PGI2]], [[NE]], [[AT]], [[ADH]] → ↑[[GFR]]
> if severe → [[nephron loss]] → [[ATN]]

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[azotemia]] [[oliguria]]
> [[BUN Cr ratio]] > 15
> [[FENa]] < 1%
> [[Uosm]] > 500
> 
> [[hyaline cast]]
> giving [[isotonic fluids]] increases [[UOP]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> reversible if given fluids

> [!example] Relationships
> if hypoperfusion persists, it could become [[ATN]] and [[ATN]]
> [[ATN]] [[UOP]] is not improved by [[isotonic fluids]]
> [[renal artery stenosis]]
