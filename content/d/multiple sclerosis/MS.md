[[CNS demyelination]] [[UMN]] 
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[WBC]] attack [[myelin]] of [[oligodendrocyte]]

> [!info] Etiology
> most common [[UMN]] in young adults
> young women, born spring, far from equator
> [[vD deficiency]]
> [[smoking]]
> HLA-DRB1
> always in [[EBV]] infected people

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> autoimmune → ⦻ [[myelin]] [[oligodendrocyte]]
> ⦻ [[MLF]] → [[diplopia]] [[INO]]
> [[optic neuritis]] → [[afferent pupillary defect]]
> 
> primary relapsing remitting pattern
> secondary progressive course
> 40-50 yr old men primary progressive variant
> [[marburg MS]]
> [[balo's concentric sclerosis]]
> [[MDD]]
> [[transverse myelitis]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> worse with heat
> scanning "drunk" speech 
> [[trigeminal neuralgia]]
> [[transverse myelitis]]
> [[hemiparesis]]
> [[ataxia]]
> [[uhthoff phenomenon]]
> [[lhermitte's sign]]
> [[atonic bladder]] or [[urgency]]

> [!question] Tests
> ★ [[MRI]] [[dawson's fingers]]
> 
> active plaque s with [[foamy macrophages]]
> perivenular [[lymphocytic infiltrate]]
> [[gliosis]]
> [[LP]] [[IgG oligoclonal bands]]
> [[visual evoked response test]]
> [[radiocontrast]] leakage

> [!abstract] Treatment
> acute  [[methylprednisolone]]
> 
> Trad prevention: [[IFN beta-1]] [[glatiramer]] [[dimethyl fumarate]]
> WBC disabling: [[natalizumab]] [[ocrelizumab]] [[fingolimod]] [[teriflunomide]] 
> [[c/alemtuzamab]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[neuromyelitis optica]] only affects eyes and spinal cord, and causes respiratory failure
