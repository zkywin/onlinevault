[[autosomal dominant]]
"rocklike bone"
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> weak [[osteoclast]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> CLC17[[Δ]] [[carbonic anhydrase]] II 
> inability to acidify bone

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> very dense trabeculae
> [[aplastic anemia]] from woven bone replacement
> [[bone marrow]] impairment "myelophthisic process"
> [[liver]] and [[spleen]] hematopoiesis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[pancytopenia]] 
> [[hepatosplenomegaly]]
> [[fracture]] (ironically)
> [[cranial nerves]] impingement
> [[hydrocephalus]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> hyper dense [[XR]]
> "erlenmeyer flask" bone shape

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[bone marrow transplant]] (introduce normal [[osteoclast]])

> [!example] Relationships
> [[RTA 2]] is closely associated
> opposite of [[osteoporosis]]
