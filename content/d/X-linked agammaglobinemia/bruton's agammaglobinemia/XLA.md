[[B-cell failure]]
[[x-linked recessive]] agammaglobinurea
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> no B cells

> [!info] Etiology
> BTK[[Δ]] [[tyrosine kinase]]
> males!

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> no [[BCR]] light chain
> failure of [[pre B cell]] mature → [[B cell]]
> deficiency of [[IgA]]
> related [[rheumatoid arthritis]] [[pernicious anemia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[otitis media]]
> [[rhinosinusitis]]
> [[pneumonia]]
> [[viral meningitis]]
> [[encapsulated]]
> enteroviruses: [[coxsackie B virus]] [[poliovirus]] [[echovirus]] [[cold]] [[giardia]]

> [!question] [](coxsackie%20B%20virus.md)[[flow cytometry]]
> ↓ [[CD19]] [[CD20]]
> underdeveloped [[lymphoid follicle]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[IVIg]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[SCID]] failure to thrive
> [[CVID]] has normal B count
> [[transient hypogammaglobinemia of infancy]]
