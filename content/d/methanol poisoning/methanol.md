[[HAGMA]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> uglier sibling of ethanol
> blindness

> [!info] Etiology
> "wood alcohol"
> ingestion of antifreeze or moonshine alcohol
> inhaled paint thinner

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> forms [[formaldehyde]] which is toxic to the retina

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[HAGMA]] with [[Sosm]] rising first from aldehydes
> retinal toxicity
> permanent blindness
> [[putamen]] necrosis

> [!question] Objective Findings
> hyperemic optic disc

> [!abstract] Treatment
> inhibition of alcohol dehydrogenase
> [[fomepizole]]
> [[EtOH]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[ethylene glycol]] affects kidneys
