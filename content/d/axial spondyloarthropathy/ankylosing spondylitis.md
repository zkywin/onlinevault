[[seronegative arthropathy]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[vertebrae]] inflammation + fusion
> [[ankylosis]] + [[spondylosis]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> young adult male
> HLA-B27

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ossification of [[vertebrae]] to [[anterior longitudinal ligament]]
> [[sacroiliitis]] 
> [[uveitis]], [[iridocyclitis]]
> [[idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis]]
> [[aortic regurg]]
> [[osteoporosis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> back pain improves with exercise

> [!question] Objective Findings
> "bamboo spine"
> [[SI joint]] [[XR]] findings
> tragus to wall, occipital to wall
> radiographic type
> non-radiographic type

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[TNFα]] [[mAb]] → improved mobility
> [[IL-17]] [[mAb]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[reactive arthritis]]
> [[psoriatic arthritis]]
> essentially [[rheumatoid arthritis]] version of [[spondylosis]]
