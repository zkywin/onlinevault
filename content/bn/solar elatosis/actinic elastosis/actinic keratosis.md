[[benign neoplasm]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> "[[SCC]] in situ"

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> sun exposure, old age
> [[p53]] mutation

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> deposit of UV damaged [[elastin]] tissue
> 10-20% [[SCC]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[scale]] [[plaque]] face back neck
> erythematous hyperkeratotic
> "sandpaper feel"
> itching

> [!question] Objective Findings
> atypia at [[stratum basale]] only
> [[parakeratosis]]
> [[hyperkeratosis]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[cryotherapy]] if isolated
> [[5-fU]] or [[imiquimod]] if diffuse

> [!example] Relationships
> [[lentigines]]
> [[seborrheic keratosis]]
> [[SCC]] goes full thickness
