[[EMERGENT]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> twisted blood supply and spermatic cord
> first venous then arterial

> [!info] Etiology
> bell clapper deformity
> congenital [[processus vaginalis]] abnormality
> [[cryptorchidism]] 10x higher risk


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> twist 90 to 180 degrees
> red infarct because vein is blocked
> testicular viability drop between 6-8 hrs
> [[azospermia]] but normal [[testosterone]]
> [[hydrocele]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> pain not positional
> nausea vomiting
> lower abdominal pain, inguinal pain
> prehn sign (elevate the testicle) not reliable

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ultrasound evaluation "heterogenous testis"
> [[TWIST scoring]]
> [[prehn's sign]]
> check pyuria
> rotate medial to lateral 180 check relief
> cremasteric reflex

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[orchiopexy]] bilateral (risk of future torsion)
> [[orchiectomy]] if not viable
> untwist or put in a prosthesis 

> [!example] Relationships
> [[testicular appendage torsion]]
> Ddx:  [[epididymitis]] [[orchitis]] [[indirect inguinal hernia]] [[hydrocele]]
