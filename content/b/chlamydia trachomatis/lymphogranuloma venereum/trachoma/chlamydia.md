> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> A-C [[conjunctivitis]]
> D-K [[PID]]
> L1-3 lymphogranuloma venearum
> [[atypical pneumonia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> asymptomatic in women, symptomatic in men
> clear discharge
> burning urination
> staccato cough
> [[neovascularization]]
> follicular [[conjunctivitis]]
> painless [[superficial inguinal nodes]] lymphadenopathy

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[STD testing]]
> absent of identifiable bacteria on gram stain

> [!abstract] Treatment
> conjunctivitis cannot be prevented
> 
> [[azithromycin]] for [[pregnancy]], baby, or A-C
> otherwise [[doxycycline]]
> 
> test of cure 2 weeks after
