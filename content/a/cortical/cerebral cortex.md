The [[central sulcus]] separates [[frontal]] from [[parietal]]. The [[a/sylvian fissure]] separates both from the [[temporal]]. The [[a/parieto-occipital sulcus]] separates the [[occipital]] from the rest.

The [[a/arcuate fasciculus]] connects broca's in the [[frontal]] with wernicke's in the [[temporal]]. Buried within the [[a/sylvian fissure]] is the [[a/insular cortex]] and [[a/operculum]].

The [[a/paracentral lobule]] is within the interhemispheric division, between the [[frontal]] and [[parietal]]. Also nestled in between the hemispheres is the [[a/cingulate gyrus]].
