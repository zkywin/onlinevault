[[liquefactive necrosis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> "autodigestion"
> [[d/necrotizing pancreatitis]]

> [!info] Etiology
> I GET PP SMASHED
> 
> 1 [[EtOH]] unclear mechanism
> 2 [[cholelithiasis]] [[common bile duct]]/[[ampulla of vater]]
> 3 [[TG]] > 1000
> 
> [[CF]] → blocked [[pancreatic duct]]
> [[trauma]] from seatbelt
> [[mumps]]
> [[GLP-1s]] [[sulfonamides]] [[6-MP]]
> [[furosemide]] [[hydrochlorothiazide]]
> brown recluse, scorpion, gila monster
> [[IgG4 plasma cells]]
> [[hypercalcemia]]
> [[duodenal ulcer]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[ranson's criteria]]
> 
> [[ileus]]
> [[chylomicron]] lodge in capillary
> [[fatty acid]] damage the area
> autoactivation of [[trypsinogen]]
> autodigestion
> [[liquefactive necrosis]]
> [[fat necrosis]]
> [[SIRS]] [[DIC]] [[ARDS]] 
> [[pancreatic pseudocyst]] [[abscess]]
> [[fat necrosis]] -> [[hypocalcemia]]
> ↑vascular permeability → [[hypotension]]
> [[multi-organ failure]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[epigastric pain]] radiating to back
> [[vomiting]]
> [[purpura]] only 3% or less of cases
> [[hypotension]] from vascular permeability

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑↑↑ 🜲 [[pancreatic lipase]] x3 normal lim (15)
> ↑↑↑ [[α-amylase]] 
> 
> [[CT]] with contrast if not 3x normal levels
> if [[ALT]] > 150, suggests [[gallstone pancreatitis]]
> severity assessed by [[BUN]] and [[Hct]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[NPO]] (or use [[NJ tube]])
> [[isotonic fluids]] → compensate for 2/2 [[AKI]]
> pain control
> [[madaria et al.]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[pancreatic insufficiency]]
> [[chronic pancreatitis]]
