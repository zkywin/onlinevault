
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> head shape deformation

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> 80-90% sporadic
> 10-20% [[FGF]]1 or [[FGF]]2 receptor mutation
> baby on side worse than on back

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> premature bony plate growth through [[suture]] 
> premature fusion

> [!danger] Symptoms
> #1 saggital suture - scaphocephaly
> coronal suture - brachycephaly
> metopic suture - trigonocephaly

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> surgical re-opening of sutures

> [!example] Relationships
> [[microcephaly]] is more of a brain problem
