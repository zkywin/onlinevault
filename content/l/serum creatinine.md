Scr
> [!tip] Function (0.6-1.2 mg/dL)
> ∝ [[GFR]]⁻¹
> overestimates [[GFR]] by 20% since [[Cr]] is secreted at [[distal tubule]]

> [!info] Location

> [!warning] Calculation
> Cockcroft Gault in men = ((140-age) x ideal body weight) ÷(72 x serum creatinine)
> Schwartz in children = k x length ÷ serum creatinine

> [!example] Relationships
> underestimates [[GFR]] with ketoacids or [[cimetidine]] [[trimethoprim]] (lowered secretion)
> overestimates [[GFR]] in low muscle mass (lowered production)
> unstable as a measurement in [[cardiorenal syndrome]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
