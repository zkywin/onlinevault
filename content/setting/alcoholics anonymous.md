> [!info] 12 Steps
>1 admit we are powerless over alcohol. admit our lives are unmanageable. "i can handle it... it's not that bad... i can figure this thing out... i don't need your help"
>
>2 we came to believe that a power greater than ourselves could restore us to sanity "i'm not running the show. "
>
>3 we made a decision to turn our willa and our lives over to god as we understand it "shut up and listen... go to meetings until you like them"
>
>4 we made a fearless and searching moral inventory of ourselves. "what secrets do i not admit to anyone else" think it, write it, read out loud, read out loud to mirror, read to others, read it to a group
>
>5 you admit to god, yourself, and another, the nature of your wrongs. "pastor, minister, etc. just be a human being to listen"
>
>6 we were entirely ready to have god remove all these defects of character
>
>7 we asked god to remove us of our shortcomings
>
>8 made a list of all the persons we harmed and developed the willingness to make ammends
>
>9 we have made ammends to people we have harmed (when acceptable) don't go too far for people who may be hurt by our admittance
>
>10 on a daily basis go through the inventory and process. and where you fell short. forgive yourself, verbalize them to yourself
>
>11 Sought through prayer and meditation to improve our conscious contact with God as we understood Him, praying only for knowledge of His will for us and the power to carry that out.
>
>12 Having had a spiritual awakening as the result of these Steps, we tried to carry this message to alcoholics, and to practice these principles in all our affairs. "do it by example and not by pushing it on others"
