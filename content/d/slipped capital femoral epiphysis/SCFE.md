[[salter harris fracture]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> #1 hip disorder in adolescents

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> obese boys 10-14
> [[hypothyroidism]], other endocrinopathy

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[anterior thigh]] atrophy
> [[superficial gluteal muscles]] atrophy

> [!danger] Symptoms
> limited internal rotation
> knee pain
> "knee catching"

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> surgical screw fixation
> do not attempt closed reduction
