[](PALLIATIVE.md)[](PALLIATIVE.md)cardiopulmonary resuscitation
> [!info] Mechanism
> cycles of 30 [[chest compressions]] with 2 breaths
> in children cycles of 15 with 2 breaths if you have another rescuer

> [!danger] Uses
> for [[pregnancy]] women do the same thing
> [[sudden cardiac death]]
> pediatric [[bradycardia]] < 60 bpm

> [!abstract] Formulations
> [[chest compression feedback monitor]]

> [!warning] Priorities
> 
> 1. compressor: swapped every 2 minutes (5 cycles)
> 2. airway: provides breaths without overventillating 
> 3. defibrillator: and coach for the rest
> - team leader: feedback
> - timer: interruptions and drug (chest compression fraction)
