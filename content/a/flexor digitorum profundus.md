[[anterior forearm]]
> [!tip] Function
> flex wrist
> flex digits 2-5

> [!info] Location
> from [[ulna]]
> to [[distal phalanx]] 2-5


> [!warning] Structure
> [[ulnar nerve]] + [[median nerve]]
> [[ulnar artery]] + anterior interosseous artery

> [!example] Relationships
> opposite of [[extensor digitorum]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
