[[β-hemoglobinopathies]]
chromosome 11 [[autosomal recessive]] 
> [!tip] In Short
> mutant polymerizing β globin

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> βs [[missense  Δ]] [[Val]] -> [[Glu]] [[HbS]]
> TRAIT β/βs
> DISEASE βs/βs

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> low O2 → [[HbS]] polymerization
> de-sickling and re-sickling damages membrane
> vasooclusion → [[pain crisis]]
> 
> [[extravascular hemolysis]] → [[erythroid hyperplasia]]
> [[hypovolemic shock]] from spleen sequestration
> [[avascular necrosis]] [[chest syndrome]] 
> [[FSGS]] [[priapism]] [[hyposthenuria]] [[RCC]]
> [[vB9 deficiency]]
> [[d/splenic sequestration]]
> 
> [[asplenic]] by 5yrs → [[H flu]], [[encapsulated]]
> [[strep pneumoniae]] → [[sepsis]] → [[ceftriaxone]]
> [[salmonella]] → [[osteomyelitis]]
> [[parvovirus]] → [[aplastic crisis]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[anemia]] 
> [[dactylitis]]

> 



> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[EPO]]
> low [[ESR]]
> [[howell-jolly body]]
> sodium metabisulphite sickling test

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[exchange transfusion]] for acute problem
> 
> [[penicillins]] Ppx till age 5
> [[hydroxyurea]] → ↑[[HbF]]
> [[transfusion]]
> [[bone marrow transplant]]

> [!example] Relationships
> HET with [[β-thalassemia]] or [[d/hemoglobin C disease/HbC]]
> ∝ [[asthma]]
