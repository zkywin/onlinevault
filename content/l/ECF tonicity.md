"hypertonic", "hypotonic" to the cell, ECF, extracellular fluid tonicity
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> the effect of [[Sosm]] on the size of a cell

> [!info] Calculation (pretty much the exact same as [[Sosm]] but without [[BUN]])
> estimated by [[ECF tonicity]] = (2xNa+)
> if hyperglycemic, [[ECF tonicity]] = (2xNa+) + [[glucose]]/18



> [!question] Mechanism
> permeable solutes do not contribute ([[urea]], [[glucose]])
> impermeable solutes contribute (Na+)

> [!danger] Diseases

> [!example] Relationships
> primarily determined by [[hydrostatic]] and [[oncotic]]
> [[lymph flow]] is an important factor in draining fluid
