[[UMN]] [[stroke]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> hypoperfusion
> [[ischemia]] between [[ACA]] [[MCA]] [[PCA]] zones

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> can result from [[sudden cardiac death]]
> damage starts in areas between artery supplied territories
> may also affect [[hippocampus]] and [[purkinje cell]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> "man in a barrel syndrome"
> can only move distal muscles
> cortical blindness if between [[MCA]] and [[PCA]]
> proximal muscle weakness if between superficial and deep [[MCA]]

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[hypoxic brain injury]]
