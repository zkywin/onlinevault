> [!tip] In Short
> primarily overeating
> binging weekly for 3 mo

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> late adolescence/young adulthood
> [[c/ipecac]] abuse

> [!warning] Pathogenesis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> normal or high BMI
> severity based on frequency, where mild is 1-3 times a week
> [[parotid gland]] enlargement

> [!question] Tests
> [[EKG]] for [[d/arrhythmia]] risk

> [!abstract] Treatment
> give electrolytes to stabilize
> [[CBT]]
> [[fluoxetine]] [[SSRI]]

> [!example] Relationships
> unlike [[anorexia nervosa]] BMI is up or normal, age is older, although 10-15% crossover
> very strongly associated with substances
> [[d/cyclic vomiting syndrome]]
