> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[penicillins]]
> [[cephalosporins]]
> [[carbapenems]]
> 
> [[monobactams]] (less cross-reactive)


> [!info] Mechanism
> bind target [[PBP]] transpeptidases

> [!danger] Uses
> [[MSSA]]
> [[GAS]]
> add [[probenecid]] to up half life
> add beta-lactamase-inhibitor in resistant strains

> [!abstract] Formulations


> [!warning] Metabolism
> mostly renal 
> liver exceptions [[ceftriaxone]] and [[nafcillin]]

> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> [[HSR 1]] cross reactivity
> [[HSR 2]] nephritis
> [[HSR 3]] [[serum sickness]]
> [[HSR 4]] [[SJS]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[antibiotic resistance]]
> ineffective [[acid fast]], [[protozoan]], [[intracellular]], [[virus]]
> [[probenecid]] increases renal half life
