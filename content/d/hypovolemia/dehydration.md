
> [!tip] In Short
> 1 loss of total body water
> manifests as [[hypernatremia]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[bacterial gastroenteritis]]
> [[pyloric stenosis]]
> [[vomiting]]
> [[diarrhea]]
> [[hypokalemia]], [[diuretic]], [[CF]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> mild 3-5% (thirst, normal vitals)
> moderate 6-9% (dry, sunk fontanelle, irritable)
> severe 10-15% (very dry oral mucosa, lethargy)

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[thirst]]
> confusion

> [!question] Objective Findings
> 1kg weight loss = 1L water loss
> [[capillary refill]]
> [[suture]] sunken

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[rehydration]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[hypovolemic shock]] also involves [[Na+]] loss
