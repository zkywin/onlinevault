
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> chronic dermatosis (thin white scarring)
> [[vulva]] only

> [!info] Etiology
> can happen in the young

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> very slight increased risk [[SCC]] [[vulvar carcinoma]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> "figure of 8" around vulva and anus
> [[vulvar pruritis]]
> [[dyschezia]], [[anal fissure]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> white plaques at [[vulva]]
> punch biopsy to rule out [[SCC]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]] clobetasol

> [!example] Relationships
> [[lichen planus]] is different
> only vulva, not vagina
