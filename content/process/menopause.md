
> [!tip] In Short
> [[amenorrhea]] for 12 mo

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> run out of [[ovarian follicle]]
> median age 51
> [[smoking]] makes earlier

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> preceded by some abnormal periods
> loss of [[estradiol]] and [[progesterone]]
> ↓ [[inhibin]] ↑ [[FSH]]
> ↑ [[RANKL]] → [[osteoporosis]]
> risk of [[ischemic stroke]] [[CAD]]
> all progesterone is from adipose

> [!danger] Symptoms
> hot flashes (estrogen dips)
> atrophy of [[vagina]]
> check bleeding history for [[endometrial carcinoma]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑[[FSH]] especially if [[hysterectomy]] history and unclear menstrual cycles

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[Ca2+]] 1200 mg
> [[HRT]] if >60yrs, menopause within 10yrs
> [[DEXA]]
> [[colonoscopy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[estrone]] still made in [[fat cell]]
> [[ovarian insufficiency]]
