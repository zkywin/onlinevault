
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> stop of both air flow and respiratory movement
> [[s/apnea]]

> [!info] Etiology
> [[hindbrain]] damage
> upper [[C spine]] lesion
> [[myotonic dystrophy]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> poor sensing of [[PaCO2]] level


> [!danger] Symptoms
> low ventilation while awake, worse when asleep
> [[cheyne-stokes respiration]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[polysomnography]] shows no abdominal effort during apnea
> 5x/hr cessation of breathing > 10 seconds

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[CPAP]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[OSA]]
