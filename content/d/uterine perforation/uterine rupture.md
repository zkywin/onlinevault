
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> prior [[c-section]] [[myomectomy]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[3rd trimester]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> fetal parts palpable abdominally 
> "abdominal protuberance"
> contractions stop with onset of bleeding

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[late deceleration]] in some cases
> loss of station
> "staircase pattern" of tocodynamics/contraction

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[laparotomy]] exploration + [[c-section]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[cervical dystocia]] also loss of station
