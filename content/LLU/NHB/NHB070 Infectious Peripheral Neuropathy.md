Post-herpetic neuralgia is a pain syndrome that remains 4 months after a reactivation episode of [[VZV]]. Unilateral facial paralysis as a result of CN7 inflammation [[bell's palsy]], is called [[ramsay hunt syndrome]] when it is caused by [[VZV]] in the [[a/geniculate ganglion]] and has ear vesicles.

A humoral immune attempt to kill gram negative [[campylobacter jejuni]] using antibodies against LPS can result in [[guillain-barre]], which damages peripheral nerve schwann cells. This causes an acute, flaccid, ascending paralysis.

[[leprosy]] is an intracellular parasite that lives within schwann cells. In a normal host, infection is warded off or has a tuberculoid course with some minor nerve/skin damage. But for people with poor immune systems, it can take on a very severe lepromatous course.

[[botulism]] produces toxins in aerobic environments and ingestion is taking in the toxin, which causes descending paralysis like [[lambert-eaton syndrome]]. Infections of wounds have similar symptoms. #done
