[[venous thrombosis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Detailed Description
> in young adults
> risk factors include [[OCP]], pregnancy, hormone therapy
> most often occlusion at [[a/superior sagital sinus]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> increased cranial pressure
> headache
> [[diplopia]]
> [[s/papilledema]]
> focal weakness of legs
> [[seizure]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> elevated [[D-dimer]]
> CT or MR venogram
> [[bilateral parasagittal ischemic and hemorrhagic lesions]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> anticoagulation
