[[EMERGENT]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[iris]] [[hypoxia]] or [[neovascularization]]
> sudden dilation of the eye (going into dark room)
> worsened by [[Epi]] [[anticholinergics]] [[atropine]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> dilated pupil
> halos around lights

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[goinoscopy]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> ★ laser peripheral iridotomy within 24-48 hours
> [[pilocarpine]] → contracts pupillary sphincter
>  [[osmotic diuretic]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[glaucoma]]
> [[uveitis]] has constricted pupil
> [[subconjunctival hemorrhage]] is painful
