
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> infection within [[synovium]] 

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[MSSA]] #1
> [[kingella kingae]] if < 2 yo
> [[gonorrhoeae]] 40% of 18-30 yo
> [[staphylococcus epidermidis]] of prosthetic joint
> [[streptococcus viridans]] after dental procedure
> [[enterococcus faecium]] after urogenital surgery

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[cartilage]] damage

> [!danger] Symptoms
> single swollen warm joint
> excruciating pain with movement
> associated [[URI]] or [[boil]]
> hip held flexed, abducted, and externally rotated to reduce pain

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[osteomyelitis]]
> [[OA]] [[MS]]
> differential [[vB3]] podagra, [[gout]]
> if range of motion intact [[prepatellar bursitis]]
