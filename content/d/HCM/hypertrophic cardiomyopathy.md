[[autosomal dominant]] 
[[cardiomyopathy]] [[concentric LVH]] 
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[LV]] nonuniform hypertrophy

> [!info] Etiology
> ★ 60-70% Δ [[sarcomere]] β-[[myosin]] heavy chain
> young athletes

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> myofibril disarray → distorted structure
> ↓[[LV]][[EDV]] → [[HOCM]]
> [[vfib]] or [[vtach]] → [[sudden cardiac death]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[syncope]] on exercise

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[EKG]] low [[qrs complex]] amplitude
> "spike and dome" carotid pulse
> dissaray and [[fibrosis]] of [[cardiac muscle]]


> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[restrictive cardiomyopathy]]
> [[concentric LVH]] is totally uniform
> 
> making work easier for the heart with [[digoxin]] [[milrinone]], [[diuretic]] [[nitrate]] [[ACEi]] [[non-DHP CCB]] worsens [[syncope]]
