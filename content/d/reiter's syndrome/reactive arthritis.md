[[seronegative arthropathy]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> inflammation of [[mucosa]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> ★ [[chlamydia]] [[UTI]]
> [[bacterial gastroenteritis]] [[campylobacter jejuni]]
> HLA-B27


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> a few weeks after [[urethritis]] or [[bacterial gastroenteritis]]
> relapsing disease for up to 6 months
> [[sacroiliitis]] 20%

> [!danger] Symptoms
> asymmetric arthritis
> mouth ulcers
> [[conjunctivitis]]
> [[urethritis]] with discharge
> spine, knee, achilles, plantar fascia
> hyperkeratotic vesicles [[palm and sole involving]]
> serpigenous glans penis dermatitis

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[NSAID]]
> [[Abx]] for underlying infection

> [!example] Relationships
> distinguish from [[gonorrhoeae]]
> [[sjogren syndrome]]
> [[transient synovitis]]
