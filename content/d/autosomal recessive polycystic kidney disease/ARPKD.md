[[autosomal recessive]] infantile PKD
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology
> PKHD1 gene

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[pulmonary hypoplasia]] death
> [[oligohydramnios]] [[potter sequence]]
> associated with [[congenital hepatic fibrosis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> large kidneys at birth
> [[portal hypertension]] [[ascites]] in children

> [!question] Objective Findings
> tons of tiny, radial cysts
> cysts only in [[collecting tubule]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> in kids unlike [[ADPKD]] and rarer!
