
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> presentation: 🜲 vertex/cephalic, breech, shoulder
> position: 🜲 occiput anterior, posterior
> lie: 🜲 longitudinal, transverse, oblique

> [!question] Mechanism
> transverse lie can automatically convert
> transverse lie in labor → [[c-section]]
> face presentation: mentum anterior vaginal
> mentum posterior → [[c-section]]

> [!danger] Diseases
> may be worsened by [[spina bifida]]
> if presentation uncertain, do [[US]]

> [!example] Relationships
> no [[external cephalic version]] if in labor
