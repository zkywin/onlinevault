"king ANTHRa's AX"
[[gram positive]] [[rods]] [[encapsulated]] [[aerobe]]
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> spores in soil, animal skins
> skin trauma, inhalation, [[IV drugs]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> protective antigen facilitates factor entry
> edema factor increases [[cAMP]] to slow phagocytosis
> lethal factor [[MMP]] cleaves [[RAS-MAPK pathway]] causes [[necrosis]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> black eschar (with erythematous ring)
> dry cough
> pulmonary hemorrhage/hemorrhagic mediastinitis
> widened mediastinum [[XR]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> chains of bacilli
> spore forming
> protein capsule of poly-D glutamate
> obligate aerobe

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[fluoroquinolones]]
> [[doxycycline]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[botulism]] also spore forming
> bacillus cereus also spore forming ([[food poisoning]] from fried rice)
