
> [!tip] In Short
> [[menopause]]
> unopposed [[estrogen]] by [[progesterone]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> age
> ↑[[estrogen]] exposure from [[obesity]] [[PCOS]] 
> ↓[[progesterone]] from [[menopause]]
> 
> [[tamoxifen]] [[HRT]]
> [[granulosa-theca cell tumor]]
> [[infertility]]
> [[aromatase]] → ↑[[estrone]]
> 
> north america, europe


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> simple 1%
> complex 3%
> simple + atypia 9%
> complex + atypia 27%
> [[endometrial carcinoma]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> postmenopausal uterine bleeding
> [[anovulation]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[TVUS]]
> simple: glands and stroma hypertrophy together
> complex: glands > stroma 
> 
> [[endometrial biopsy]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> 🟊 [[progestin]] high dose (without atypia) 
> with atypia, often get [[endometrial biopsy]]
> oral medroxyprogesterone
> 
> [[hysterectomy]] after children
