[[obstructive lung]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[chronic bronchitis]] + [[emphysema]]

> [!info] Etiology
> [[smoking]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> irreversible obstruction
> respirations driven by [[O2]] drop at [[chemoreceptor]] not [[CO2]]
> [[H flu]] [[community acquired]] [[pneumonia]]
> [[COPD exacerbation]]
> #1 cause of 2° spontaneous [[pneumothorax]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> will cause [[pitting edema]] because [[CO2]] is a vasodilator that increases [[hydrostatic]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> see [[obstructive lung]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[LAMA]] → [[LABA]] → [[corticosteroids]]
> cessation of [[smoking]]
> if [[SpO2]] <[](smoking.md)]] the only proven ↑ survival
> excessive [[O2]] → ↑[[dead space]] → [[hypercapnia]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[emphysema]]
> [[asthma]]
