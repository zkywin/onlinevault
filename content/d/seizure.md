
> [!tip] In Short
> cortical chain reaction

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[EtOH]] [[benzos]] withdrawal
> [[roseola infantum]] febrile
> cortical strokes, tumors
> [[hypoglycemia]]
> 
> kids [[febrile seizure]]
> elderly [[ischemic stroke]]
> adult [[ischemic stroke]]/[[cancer]]/[[trauma]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> #1 [[1° general]] ie. [[absence seizure]]
> [[focal aware]] → [[focal to general]]
> [[focal impaired]] 

> [!danger] Symptoms
> after waking, sleepy "postictal state"
> [[post-ictal state]]
> [[automatism]]
> [[tachycardia]]
> [[pupil dilation]]
> eye deviation ⤩ to the focus
> hand is ⇊ to focus

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[EEG]]
> [[MRI]]
> [[CT no contrast]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> identify a lesion or do metabolic panels
> [[levetiracetam]], [[fosphenytoin]] [[valproate]]
> ⦻ [[cephalosporins]] [[aminophylline]] [[clozapine]] [[bupropion]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[d/epilepsy]] involves recurrent seizures
> [[syncope]] is triggered by standing or emotional trauma and tongue biting is rare
> [[todd paralysis]]
> [[ECT]]
