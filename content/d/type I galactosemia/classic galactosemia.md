[[autosomal recessive]] [[newborn hypoglycemia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> deficiency of [[G1P uridyltransferase]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> baby drinking milk

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑ of galactose1p
> [[PO4-]]/[[ATP]] starvation → [[hemolysis]]
> [[aldose reductase]] → toxic [[galactitol]]
> → [[WBC]] failure → [[e. coli]] [[sepsis]] in neonates!!

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[hepatomegaly]] [[jaundice]] [[failure to thrive]]
> [[cataracts]]
> [[intellectual disability]]
> [[vomiting]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[dipstick]] positive?

> [!abstract] Treatment
> screening mandated
> exclude dietary [[galactose]] and [[lactose]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[galactokinase deficiency]] only cataracts
> [[fructose intolerance]] is analogous
