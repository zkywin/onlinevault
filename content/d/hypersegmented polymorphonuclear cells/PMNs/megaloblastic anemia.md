[[macrocytic anemia]] [[anemia]]
> [!tip] In Short
> [[nucleotide synthesis]] problems
> the cytoplasm matures but the nucleus doesn't

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[pernicious anemia]]
> [[vB9 deficiency]]
> [[vB12 deficiency]]
> [[orotic aciduria]]
> [[MTX]] [[5-fU]] [[hydroxyurea]]
> [[zidovudine]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> enlarged [[RBC]]
> immature nuclei
> ruptured megaloblasts in marrow → ↑[[LDH]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> growth retardation
> [[glossitis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[Hct]]
> high [[MCV]]
> hypersegmented [[neutrophil]] > 6 lobes
> "megaloblasts" in [[bone marrow]]
