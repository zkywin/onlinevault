[[alpha synuclein]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[parkinsonism]] + ⦻ [[ANS]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> age 50-60

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> basal nuclei damage 
> lateral column damage → autonomic problmes
> [[REM sleep behavior disorder]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[parkinsonism]]
> [[ataxia]]
> autonomic: [[orthostatic hypotension]] [[neurogenic bladder]]


> [!question] Tests
> MRI [[l/hot crossed buns]]
> depigmentation of the substantia nigra
> severe atrophy of cerebellum, basal pons, and olivary nuclei

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> younger than [[parkinson's]]
> like [[PSP]], will cause earlier falling
> unlike [[PSP]] has autonomic instability
> NOT [[spinal muscular atrophy]]
