[[HSR 3]] and [[HSR 4]] 
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[PADI]] citrullinated neoantigen
> "morning stiffness"

> [!info] Etiology
> female
> HLA-DR4
>[[pneumoconioses]] "caplan syndrome"

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis]]
> [[PADI]] citrullinates self antigens
> [[Th1 cell]] [[Th17 cell]] activation [[HSR 4]]
> [[macrophage]] [[TNFα]]
> [[synovium]] proliferation resulting in [[pannus]]
> [[plasma cell]] [[rheumatoid factor]] [[HSR 3]]
> rheumatoid nodules with [[fibrinoid necrosis]]
> 
> [[osteoporosis]]
> [[cancer]]
> [[felty syndrome]]
> [[secondary amyloidosis]]
> [[atlantoaxial instability]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> morning stiffness > 1hr, better after movement
> spared DIP
> subcutaneous nodules
> ulnar deviation
> [[tenosynovitis]]
> boutanniere, swan neck
> fibrous ankylosis (near fused joints)
> bilateral [[carpal tunnel syndrome]]
> [[C1]] [[C2]] [[subluxation]] [[atlantoaxial instability]]
> [[keratitis]] [[scleritis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[leukocytosis]] 2-50k
> [[anti-CCP]]
> polyclonal gammopathy
> rheumatoid nodules [[fibrinoid necrosis]]
> joint space narrowing
> erosions
> periarticular osteopenia


> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[DMARD]] [[anti-malarials]] [[MTX]] [[leflunomide]]
> [[corticosteroids]]
> [[NSAID]]
> [[sulfasalazine]]
