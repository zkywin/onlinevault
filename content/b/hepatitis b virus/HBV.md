hepatitis B virus
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> proliferative phase: [[HBsAg]] on [[hepatocyte]] [[MHC 1]]
> [[Tc cell]] → [[FAS ligand]] → [[hepatocyte]] death
> 
> integrative phase: [[DNA]] incorporation → [[HCC]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[maculopapular rash]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> screening if high risk/[[pregnancy]]
> 
> [[HBV DNA]] > 2000 means active
> [[HBsAg]] = active with ↑ [[AST÷ALT]]
> [[anti-HBc]] = infected ONLY (IgM early IgG late)
> 
> [[anti-HBs]]= immunized or previous infection
> [[HBeAg]] predicts infectivity

> [!abstract] Treatment
> exposure/vaccination → lifelong immunity
> no post-exposure prophylaxis if vaccinated
> [[HBV vaccine]]
> [[HBVIg]]
> 
> treat if persistent > 4wks, combo with other infections, [[ALT]], [[cirrhosis]], high viral load
> traditionally [[interferon]] + [[lamivudine]]
> [[entecavir]] [[tenofovir]]
