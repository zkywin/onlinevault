[[myeloproliferative disorder]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> JAK2[[Δ]] [[JAK]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> increased [[RBC]]
> sluggish blood flow
> [[thrombosis]] increases viscosity
> "spent phase failure" [[myelofibrosis]]
> 
> [[AML]] [[CML]]
> [[gout]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> facial [[flushing]]
> aquagenic [[s/itching/pruritis]] after hot shower
> [[DVT]] [[bud chiari syndrome]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[Hct]], [[polycythemia]] 
> absence of [[RCC]] [[EPO]]
> absence of lung hypoxia

> [!abstract] Treatment
> phlebotomy/blood letting
> [[hydroxyurea]]
> [[aspirin]] for thrombosis prophylaxis

> [!example] Relationships
> [[waldenstrom macroglobinemia]] also thickens blood
> [[polycythemia]] from [[burn]]
