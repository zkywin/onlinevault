> [!info] Definitions
> Glial brain tumors are [[stain/GFAP]] positive, excluding [[macrophage]]
> Tumor grading ranges from slow-growing [[WHO 1]], infiltrative [[WHO 2]], ugly [[WHO 3]], and necrotic [[WHO 4]].
> "Blastoma" referes to an aggressive tumor in children with the exception of [[hemangioblastoma]]

Leakage from fragile vessels show up as [[radiocontrast]] contrast enhancement. [[ring-enhancing mass]] often represents a necrotic center with a leaky ring. [[homogenous enhancing mass]] is probably not necrotic, and [[mural-nodule-enhancing mass]] tend to be low-grade tumors. [[calcification]] in the brain means either [[craniopharyngeoma]] or [[pineal gland]].

[[astrocytoma]] [[coffee bean nuclei]] tumors have many subtypes. Additional tumors include[[oligodendroglioma]] with [[fried egg cells]], [[ependymoma]] with [[perivascular pseudorosettes]], [[choroid plexus tumors]] with [[papillae]], and [[ganglioglioma]] which are pretty much [[pleomorphic xanthoastrocytoma]]
