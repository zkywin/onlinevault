
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> stand-in for urine [[Na+]] 
> loss of [[HCO3-]] can inappropriately affect urine [[Na+]] and skew it

> [!question] Mechanism
> low [[urine Cl-]] is a key sign of high [[HCO3-]] in the entire system

> [!danger] Diseases
> low implies [[hypokalemia]] and [[metabolic alkalosis]] with [[vomiting]]
> high implies [[bartter syndrome]] or [[gitelman syndrome]] or even [[2° hyperaldosteronism]]
