
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> long [[qt interval]] > 440ms

> [!info] Etiology
> congenital prolongation, [[Na+]] [[K+]] channel defect
> [[romano-ward syndrome]]
> [[jervell and lange-nielsen syndrome]]
> 
> [[anti-arrhythmics]]
> [[arsenic]]
> [[chloroquine]]
> [[diuretic]]
> [[anti-emetics]]
> [[antifungals]]
> [[atazanavir]]
> [[opioid]]
> [[levofloxacin]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[sudden cardiac death]]
> [[torsades de pointes]] [[vfib]]
> [[hypokalemia]] reduces [[Na-K pump]] and [[IKr]] repolarization


> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> p wave more than half way between Q-Q

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[HCO3-]] will narrow the QT
> [[β1 blockers]] → ↓[[sympathetic]] → ↓[[qt interval]]

> [!example] Relationships
> increases risk of [[tachyarrhythmia]], should any [[Na+]] current come in
