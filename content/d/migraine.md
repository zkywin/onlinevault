> [!tip] Condensed Description
> intense cortical depolarization

> [!info] Etiology
> [[d/hemiplegic migraine]]
> [[CN V]] overactivity → innervates blood vessels
> triggers: stress, bright lights, odors, food

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[CN V]] releases [[CGRP]], [[substance P]]
> spreading depolarization
> irritation of [[CN V]], [[meninges]], vessels

> [!danger] Symptoms
> 60% unilateral throbbing/pulsing pain
> 4-72 hours
> [[photophobia]]
> auras
> [[vomiting]]
> [[nausea]]

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment (3+ days > 2+/month)
> in acute: [[anti-emetics]]
> abortive: [[sumatriptan]] [[ergotamine]] [[NSAID]], [[acetaminophen]]
> prophylactic: [[β1 blockers]], [[topirimate]], [[valproate]]
> other: [[amitryptiline]] [[NSAID]] [[botox]] 
> 
> in [[pregnancy]] [[acetaminophen]] [[propanolol]]
