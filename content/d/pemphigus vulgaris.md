[[HSR 2]] intraepidermal
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> autoimmune [[desmosome]] destruction
> VERY fragile, flaccid blisters

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors 
> [[IgG]] against desmoglein (3 and 1)
> affects older individuals 40-60
> [[paraneoplastic]] [[non-hodgkin lymphoma]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[skin]] and oral [[mucosa]]
> splitting of [[stratum spinosum]]
> [[stratum basale]] anchored by [[hemidesmosome]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> thin walled blisters easily ruptured
> [[crust]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> fish net of [[IgG]] on [[IF]]
> "acantholysis" = separation of [[keratinocyte]]
> "tombstone appearance" of [[stratum basale]]
> [[nikolsky sign]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> systemic [[corticosteroids]]
> [[mycophenolate]] or [[rituximab]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[bullous pemphigoid]] tough hemidesmosome defect
> [[SSSS]] also nikolsky
> [[SJS]] differential
