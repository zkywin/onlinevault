[[inflammatory myopathy]] [[polymyositis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology
> 40-50 yr old women
> ∝ [[adenocarcinoma]] [[ovary]] [[lung]] [[pancreas]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[IFN-α]] [[IFN-β]]
> [[Th cell]] inflammation and atrophy at [[perimesium]] 
> [[lymphocytic vasculitis]] 
> 55% risk of [[cancer]] ovarian, gastric
> [[restrictive lung]]
> [[enteric vasculitis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> PROXIMAL [[hypotonia]]
> myalgia
> [[heliotrope rash]]
> [[malar rash]], shawl rash, cape sign
> [[calcinosis]] 
> [[gottron lesions]]
> in children, erythroderma on the head
> shawl and face rash

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[CK]]
> [[anti-Jo-1]]
> [[tubuloreticular inclusions]]


> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[IVIg]]
> [[corticosteroids]]
 > screen for [[cancer]]
 

> [!example] Relationships
> [[polymyositis]] but worse
> [[hyperthyroidism]], [[hypokalemia]], [[systemic scleroderma]], [[sarcoidosis]], [[secondary amyloidosis]]
