Constitutional: Weakness, fatigue, weight change (loss or gain), night sweats, fever/chills, sensitivity to heat or cold, sleeping pattern

Skin: Rashes, itching, dryness, sores, changes in hair or nails changes in color.

Head: Headache, injury, dizziness, light headedness, fainting, loss of consOciousness (falls), growths on head.

Eyes: Pain, change/double/blurred vision, spots, flashing light, inflammation/redness, discharge or glasses, chronic or past eye disorders.

Ears: Loss of hearing, tinnitus, pain, infections, discharge or vertigo.

Nose: Loss of smell, frequent colds, hay fever, obstruction, excess discharge, epistaxis

Mouth/Throat: Sores, sore throat, bleeding gums, dry mouth, hoarseness, tooth pain, sense of lump/mass in throat with swallowing, dentures or change in taste.

Respiratory: Shortness of breath at rest or with exertion, cough, hemoptysis, sputum (color, quantity) production, dyspnea, wheezing, pleurisy, snoring/stop breathing

Cardiovascular/Heart: Chest pain, discomfort or pressure, palpitations/irregular heartbeat, orthopnea, syncope, PND-paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, dyspnea at rest or exertion, cyanosis, peripheral edema, claudication, ulcers on legs.

Gastrointestinal/GI: Change in appetite, dysphagia, heartburn, abdominal distress or pain, belching or excess gas, abdominal enlargement, nausea, vomiting, vomiting of blood, rectal bleeding, melena, constipation or diarrhea, hemorrhoids.

Genitourinary/GU: Dysuria, frequency, polyuria, nocturia, urgency, hematuria, incontinence, burning or pain during urination, urinary infection, kidney or flank pain, kidney stones,

Male: Sores on penis, impotence, libido, pain with intercourse, testicular pain or swelling

Female: Dysmenorrhea, PMS, vaginal discharge, itching, sores, lumps, dysfunctional uterine bleeding.

Endocrine: Excessive sweating, thirst or hunger, change in glove or shoe size, polyuria, weight gain or loss, palpitations, heat or cold intolerance.

Blood/lymphatic: Easy bleeding or bruising, clotting problems, lymph node enlargement or pain.

Joints and muscles: muscle cramps, muscle pain, muscle weakness, pain in joints, swollen joints, stiffness or deformity of joints.

Neurologic: Numbness or loss of sensation, extremity weakness, facial droop, changes in speech, tremors or other involuntary movements, seizures, dizziness, gait abnormality, coordination abnormality, right or left sided weakness, memory loss or depression.

Psychiatric: Nervousness, tension, anxious, mood (sad or depressed), memory change, confusion, hallucinations, suicidical or homicidal ideation, if relevant
