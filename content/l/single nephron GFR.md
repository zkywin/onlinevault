[[nephron loss]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> = total [[GFR]] ÷ [[nephron]] count

> [!question] Mechanism
> 1. compensatory rise in function of remaining [[nephron]]
> 2. hypertrophy of [[glomerulus]] and [[podocyte]] temporarily abates [[hydrostatic]]
> 3. eventually, podocytes detach and hyperfiltration occurs
> 4. the [[nephron]] undergoes hypertrophy to absorb the hyperfiltrate

> [!danger] Diseases
> [[single nephron GFR]] up while overall [[GFR]] falls in [[CKD]]
> both fall in [[AKI]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[RAAS]] activates inflammatory pathways that result in glomerulosclerosis and tubular fibrosis
