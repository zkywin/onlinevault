[[SBO]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> "intestinal problem"
> lack of neurons in [[large intestine]]
> "transition zone"

> [!info] Etiology
> ∝ [[down syndrome]]
> [[RET]] loss of function
> [[neural crest]] migration failure
> absent [[meissner's plexus]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> failure to pass meconium in 48 hrs
> explosive diarrhea
> [[bilious emesis]]
> [[squirt sign]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> cone shape "transition zone" barium enema 
> rectal suction biopsy to get [[submucosa]]
> anorectal manometry if > 1 mo old

> [!abstract] Treatment
> resect the area without the [[meissner's plexus]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[meconium ileus]] dilated small bowel no squirt (also higher on imaging)
> [[volvulus]] no squirt
