[[LMN]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ⦻ [[CN VII]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[HSV-1]] reactivation
> [[sarcoidosis]] (heerfordt syndrome)
> [[forceps delivery]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[facial paralysis]] complete
> [[synkinesis]] failed [[wallerian degeneration]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[CN VII]] unilateral facial paralysis, loss of anterior 2/3 taste
> sensitive ear ([[a/stapedius]] damage)
> not able to close one eye
> pain behind the ear

> [!question] Tests

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[ramsay hunt syndrome]] has CN8 problems
> [[↓CBT]] [[lacunar stroke]] only lower facial weakness
> further workup ie. mass if hearing loss too
