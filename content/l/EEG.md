electroencephalogram
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> measures slow postsynaptic potentials, not fast

> [!info] Frequencies
> Beta >13 Hz
> Alpha >8 Hz (waking)
> Theta >4 Hz (drowsy)
> Delta <4 Hz (deep sleep)

> [!danger] Pathologies
> spikes and sharp waves are not good
> [[generalized activity EEG]]
> [[EEG centrotemporal sharp waves]]
