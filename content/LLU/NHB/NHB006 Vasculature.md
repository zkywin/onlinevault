anterior circulation originates from [[a/internal carotid]]
[[ACA]] supplies the medial cortex
[[MCA]] supplies the lateral cortex, giving off [[lenticulostriate artery]] to supply [[posterior internal capsule]] and [[genu]]

posterior circulation comes from [[a/vertebral artery]]
[[medulla]] is supplied by [[a/anterior spinal artery]], [[a/posterior inferior cerebellar artery/PICA]]
[[cerebellum]] is supplied by [[a/posterior inferior cerebellar artery/PICA]],  [[basilar artery]], and [[a/superior cerebellar artery]]
[[pons]] is supplied by [[basilar artery]]
[[midbrain]], [[temporal]], [[occipital]], [[thalamus]], [[posterior internal capsule]] are all supplied at least in part by [[PCA]]

Venous drainage occurs through the [[a/superior sagital sinus]] and [[a/inferior sagital sinus]]. There is more that has been deemed low priority information #done
