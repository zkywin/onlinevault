[[central cyanosis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> L heart does all the work


> [!info] Etiology

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ⦻ [[tricuspid valve]] ⦻ [[RA]] → [[RV]]
> [[RA]] → [[ASD]] → [[LV]]
> (bypasses [[RV]])
> 
> [[LV]] or [[aorta]] [[PDA]] → [[RV]] → [[PA]] 
> [[left to right shunt]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[cyanosis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[left axis deviation]]
> decreased pulmonary markings (under circulation)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[alprostadil]]
> surgery

> [!example] Relationships
> like [[transposition of great vessels]] because lungs are bypassed
