[[hypertrophic cardiomyopathy]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> hypertrophy of ventricular septum

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> bartonella
> [[diabetic embryopathy]] → ↑[[insulin]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[mitral valve]] is sucked foward and blocks flow

> [!danger] Symptoms
> ↓[[preload]] and ↓[[afterload]]
> → ↓[[LV]] volume 
> → worsened obstruction 
> → louder murmur

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↓[[preload]] → ↑murmur
> [[MSM]] at [[2nd ICS right]] [[aortic band]]
> ![[Pasted image 20231213162252.png]]
> [[left axis deviation]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[β1 blockers]] → increase filling time
> [[non-DHP CCB]]
> adequate hydration → keep open
> [[diabetic embryopathy]] self resolving

> [!example] Relationships
> dynamic auscultation like [[mitral regurg]]
