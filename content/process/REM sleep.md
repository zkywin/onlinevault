[[process/sleep]] rapid eye-movement sleep
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> episodes of REM get longer through the night
> most but not all dreams are in REM sleep
> Of all neurotransmiters, [[ACh]] from the [[pons]] is the most important for [[REM sleep]] 

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[ACh]] → ↑[[CN X]] tone


> [!danger] Symptoms
> nightmares
> loss of motor tone

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[EEG]] theta waves
> [[EOG]] conjugate ocular movement
> [[EMG]] no movement at all

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[NREM sleep]] has some [[EMG]] activity and no [[EOG]]
> in elderly [[REM sleep]] disorders may indicate neurodeneration
> [[REM sleep behavior disorder]]
