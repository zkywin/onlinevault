[[antifungals]] -conazoles
"pine CONE"
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[antifungals]] with [[cryptococcal meningitis]] support

> [!info] Mechanisms
> inhibits [[ergosterol]] synthesis from lanosterol
> inhibit fungal cytochrome 14α-demethylase

> [!danger] Uses
> [[candida]] esophagitis in [[HIV]]
> [[cryptococcal meningitis]]
> [[aspergillus fumigatus]]

> [!abstract] Formulations
> [[imidazole]]
> [[fluconazole]]
> [[voriconazole]]
> [[itraconazole]]
> [[clotrimazole]]
> [[miconazole]]
> [[ketoconazole]]
> [[isavuconazole]]

> [!warning] Metabolism

> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> [[CYP450]] inhibitor
> [[QTc prolongation]]
> GI distress
> [[hepatotoxicity]]
> [[gynecomastia]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[terbinafine]] also blocks [[ergosterol]] synthesis
> [[echinocandin]] does not have meningitis support
