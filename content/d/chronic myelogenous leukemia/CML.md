[[granulocytes]] [[myeloproliferative disorder]] [[leukemia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[Ph chromosome]] BCR-ABL fusion gene

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> chronic stage: asymptomatic < 2% blasts
> accelerated stage: [[leukocytosis]]
> [[blast crisis]]: > 20% blasts → 2/3 [[AML]] (1/3 [[ALL]])
> 
> [[myelofibrosis]] → [[extramedullary hematopoiesis]]
> [[thrombosis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[fatigue]]
> [[splenomegaly]]
> [[thrombocytopenia]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑ myelocytes (neutrophil precursors)
> ↓ [[WBC alk phos]]
> ↑ [[granulocytes]] [[myeloblast]]
> rarely high [[basophil]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[tyrosine kinase inhibitors]] management
> [[bone marrow transplant]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[AML]]
> [[leukemoid reaction]] has ↑[[WBC alk phos]] ↑[[neutrophil]]
