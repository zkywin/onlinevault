
> [!info] Definitions
> [[d/dementia]]: also known as "Major Neurocognitive Disorder" involves 4 A's:  [[s/amnesia]] [[language]] [[apraxic gait]] [[s/agnosia]]
> [[d/encephalopathy]]: is more sudden, less alert

[[mild cognitive impairment]] is the precursor to [[alzheimer's]], which is a [[Aβ amyloid]] cause of [[dementia]] unlike [[lewy body dementia]]. However, those two in addition to [[vascular dementia]] can be seen together in [[mixed dementia]]. Whereas the top 3 dementias involve memory and cognition, [[frontotemporal dementia]] involves social behavior. All of them eventually develop [[parkinsonism]], which have a different kind of muscle dysfunction than [[huntington's disease]].

Rarer forms of dementia include [[creutzfeld-jacob disease]], which can be familial, [[HIV]], and [[d/korsakoff's syndrome]] as a result of [[wernicke's encephalopathy]]. [[normal pressure hydrocephalus]] is over-diagnosed and can usually be narrowed down to one of the mentioned diseases. #done
