[[IUGR]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> before 37 weeks
> late preterm 34-37 weeks
> VERY preterm < 28 weeks

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> corrected age = actual age - weeks premature
> [[PPROM]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[hypothermia]] [[hypoglycemia]] [[hypotension]]
> [[hyperbilirubinemia]] [[hypocalcemia]]
> 
> [[NRDS]] [[NEC]] [[PDA]]
> [[germinal matrix hemorrhage]]
> [[periventricular leukomalacia]]
> [[neonatal jaundice]] [[kernicterus]]
> [[retinopathy]]
> [[hydrops fetalis]]
> [[sepsis]]
> [[TORCH infection]]
> [[zika virus]]


> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> negative [[fetal fibronectin]] can rule it out

> [!abstract] Treatment
> relax/delay labor using [[tocolytics]]
> [[MgSO4]] for ppx for [[cerebral palsy]]
> monitor for [[Fe deficiency]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[postterm pregnancy]]
> [[PPROM]]
