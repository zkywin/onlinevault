[[CHRONIC]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> mechanical [[chondrocyte]] injury
> "afternoon soreness"

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> Primary Cause: biomechanical stress
> Secondary Causes include [[hemochromatosis]], [[alkaptonuria]], [[rheumatoid arthritis]], [[acromegaly]], [[syphilis]], [[CPPD]], [[meniscus]] tear
> [[spondylosis]] [[obesity]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> injury disrupts [[MMP]] [[ADAMTS4]] control
> [[collagen 2]] [[proteoglycan]] damage
> [[TGF-β]] cause [[bone spur]]
> chunks breaking [[joint mice]] 
> complete cartilage loss and [[subchondral bone cyst]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> morning stiffness < 20 min
> better with rest, worsens with movement
> PIP bouchard and DIP heberden nodes [[bone spur]]
> hip OA felt at groin
> [[bunion]]
> [[crepitus]]
> heberden DIP nodule, bouchard PIP nodule

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[XR]] joint space narrowing (asymmetric)
> [[XR osteophyte.png]]
> [[XR]] DIP damage

> [!abstract] Treatment
> nonpharmacological, [[PT]]
> [[NSAID]] [[duloxetine]] [[capsaicin]]
> [[arthroplasty]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[CPPD]] comorbid [[synovial fluid]] crystal formation
> [[rheumatoid arthritis]] no DIP
> [[intervertebral disc]] disease
> [[ankylosing spondylitis]]
> [[frailty]]
