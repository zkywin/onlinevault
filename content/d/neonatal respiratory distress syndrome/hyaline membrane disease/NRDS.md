like [[ARDS]] in kids
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> lack [[alveolar surfactant]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[prematurity]] especially < 30 wks
> [[diabetic embryopathy]] → direct ↓[[alveolar surfactant]]
> [[c-section]] 
> (vaginal birth stress ↑[[cortisol]] ↑[[alveolar surfactant]])
> maternal [[DM]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> maternal [[hyperglycemia]] → ↑[[insulin]] from fetus → ↓[[cortisol]] → ↓[[sphingomyelin]]
> ↓[[alveolar surfactant]] → inflammation → airway resistance
> [[atelectasis]] and [[VQ mismatch]]
> [[respiratory acidosis]]
> ∝ [[PDA]]
> [[O2]] → [[BPD]] [[retinopathy]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> high respiratory effort after birth
> [[hypoxemia]]
> [[central cyanosis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[LS ratio]] < 1.5
> ethanol foam stability test

> [!abstract] Treatment
> #1 [[CPAP]] [[O2]]
> #2 [[intubation]] artificial surfactant
> 
> [[O2]] can cause [[free radicals]] damage, blindness [[BPD]]
> give maternal [[corticosteroids]] [[betamethasone]]


> [!example] Relationships
> [[insulin]] inhibits [[alveolar surfactant]] production
> [[ARDS]]
> [[emphysema]]
> [[transient tachypnea of newborn]]
