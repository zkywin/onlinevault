[[anti-platelet drugs]] [[NSAID]]
> [!info] Mechanism
> irreversible [[COX]] inhibitor
> decreases [[TxA2]] [[clotting cascade]]
> decreases [[prostaglandin]]
> lower [[plt]] activation

> [!danger] Uses
> [[kawasaki disease]]
> [[colon cancer]]
> [[preeclampsia]]
> [[CAD]] (no longer recommended if 10 yrs risk > 10%)

> [!abstract] Formulations
> 300-2400 mg/day antipyretic [[analgesics]]
> [[sulfasalazine]]

> [!warning] Metabolism
> treat OD with [[HCO3-]] to excrete out urine

> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> ⚠ kids [[reye's syndrome]]
> [[fever]] [[tinnitus]] [[tachypnea]]
> ↑[[leukotriene]] → "pseudoallergy"
> [[medulla]] → [[respiratory alkalosis]] 6-8hrs → [[HAGMA]]
> [[AERD]]
> [[liver]] damage

> [!example] Relationships
> [[bernard-soulier disease]] also aggregation interference
> no longer recommended for CVD prophylaxis
> not affected by [[lithium]] levels
