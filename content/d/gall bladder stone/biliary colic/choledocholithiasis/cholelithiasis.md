[[extrahepatic cholestasis]]
> [!tip] In Short
> [[bile]] imbalance

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> 40 years old, hispanic, multiparity (Fat Fertile Forty)
> elderly implies cancer
> 
> ↓ motility from [[fasting]], [[TPN]]
> ↑[[cholesterol]] ↓[[bile salts]] [[lecithin]]
> [[cirrhosis]]
> ⦻ [[ileum]] absorption [[crohn's]]
> [[fatigue]] [[CF]]
> [[bile acid resin]]
> [[fibrates]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> high [[cholesterol]] or [[direct bilirubin]]
> [[cholesterol stones]] [[bilirubin stones]]
> [[acute cholecystitis]] 
> [[choledocolithiasis]]
> [[porcelain gallbladder]] [[gallbladder carcinoma]]
> [[acute pancreatitis]] if the [[ampulla of vater]] opening compressed

> [!danger] Symptoms
> often asymptomatic
> [[biliary colic]]
> pain when [[CCK]] signals contraction
> 30 min pain before subside
> fatty meals brings on

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[US]] is the best first test
> maybe [[ERCP]] later
> not visible on [[XR]]
> [[CT]] is less sensitive

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[cholecystectomy]]
> [[ursodeoxycholic acid]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[progesterone]] slows [[gallbladder]] emptying
> [[acalculous cholecystitis]]
