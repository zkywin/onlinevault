> poor exhale ↑[[TLC]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[chronic bronchitis]]
> [[emphysema]]
> [[asthma]]
> [[bronchiectasis]]
> [[COPD]] 

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> trapping of air
> inflated lung
> weak diaphragm

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[pulsus paradoxus]]
> deep breaths

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑ [[TLC]] (air trapping)
> ↑ [[FRC]]
> ↓ [[FEV1]], [[FVC]], [[FEV1÷FVC]]


> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships

> combo with [[restrictive lung]] = [[sarcoidosis]]
