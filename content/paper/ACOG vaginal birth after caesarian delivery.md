The document "Vaginal Birth After Cesarean Delivery" is an updated practice bulletin by ACOG, focused on the clinical management and considerations for trial of labor after cesarean delivery (TOLAC). Below is a summarized overview:

### Key Highlights:

1. **TOLAC Definition and Benefits**:
    
    - TOLAC refers to a planned vaginal delivery attempt after a prior cesarean delivery.
    - Successful TOLAC resulting in vaginal birth after cesarean (VBAC) offers benefits such as:
        - Reduced maternal morbidity.
        - Avoidance of major abdominal surgery.
        - Lower risks of complications in future pregnancies.
2. **Risks and Candidacy**:
    
    - Risks include uterine rupture (0.5%-0.9% with low-transverse incisions) and complications if TOLAC fails.
    - Ideal candidates:
        - One prior low-transverse cesarean incision.
        - No contraindications like placenta previa or prior uterine rupture.
    - Special considerations for twin gestations, obesity, macrosomia, or uncertain scar types.
3. **Management and Monitoring**:
    
    - Continuous fetal heart rate monitoring is essential during TOLAC.
    - Epidural analgesia is safe and may improve patient comfort without increasing risks.
    - Facilities offering TOLAC must have immediate access to emergency cesarean delivery capabilities.
4. **Success Predictors**:
    
    - Higher VBAC likelihood in women with prior vaginal delivery or non-recurring cesarean indications.
    - Lower success rates associated with labor induction, advanced maternal age, obesity, or macrosomia.
5. **Counseling and Decision-Making**:
    
    - Shared decision-making is critical, considering individual risks, success probability, and patient preferences.
    - VBAC calculators can assist in estimating success probabilities and tailoring discussions.
6. **Contraindications**:
    
    - High-risk uterine scars (classical or T-incision).
    - Situations requiring immediate delivery where resources for emergency care are unavailable.
7. **Induction and Augmentation**:
    
    - Induction remains an option but carries a higher risk of uterine rupture.
    - Prostaglandin agents, especially misoprostol, are contraindicated at term.
8. **Facility Guidelines**:
    
    - TOLAC should be conducted in settings with adequate resources for immediate surgical intervention.
    - Home birth is contraindicated due to unpredictability of complications.
