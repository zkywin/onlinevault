chromosome 17 [[neurocutaneous syndromes]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> nerve tumor suppressor mutation

> [!info] Detailed Description
> neurofibromin[[Δ]] tumor suppressor
> rampant [[RAS-MAPK pathway]] signaling
> half sporadic
> [[autosomal dominant]]  

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumor]]
> [[meningioma]]
> [[pheochromocytoma]]
> [[optic glioma]]
> [[berry aneurysm]] 
> [[2° HTN]]
> [[CML]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[cafe au lait spots]], axillary, inguinal freckling
> [[lisch nodules]]
> [[neurofibroma]]
> short stature, bone problems

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[S100]] stain
> vascular abnormalities of kidney

> [!abstract] Treatment
> regular ophthalmology appointments
> prepare to treat malignant neoplasms

> [!example] Relationships
> [[d/legius syndrome]] is very similar but mutation of [[g/SPRED1]]
> [[meningioma]] [[astrocytoma]] risk factor
