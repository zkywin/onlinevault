
[[benign neoplasm]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> fascicle [[smooth muscle]] [[muscle tumor]]
> luminal/submucosal (bleeders)
> intermural
> subserosal (mass effect)

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors (1/4 white, 1/2 black)
> in the [[uterus]]
> 70-80% present middle aged women
> [[estrogen]] [[progesterone]] receptors

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> mostly asymptomatic
> 
> [[carneous degeneration]]
> may self-degenerate [[menopause]]
> prolapse "labor pains"
> [[infertility]]
> 
> DO NOT become [[leiomyosarcoma]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[menorrhagia]]
> [[dysmenorrhea]]
> irregularly enlarged uterus
> "week sized uterus"
> "finger breadths below umbilicus"

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[US]] pelvis
> [[sonohysterogram]]
> [[PAS stain]]
> soft tissue type suspicious for [[leiomyosarcoma]]
> whorled pattern of uterine smooth muscle bundles

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[OCP]], [[progestin]] [[GnRH]] agonist, [[hormonal IUD]]
> [[prostaglandin]] inhibitors
> if obstructing exit, [[c-section]]
> 
> [[uterine artery embolization]]
> [[myomectomy]]
> most common indication for [[hysterectomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> may get smaller during [[pregnancy]] due to diversion of blood flow
