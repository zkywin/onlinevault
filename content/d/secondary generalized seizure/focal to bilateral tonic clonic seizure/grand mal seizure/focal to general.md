AKA "grand mal" [[seizure]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> start from focal seizure

> [!info] Detailed Description
> spread to [[frontal eye field]] will disable contralateral [[PPRF]]
> [[lactic acidosis]] → [[HAGMA]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> eyes deviate towards the shaking
> truncal musculature contraction
> tonic phase (stiff)
> clonic phase (shaking)
> tongue biting

> [!question] Objective Findings


> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> can start as [[focal aware]] or [[focal impaired]]
