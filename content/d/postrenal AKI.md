[[AKI]] bilateral [[urinary obstruction]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> blocked [[ureter]]
> [[hydronephrosis]] bilateral
>  ischemia of juxtamedullary nephrons → "post-obstructive diuresis"

> [!info] Etiology
> pressure rises within [[nephron]] and [[bowman's capsule]]
> [[glomerulus]] arterioles are squeezed by the pressure, [[TxA2]], [[AT2]]
> low [[GFR]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[oliguria]] to anuria
> [[post-obstructive diuresis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[hydronephrosis]]
> [[BUN Cr ratio]] > 15 or varies
> [[azotemia]]
> [[FENa]] and [[Uosm]] normal
> 
> long-term causes [[BUN Cr ratio]] < 15, abnormal [[FENa]] and [[Uosm]]
> channels become downregulated

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[BPH]]
> pelvic cancers
> congenital urinary abnormalities
