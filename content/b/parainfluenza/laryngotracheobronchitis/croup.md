[[paramyxovirus]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> 6mo - 3 years

> [!warning] Pathogenesis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> barking [[cough]]
> inspiratory [[stridor]] worse with crying
> [[fever]]
> [[rhinorrhea]]
> worsened by dry air

> [!question] Objective Findings
> "steeple sign"

> [!abstract] Treatment
> cold air
> [[dexamethasone]] oral + humidifier if [[SpO2]] decent
> racemic [[Epi]] to reopen the throat + [[O2]]
> rarely [[intubation]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[bacterial tracheitis]] not improved with [[Epi]]
> viral [[URI]] not as severe
> [[epiglottitis]] drooling
