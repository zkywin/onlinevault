[[nephrotic syndrome]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Detailed Description
> glycation of efferent arteriole → ↑[[GFR]] → 
> glomerular hyperfiltration → sclerosis
> ⦻ [[GBM]] negative charge → [[microalbuminuria]]
> ↑[[collagen 4]] → thickened [[GBM]] and [[mesangium]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[arteriosclerosis]]
> [[pyelonephritis]]
> papillary necrosis

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[proteinuria]] ↑[[ACR]]
> [[kimmelstiel-wilson nodule]] or diffuse sclerosis

> [!abstract] Treatment
> control [[BP]]
> [[ACEi]] for [[nephrotic syndrome]] if [[ACR]] > 30
> 
> increase [[insulin]] at low stage [[CKD]]
> decrease [[insulin]] at high stage [[CKD]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[hypertensive nephrosclerosis]]
> most important cause of [[CKD]]
> may be confused with [[goodpasture syndrome]]
