
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> binding to [[CD21]]
> [[B cell]] clonal in lymph node

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> reactive [[CD8]] [[Tc cell]] → [[hepatomegaly]]
> [[burkitt lymphoma]]
> [[nasopharyngeal carcinoma]]
> [[cold AIHA]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> risk of upper airway obstruction
> tonsillar enlargement
> [[splenomegaly]] → risk of rupture
> prolonged fatigue
> [[pharyngitis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[monospot test]] may be negative early on
> 🟊 serologic viral capsid antigen

> [!abstract] Treatment
> viscous [[lidocaine]]
> in kids ⦻ [[aspirin]]
> [[corticosteroids]]
> avoid contact sports 3-4 weeks 
> (risk [[spleen]] rupture)

> [!example] Relationships
> [[peritonsillar abscess]]
> [[CMV]] if monospot negative
> [[gonorrhoeae]] disseminated is nontender
