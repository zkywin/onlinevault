
> [!tip] In Short
> painless dilation at [[2nd trimester]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> 2+ previous [[prematurity]] or pregnancy loss
> shortened cervix < 2.5 cm at [[2nd trimester]] [[US]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> dilated/effaced cervix on exam
> can cause spotting

> [!question] Objective Findings
> "funneled" lower uterine segment
> [[US]] at [[2nd trimester]] (not 3rd due to physiologic shortening)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> incidental short cervix → topical [[progesterone]]
> [[cerclage]]
> don't exercise please
