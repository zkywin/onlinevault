acute interstitial nephritis, drug induced, hypersensitivity, analgesic hypersensitivity
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[HSR 4]] of kidney
> [[ATN]] is toxic/necrosis while [[AIN]] is an allergic reaction

> [!info] Etiology
> D [[diuretic]]
> R [[rifampin]]
> A [[Abx]] [[penicillins]] [[cephalosporins]]
> I [[PPI]]
> N [[NSAID]]
> S [[sulfonamides]]
> [[pyelonephritis]]
> [[multiple myeloma]] [[primary amyloidosis]] [[sarcoidosis]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> Analgesic nephropathy: more than 2kg of analgesics consumed, sometimes [[acute renal papillary necrosis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[oliguria]]
> [[fever]]
> [[maculopapular rash]]
> [[dark urine]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> normal [[nephron]] but inflammation in [[interstitium]]
> scant [[neutrophil]]
> [[eosinophil]] in urine (from drug-allergy)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> stop the drug solves it

> [!example] Relationships
> [[ATN]] doesn't heal as well
