[[EMERGENT]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> air in the [[pleura]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> primary spontaneous: bleb rupture young thin male
> secondary spontaneous: rupture of [[emphysema]]
> puncture
> [[marfan syndrome]]
> [[COPD]]
> [[cancer]]
> [[lung abscess]]
> [[pneumonia]]
> [[Tb]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> intrapleural pressure = atmospheric pressure
> ⦻ intrapleural pressure → ↑[[TLC]] 
> → ↓[[compliance]] ([[atelectasis]])
> [[tension pneumothorax]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[crepitus]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> "deep sulcus sign" on supine costophrenic angle
> tracheal deviation not always seen
> dull percussion
> hyperresonance
> [[l/diaphragmatic excursion]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> if apex-cupola < 3cm, ↺[[XR]] 4-6 hrs
> [[chest tube]] especially for ventilator caused
> 
> pigtail catheter
> flutter valve (taped 3/4 sides)

> [!example] Relationships
> [[pleural effusion]] is fluid
> [[hemothorax]] is blood and clots
