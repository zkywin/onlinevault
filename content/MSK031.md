### 01
Recognize the most common manifestations of non-hereditary connective tissue diseases (autoimmune rheumatic diseases) such as systemic lupus erythematosus, rheumatoid arthritis, spondyloarthropathies, large/small/medium/variable-vessel vessel vasculitides, systemic sclerosis, Sjogren’s syndrome, inflammatory myopathies, mixed connective tissue disease, relapsing polychondritis, polymyalgia rheumatica
a. Recognize inflammatory arthritis as a manifestation of these connective tissue
diseases
b. Recognize key components of classification criteria that would lead you to suspect a
rheumatic disease
c. Identify basic laboratory and serologic tests that would lead you to identify organ-
based problems, and diagnose a specific rheumatic disease

### 02
Identify the most common extramusculoskeletal/organ-system based manifestations of autoimmune inflammatory rheumatic diseases: Ocular, Otorhinolaryngologic/Upper Airway,
Dermatologic, Cardiovascular, Pulmonary/Lower Airway, Serositis, Renal, Neurologic, Gastrointestinal (see examples below)

### Examples
Inflammatory eye disease
- uveitis in spondyloarthropathies
- scleritis in RA
- keratoconjunctivitis in Sjogren’s Syndrome
- retinitis in SLE
Otorhinolaryngologic/Upper Airway
- Sinus disease in GPA/WG, tracheal stenosis in GPA/WG
Dermatologic/Skin
- acute cutaneous lupus
- skin thickening/fibrosis with sclerodactyly in scleroderma
- heliotrope and Gottron’s rash in dermatomyositis
Cardiovascular disease
- myocardial infarction in SLE and RA
- pulmonary hypertension in scleroderma
- raynaud’s disease in scleroderma
Pulmonary/Lower Airway
- interstitial lung disease in scleroderma, RA, dermato/polymyositis, AS
Serositis
- pleurisy, pericarditis in SLE and RA
Renal disease
- glomerulonephritis in SLE and vasculitides
- distal RTA in Sjogren’s Syndrome
Neurologic disease
- stroke, myelitis in SLE
- mononeuritis multiplex in vasculitis)
Gastrointestinal
- esophageal dysfunction – dermato/polymyositis, scleroderma
- intestinal disorders in Scleroderma
