
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> GI [[ischemia]]

> [!info] Etiology
> [[prematurity]]
> [[NG tube]] feeds
> [[baby formula]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[HSR 4]] to [[gram negative]]
> [[necrosis]] of [[ileum]] or [[colon]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> sudden bowel distension
> [[bilious emesis]]
> coffee ground emesis
> may have "yellow seedy stools"

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[GI perforation]]
> "pneumatosis intestinalis" (air within bowel wall)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> IV nutrition
> stop [[NG tube]] feeds
> [[Abx]] for anaerobes
> resection
> [[TPN]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[volvulus]] has no large intestine air (air fluid levels)
