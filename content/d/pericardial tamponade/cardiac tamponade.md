
> [!tip] In Short
> [[pericardial effusion]] that impacts heart function

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[RV]] fill failure first → [[pulmonary edema]]
> [[LV]] fails to fill → death

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[hypotension]]
> distant heart sounds
> [[JVP]] distension


> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[electrical aternans]] in [[EKG]]
> [[pulsus paradoxus]]
> equal diastolic pressures on both sides

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[pericardiocentesis]]
