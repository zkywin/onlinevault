[[tract]] (lateral)
> [!tip] Function
> anterior: pressure
> lateral: pain and temperature
> 
> small fiber


> [!info] Location
> ⤩ [[lissauer's tract]] at spinal level
> ⥥ always stays lateral after crossing

> [!warning] Structure
>[[substance P]] → [[C fiber]] [[Aδ fiber]] → [[lissauer's tract]] → [[ventral white commissure]] → [[dorsal horn]] → [[VPL]] → [[postcentral gyrus]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[CN V]] spinal trigeminal ipsilateral for face

> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[lateral medullary syndrome]]
> ⤩ pain/temperature loss
