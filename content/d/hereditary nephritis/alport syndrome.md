[[nephritic syndrome]]
> [!tip] In Short
> [[GBM]] structural failure

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> boys
> COL4A5Δ [[x-linked dominant]]
> COL4A3Δ or COL4A4Δ [[autosomal recessive]] 


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> weak [[collagen 4]] [[collagen 3]] or [[collagen 5]]
> [[HTN]], [[CKD]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[hematuria]]
> [[deafness]]
> ocular defects

> [!question] Objective Findings
> irregular [[GBM]] thickness

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[goodpasture syndrome]] is autoimmune
> [[benign familial hematuria]] is uniform
