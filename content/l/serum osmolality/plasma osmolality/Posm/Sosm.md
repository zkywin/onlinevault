275-295 mOsm/kg
> [!tip] Condensed Description (290 mmol/kg AKA mOsm)
> combined concentration of all solutes in serum
> favors fluid to interstitium (5-10 mmHg)
> regulated by [[ADH]] [[RAAS]] [[thirst]] [[ANP]]

> [!info] Calculation
> estimated by (2x [[Na+]]) + ([[glucose]]/18) + ([[BUN]]/2.8) + [[EtOH]]/4.6
> 
> true value calculated by freezing point depression
> reported mEq milliequivalents of volume may be innacurate due to [[albumin]] and lipids in blood
> [[l/osmolal gap]]

> [!question] Mechanism
> mostly Na+ and Cl-
> [[BUN]] and [[glucose]] both contribute very little

> [!danger] Diseases
> [[hypernatremia]]
> [[hyponatremia]]
> [[HAGMA]] with [[Sosm]] rising from aldehydes

> [!example] Relationships
> technically contributes to [[oncotic]]
> is lower than the osmolality of the [[interstitium]], but offset by [[albumin]]
