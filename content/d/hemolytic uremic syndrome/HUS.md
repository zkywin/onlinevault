[[platelet disorders]] [[MAHA]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[TTP]] of the [[glomerulus]] ([[plt]] only)

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> mostly < 5 years old
> [[EHEC]] [[shiga toxin]] [[gastroenteritis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> endothelial damage
> ↓ [[ADAMTS13]]
> consumption of [[plt]] only
> minimal consumption of [[clotting cascade]]
> [[MAHA]] → [[schistocyte]]
> biggest cause of [[CKD]] in children

> [!danger] Symptoms (atypical HUS = no diarrhea)
> [[BUN]]÷[[creatine]] > 20
> [[anemia]]
> [[CKD]]
> [[thrombocytopenia]]
> [[extravascular hemolysis]]
> [[dysentery]] (lack of diarrhea has worse prognosis)
> [[edema]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[schistocyte]]
> microthrombi with [[fibrinoid necrosis]] of [[glomerulus]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[DIC]] consumes clotting factors too
> [[TTP]] more CNS signs
> [[uremia]]
> [[HSP]] has no thrombocytopenia
