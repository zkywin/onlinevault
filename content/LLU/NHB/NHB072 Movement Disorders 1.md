> [!info] Definitions
> movement disorders: [[basal ganglia]] diseases without UMN or LMN findings. The supply of dopamine to UMN is affected
> hyperkinetic movement disorders: overstimulation of UMN causes involuntary movements
> hypokinetic movement disorders: slowed movements, mostly parkinsonisms

[[parkinsonism]]

Hypokinetic disorders result from not enough dopamine from the [[basal ganglia]], which is classically seen in [[parkinson's]]. However, early falling suggests [[PSP]] or [[MSA]], which also causes autonomic instability. Infarcts of the basal ganglia, tolerance of medication in [[tardive dyskinesia]], or direct drug damage in [[MPTP poisoning]] can also cause hypokinetic disorders. These can be treated by replacing dopamine with [[levodopa]] or agonists [[pramiprexole]] and [[ropinirole]]

[[hyperkinetic movement disorder]]


Meanwhile, hyperkinetic movement disorders are classically seen in famililal [[essential tremor]] and the more common [[dystonia]]. It can also be brought on by copper toxicity in [[wilson's disease]]. Unlike all the mentioned diseases, [[huntington's disease]] has choreiform movements instead of tremors. The manifestations of [[ballismus]] and [[tourette syndrome]] are also quite different from the tremors discussed. #done
