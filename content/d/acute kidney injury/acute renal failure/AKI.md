intrarenal acute kidney injury
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> abrupt ⦻ [[kidney]] 2-7 days
> diffuse, global [[nephron]] damage


> [!info] Etiology
> [[prerenal AKI]] [[renal artery]]
> [[intrarenal AKI]]
> [[postrenal AKI]] [[ureter]]
> [[acyclovir]] crystaline

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> in-hospital mortality rate of 40-50%


> [!danger] Symptoms (Stage 3)
> [[edema]]
> [[hyperkalemia]]
> [[anemia]]
> [[metabolic acidosis]]
> [[hyperkalemia]]
> [[hypocalcemia]]
> [[HTN]]

> [!question] Objective Findings (Stage 2)
> [[azotemia]]
> [[oliguria]]
> [[cast]]
> 
> [[serum creatinine]] > 0.3 mg/dL increase < 48hrs
> or x1.5 above baseline over 7 days
> [[UOP]] < 0.5 mg/kg/hr for 12 hours
> [[BUN Cr ratio]] < 15
> [[US]] to check for [[postrenal AKI]]
> [[CT]] with [[persistent nephrogram]]
> [[FENa]]
> [[l/FEurea]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> ⦻ [[metformin]] and [[NSAID]]

> [!example] Relationships
> subset of [[d/acute kidney disease/AKD]]
> associations with [[d/diabetes mellitus/DM]] and [[obesity]]
> [[neuropathy]] is rare unlike [[CKD]]
