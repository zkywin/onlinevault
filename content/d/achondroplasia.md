[[dwarfism]]
[[autosomal dominant]] chromosome 4 or [[de novo]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> bones don't lengthen

> [!info] Etiology
> FGFR3[[Δ]] activating mutation HET
> high paternal age

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> inhibits growth of [[cartilage]]
>  inhibits [[endochondral ossification]]
> inheritance risk 50%
> narrowed foramen magnum → [[hypotonia]], death

> [!danger] Symptoms
> short extremities
> normal head and chest
> frontal bossing (large forehead)
> trident hands (spock hands)

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> hypochondroplasia less severe form
> thanatophoric dwarfism more severe form
