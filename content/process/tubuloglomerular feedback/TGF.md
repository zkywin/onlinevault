tubuloglomerular feedback
> [!tip] Function
> ↑[[GFR]] → ↑[[Na+]] → [[JG cell]] → ↑[[renin]]
> ↓[[afferent flow]] → ↓[[GFR]]

> [!info] Mechanism
> high [[GFR]] and [[NFP]] causes high Na+
> increases [[distal tubule]] Na+ reabsorption
> causes [[distal tubule]] to release [[adenosine]]
> [[A1 receptor]] increases Ca++
> [[A1 receptor]] causes [[mesangium]] contraction
> [[A1 receptor]] causes decreased [[JG cell]] [[renin]] secretion

> [!warning] Structure

> [!example] Relationships
> fluid intake causes lowers [[TGF]] sensitivity, increasing excretion, low [[AT2]]
> lower fluid intake increases [[TGF]] sensitivity, decreasing excretion, high [[AT2]]
> [[furosemide]] inhibits [[TGF]]
> [[CTGF]]
> aging increases [[TGF]]
> [[GT balance]] is a failsafe

> [!danger] Pathologies
