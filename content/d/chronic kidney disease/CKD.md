chronic kidney disease, chronic renal insufficiency
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[GFR]] < 60 mL/min/kg for over 3 months
> very high [[single nephron GFR]]
> burns through [[nephron]]s like little birthday candles in a chain reaction

> [!info] Etiology
> [[anesthetics]]
> [[diabetic nephropathy]]
> [[nephrotic syndrome]] [[nephritic syndrome]]
> [[hypertensive nephrosclerosis]]
> caused by [[HUS]] in children

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> rise in [[single nephron GFR]] from [[PGE2]] and [[AT2]]
>  Stage 5 chronic kidney failure [[GFR]] (15-90) Stage 1
>  [[FSGS]] is the histological manifestation (I think)
>  low [[vD]] decreases [[Ca2+]] causing increased [[PTH]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[uremia]] 
> [[nephrotic syndrome]]
> [[premature vascular disease]]
> [[azotemia]]
> [[edema]]
> [[vomiting]]
> [[anemia]] from low [[EPO]] and high [[hepcidin]]
> 
> [[hypercoagulability]] [[neuropathy]] [[malnutrition]] [[hypogonadism]] [[hyperparathyroidism]] [[metabolic acidosis]] [[s/itching/pruritis]] [[pulmonary hypertension]]
> yellow skin from [[urobilin]] 

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[Cr]] rises inverse to [[GFR]] fall
> [[PO4-]], [[K+]], [[Na+]], [[free water]] only rise at severe disease ([[isosthenuria]])
> degree of [[proteinuria]] predicts prognosis
> low [[Hct]]
> 
> [[kidney biopsy]]
> [[US]] shrunken and granular kidneys, brighter than normal
> [[CT]] atrophy

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[kidney transplant]] and [[HD]] for [[ESRD]] patients
> [[ACEi]] and [[ARB]] relax [[glomerulus]]
> [[SGLT2]] inhibitors increase tubular [[Na+]] relax [[glomerulus]]
> higher [[insulin]] requirement at lower stage, lower at high stage
> [[vD]] to prevent 2°  [[hyperparathyroidism]]

> [!example] Relationships
>[[ATN]] and [[RPGN]] causes [[AKI]]
> [[hyponatremia]] [[hypernatremia]] happen easily due to poor tubular control
> [[mesoamerican nephropathy]]
