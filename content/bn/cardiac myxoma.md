[[benign neoplasm]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> undifferentiated [[mesenchyme]] 

> [!info] Etiology
> "induction of embryonic genes"
> most common cardiac tumor
> most common adult tumor

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> 75% at [[LA]]
> pedunculated mass in the heart
> [[mitral valve]] block
> [[IL-6]] secretion → weight loss, fevers, chills

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[fever]] [[chills]] 
> [[syncope]] 
> [[S3]] [[S4]]
> "plop sound" on mid-diastolic

> [!question] Objective Findings
> mucoid gelatinous [[thrombosis]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
