"rusty tin man"
[[fungi]] filamentous
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> animals, fomites (wrestling mat), people
> trichophyton microsporum epidermophyton
> if recurrent, reservoir somewhere on body

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[cellulitis]]
> [[immunocompromised]] → widespread infection
> 
> tinea capitis: (african american kids) carrion
> tinea barbae: beard
> tinea favosa: scalp [[hair loss]]
> tinea pedis: athlete's foot
> tinea unguium: toenails/fingernails

> [!danger] Symptoms
> honeycomb [[crust]]
> high flaking

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[KOH]]
> [[sabouraud's agar]] aerial hyphae
> [[wood's lamp test]] for capitis microsporum

> [!abstract] Treatment
> check folds for reservoirs if recurrent
> [[griseofulvin]] [[terbinafine]] oral
> [[clotrimazole]] [[miconazole]] topical

> [!example] Relationships
> consider [[granuloma annulare]]
