> encephalitis: direct brain infection
> meningitis: inflammation of brain membrane. Interestingly, CSF findings are pretty analagous for these two

Most [[viral encephalitis]] is caused by arboviruses (insect-related). In the USA, aedes mosquitos bear [[la crosse virus]] and culex mosquitos carry [[st louis encephalitis virus]]. [[equine encephalitis virus]] has a bird circulation as well as mosquitos. In Colorado and Canda, ticks are of concern with [[coltivirus]]. Just like with meningitis, [[coxsackie B virus]] [[echovirus]] are back, but [[poliovirus]] is also here.[[HSV-2]] and [[VZV]] are mostly in infants and [[HSV-1]] in adults. In the immunocompromised, [[PML]] springs up causes cell proliferation. Instead of [[mumps]] in meningitis, it is [[measles]] that affects the brain.

Two types of [[parasitic encephalitis]] trophozoites can be found, either [[brain-eating amoeba]] which travels olfactory nerves, or [[acanthamoeba]] which is dessication resistant. [[african sleeping sickness]] flagellates are transmitted by Tsetse flies.

[[fungal encephalitis]] takes hold in cancer and diabetic patients that eat away at the nose, sinuses, and brain. They can even affect the lungs. 

[[creutzfeld-jacob disease]] is a [[prions]] disease that causes rapid destruction of brain tissue. #done
