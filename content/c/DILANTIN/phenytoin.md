Dilantin #conventionalAED  [[anticonvulsants]]
> [!info] Mechanism
> ⊣ [[NaV]] ≈ [[carbamazepine]]

> [!danger] Uses
> [[focal status epilepticus]]
> [[focal aware]] [[focal impaired]] [[focal to general]]

> [!abstract] Formulations
> intravenous variant 3 times faster

> [!warning] Metabolism
> initially 1st order metabolism (1/x) 
> saturation of enzymes changes to 0 order (linear)
> levels can be checked at steady state in 5 days
> bound to albumin, #halflife 24 hours
> liver [[CYP450]] inducer
> [[UGT glucuronidation]]

> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> cerebellar, vestibular toxicity → [[diplopia]] [[nystagmus]]
> [[gingival hyperplasia]]
> [[hirsuitism]]
> [[SJS]]
> [[DRESS]]
> [[arrhythmia]]
> [[vB9 deficiency]]
> [[fetal hydantoin syndrome]]

> [!example] Relationships
> decreases concentraions of [[warfarin]] and oral contraceptives
> [[vB9]] can prevent the gingival hyperplasia
