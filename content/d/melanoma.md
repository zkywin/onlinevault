[[cancer]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[mole]] [[melanocyte]] cancer

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> tanning beds [[albinism]]
> mutation CDKN2A chromosome 9 (p16 tumor suppressor)
> [[mole]] [[FAMM syndrome]]
> not all dysplastic nevus will become melanoma

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> rarely grow in eyes, [[mets]] [[brain]] and [[lung]]
> BRAF, NRAS, C-KIT mutations
> radial growth phase, vertical growth phase
> 
> #1 [[superficial melanoma]]
> [[lentigo maligna]]
> [[nodular melanoma]]
> [[acral lentiginous]]



> [!danger] Symptoms
> full thickness biopsy (not shave)
> 
> 70% superficial spreading:  flat, variable pigmentation
> 20% nodular: typically older patients
> 15% lentigo: inkstain face, head neck older patients
> 5% acral lentiginous: palms/soles dark skin, asian/african american


> [!question] Objective Findings
> large nuclei, eosinophilic nucleoi
> abundant gray cytoplasm
> if not melanotic [[S100]] SOX-10
> HMB-45 Ag [[tyrosinase]]
> 
> [[breslow depth]]/clark's levels

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> differential benign spitz nevus in kids
> nodular melanoma
> [[SCC]] and [[melanoma]] only 20% of deaths
