> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ⦻ [[arachnoid granulations]] to absorb [[CSF]]

> [!info] Etiology

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> episodically high  [[CSF]] pressure
> [[communicating hydrocephalus]]
> ventricular enlargement affects [[corona radiata]]
> reversible [[dementia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> "wet, wobbly, wacky"
> gait [[apraxic gait]] "magnetic gait"
> subcortical [[dementia]]
> [[urgency]]
> some [[parkinsonism]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[LP]] improves symptoms

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[VP shunt]]

> [!example] Relationships
> "over diagnosed", often actually [[alzheimer's]]
> [[MDD]] no gait instability
> wide based gait opposite of [[parkinson's]]
