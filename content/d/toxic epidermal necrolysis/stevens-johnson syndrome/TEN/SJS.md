[[EMERGENT]]
"red TENgu mask falling off"
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> TEN if >30% body surface area
> SJS-TEN 10-30% body surface area
> SJS if <10% body surface area

> [!info] Etiology
> [[HLA]]B5801 for [[allopurinol]] asian/african
> B1502 [[carbamazepine]] southeast asian
> B5701 abacavir
> [[aminopenicillins]]
> [[cephalosporins]]
> [[macrolides]]
> [[anticonvulsants]]
> [[phenytoin]]
> [[mood stabilizers]]
> [[ART]]
> [[warfarin]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> 7-10 days after drug initiation
> [[FAS ligand]] [[necrosis]] [[Tc cell]]
> [[keratinocyte]] [[granzyme]] death
> [[erythema multiforme]] + [[fever]] + oral [[mucosa]]
> [[hypovolemic shock]] [[sepsis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> flu-like symptoms
> painful rash that spreads and blisters
> top layer of affected skin dies and sheds
> inability to urinate
> [[nikolsky sign]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[skin biopsy]] confirming [[necrosis]]
> [[SCORTEN]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> transfer to burn unit

> [!example] Relationships
> [[erythema multiforme]] is on the spectrum
> resembles large [[burn]]
> supportive care
> consult derm!
