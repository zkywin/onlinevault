> [!tip] Condensed Description
> poor sleep → disordered thinking

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> ∝ ICU and nursing homes
> post-[[ischemic stroke]], bacteremia, cardiac, orthopedic, cancer causes
> increased [[BBB]] permeability, excessive [[corticosteroids]], oxidative stress, inflammatory cytokines
> [[wernicke's encephalopathy]] 
> anticholinergic drugs
> poor sleep with less stage 3 and 4 non-REM sleep
> waking from [[anesthetics]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> systemic disturbances → disrupted neuronal activity

> [!danger] Symptoms
> DSM5: disturbance in attention and cognition over a short period of time in context of medical condition other than neurocognitive
> sleep-wake disturbances
> emotional disturbances
> hallucination
> Can involve hyperactivity, hypoactivity, or a mix of both

> [!question] Objective Findings
> Delirium Rating Scale–Revised-98
> [[EEG]] alpha waves lower frequency

> [!abstract] Treatment
> prevent delirium using certain sedatives [[dexmedetomidine]]
> [[typical antipsychotics]] [[haloperidol]]
> [[hydromorphone]] for pain
> [[benzos]] for CNS depressant withdrawal

> [!example] Relationships
> [[dementia]] is much slower, and has a normal, alert, EEG
>  however [[dementia]] and [[delirium]] can strongly influence each other
>  avoid [[benzos]] in most cases
