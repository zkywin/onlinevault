[[autosomal recessive]] [[familial dyslipidemia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> absolutely no fat transport

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> lack of MTP[[Δ]] to transfer B lipoproteins

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> deficiency of [[apoB48]] [[apoB100]]
> no [[chylomicron]] [[VLDL]]
> [[fat malabsorption]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[failure to thrive]]
> [[vE deficiency]] [[ataxia]]
> [[vA deficiency]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[lipids]] accumulation in [[small intestine]]
> low [[VLDL]] [[IDL]] [[LDL]]
> [[acanthocyte]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> restrict dietary [[fatty acid]]
> oral supplement [[vE]] and [[e/retinol/vA]]
