
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> supine frog legs position (pillow under leg)
> stand on right side of patient
> linear probe → vascular lower extremity preset
> depth 4 cm

> [!question] Mechanism
> superficial [[femoral vein]] is a DEEP vein
> 3-point system is taught
> compression till artery collapses
> DVT = artery compresses before vein

> [!danger] Diseases

> [!example] Relationships
> color doppler → augmentation by squeezing calf
> lymph nodes well circumscribed, not vein like
> baker's cyst, no color flow
> pseudoaneurysm, yin yang sign
> groin hematoma, scattered areas of clotting
