[[gram negative]] [[spirochete]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> ixodes deer [[tick]] nymphs
> northeast, mid-atlantic, upper midwest US

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> stage 1
> stage 2 [[myalgia]] [[arthralgia]]
> stage 3 chronic [[arthralgia]]
> 
> OspA shields from [[Ab]]
> OspC needed for infection
> VlsE has antigenic variation
> may infect CNS, tendons, etc.

> [!danger] Symptoms
> bulls-eye rash, myalgias
> knee mono-[[arthralgia]], inflammation (painless)
> [[3° AV block]]
> [[myocarditis]] 
> [[seronegative arthropathy]] [[bell's palsy]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[synovial fluid]] [[WBC]] 10-25k
> no LPS
> biopsy of edge of lesion
> [[ELISA]],  [[western blot]] for confirmation

> [!abstract] Treatment
> if tick latched > 36 hrs
> start prophylaxis within 72 hrs of tick removal
> 
> child < 8 years [[doxycycline]]
> pregnant [[amoxicillin]] or [[cefuroxime]]

> [!example] Relationships
> may coinfect with [[babesia microti]]
> [[gonorrhoeae]] [[WBC]] > 25k
