[[small vessel vasculitis]] [[nephritic syndrome]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> systemic [[IgA]] disease
> autoimmune [[GBM]] from [[GdIgA1]] [[immune complex]]

> [!info] Etiology
> mostly in children
> 50% [[URI]] → ↑[[IgA]] in days

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑[[GdIgA1]] + [[IgA]] [[IgG]] 
> [[immune complex]] → [[mesangium]]
> [[MBL pathway]] [[alternative pathway]]
> moderate [[complement]] activation
> recurrence 30%

> [!danger] Symptoms
> abdominal pain 
> [[intussusception]] ileo-ileal
> palpable [[purpura]]
> scrotal pain in 30% boys
> [[arthralgia]]
> [[hematuria]]
> [[melena]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[UA]] + [[CBC]]
> biopsy done
> normal [[plt]]
> [[IgA]] granular [[IF]] in [[mesangium]]
> focal segmental [[nephritic syndrome]]
> [[bowman's crescent]]
> [[RBC cast]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[aspirin]], regular monitoring
> normally resolves spontaneously (unless [[AKI]] bad)
> surgical intervention for [[intussusception]] (ileoileal)

> [!example] Relationships
> [[ITP]] [[HUS]] has thrombocytopenia
> [[PSGN]] occurs weeks post-infection
> ∝ [[celiac sprue]]
> [[buerger disease]] smoking related
> [[cryoglobulinemia]] no abdominal pain
