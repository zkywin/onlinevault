[[alpha synuclein]] [[dementia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> fluctuating [[dementia]] + [[hallucinations]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[alpha synuclein]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[dementia]]
> [[parkinsonism]] within 1 year of [[dementia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> fluctuating cognition
> visual [[hallucinations]]
> [[REM sleep behavior disorder]]
> [[parkinsonism]]
> memory loss
> [[capgras delusion]]
> significant overlap with [[alzheimer's]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> neuronal loss → pallor of [[substantia nigra]] [[locus coeruleus]] 
> [[alpha synuclein]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[donepezil]]
> atypical neuroleptics [[quetiapine]]
> [[rivastigmine]]
> ⚠ [[typical antipsychotics]] worsen parkinsonisms

> [!example] Relationships
> [[alzheimer's]], [[non-pick's frontotemporal lobar degeneration]] and [[frontotemporal dementia]] do not have parkinsonism
> [[parkinson's]], [[MSA]] are other [[alpha synuclein]] diseases but do not get dementia until much later
