[[intraventricular hemorrhage]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology
> infants [[prematurity]]
> disappears at 28 weeks

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> periventricular matrix blood supply is thin-walled
> grade 1-4 of scaling severity of bleed
> [[hydrocephalus]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> small ones have no issue
> large bleeds cause [[cerebral palsy]]
> [[hypotension]] [[coma]] [[seizure]]
> [[hypotonia]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> screen infants <32 weeks on [[US]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[vK deficiency]] more likely if term infant
