[[gram negative]] [[diplococci]] [[encapsulated]]
> [!tip] In Short
> [[bacterial meningitis]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> respiratory spread (dormitories, military recruits)
> [[C5-C9 deficiency]]
> <5yrs, 2/3 caused by serogroup B
> W and C highest mortality

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[LOS]] endotoxin
> [[IgA protease]]
> [[pilli]]
> [[encapsulated]]
> [[DIC]] [[waterhouse friderichsen syndrome]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[DIC]] purpura fulminans
> [[bacterial meningitis]] in college-age adults

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[chocolate agar]] for blood/CSF samples
> [[thayer martin agar]] for mucosal samples
> oxidase

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[meningococcal vaccine]]
> PPX [[ciprofloxacin]] or [[rifampin]] or [[ceftriaxone]] to all family members/close contacts if [[bacterial meningitis]]


> [!example] Relationships
> [[neisseria vaccine]]
> [[gonorrhoeae]] does not ferment maltose
> [[d/complement deficiency]] can make infections more common
> [[strep pneumoniae]] is more triangular shaped
> [[H flu]] affects infants
> [[listeria]] has cold growth
