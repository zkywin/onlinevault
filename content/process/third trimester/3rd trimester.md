[[1st trimester]] [[2nd trimester]]
> [!tip] In Short
> 28-birth wks (care Q2w till 36w, then Q1w)
> term = 36-40wks

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[labor]] [[delivery]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[prematurity]]
> [[gestational diabetes]]
> [[polymorphic eruption of pregnancy]]
> [[gestational thrombocytopenia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[bloody show]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[NST]] >32 wks
> [[GBS]] test 36-38wks
> [[STD NAAT]] repeat if high risk

> [!abstract] Treatment
> weekly follow if high risk
> ↑caloric intake 450 kcal/day
> 
> [[Tdap]] [[RhIG]] 
> [[tocolytics]] if < 34 weeks
> [[acyclovir]] if history of [[HSV-1]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[postterm pregnancy]] > 42
> [[third trimester bleeding]]
