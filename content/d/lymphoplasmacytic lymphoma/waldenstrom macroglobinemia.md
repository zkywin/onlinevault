[[non-hodgkin lymphoma]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[IgM]] clonal production

> [!info] Detailed Description
> blood becomes more viscous
> bleeding from poor [[platelet aggregation]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> visual and neurologic
> [[neuropathy]]
> [[raynaud phenomenon]]
> urticaria
> headache
> coma
> [[EMERGENT]] [[blindness]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[bone marrow biopsy]] with >10% clonal [[B cell]]
> [[SPEP]] clonal M spike is IgM specifically
> [[gamma gap]] > 4

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[plasmapheresis]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[multiple myeloma]] has bone lesions [[IgG]] and [[IgA]]
> [[MGUS]] is not IgM
> [[polycythemia vera]] also thickens blood
