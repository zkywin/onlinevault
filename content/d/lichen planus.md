
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> autoimmunity to [[stratum basale]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> ∝ [[HCV]] chronic
> drug induced BATH [[ACEi]], [[thiazide]], [[β1 blockers]], [[hydroxychloroquine]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> wrist, elbow, ankle, oral mucosa, genitalia
> drug-induced more diffuse form
> oral form relapses for years


> [!danger] Symptoms
> pruritic, polygonal, planar, purple, [[papule]]
> [[dyspareunia]]
> profuse discharge

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[whickam striae]]
> "sawtooth" inflammation of [[dermis]] [[epidermis]] junction
> basal vacuolization

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]]
> [[UV phototherapy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[psoriasis]] is scale
> [[pemphigus vulgaris]] is desmosome
> [[epidermolysis bullosa]] is basement membrane
