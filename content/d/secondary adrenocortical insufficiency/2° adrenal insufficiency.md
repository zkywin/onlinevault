[[hypoadrenalism]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> low [[ACTH]] from [[pituitary]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> suddenly stopped [[corticosteroids]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> chronic use suppresses [[pituitary]]
> sudden removal causes low [[cortisol]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> no skin findings
> no hyperkalemia

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> prevent by weaning off steroids

> [!example] Relationships
> "stress-dose steroids" even higher corticosteroids given when anticipating infection, trauma, surgery
