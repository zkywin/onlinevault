> [!tip] Condensed Description
> literally "on the brain"
> below [[arachnoid mater]]
> above [[pia mater]]

> [!info] Etiology
> [[berry aneurysm]]
> especially [[ACA]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> bleed into subarachnoid space from
> lasting cranial neuropathy
> [[communicating hydrocephalus]]
> [[lucid interval]]
> 死 rebleeding in 24 hrs (30%)
> 死 [[vasospasm]] 3-10 days later

> [!danger] Symptoms
> severe "thunderclap" [[headache]]
> [[ICP]]
> [[photophobia]]
> [[bradzinski sign]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> subhyaloid retinal hemorrhage
> [[xanthochromia]]
> blood on bottom of brain
> [[CT]] without contrast (contast can obscure)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> blood pressure control
> [[c/nimodipine]]
> neurosurgery to clip the aneurysm

> [!example] Relationships
> may be caused by rupture of [[berry aneurysm]] or [[intraparenchymal hemorrhage]]
