> [!info] Definitions
> equilibrium = p and q don't change between generations
> p = dominant allele proportion
> 2pq = heterozygote proportion
> q = recessive allele proportion

> [!tip] rare diseases
> rare diseases [[autosomal recessive]]  p ~ 1

> [!tip] x linked recessive
>  treat male and female as 2 separate species
>  q = diseased male
>  p = healthy male
>  2pq = carrier female
>  q^2 = diseased female

5 assumptions:
1. no mutations
2. no natural selection pressures
3. completely random mating
4. no net migration
5. large population
