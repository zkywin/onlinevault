[[hyperbilirubinemia]]
> [!tip] In Short
> mechanically weak hepatocytes
> rotor = failed storage
> dubin johnson = failed secretion

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> MRP[[Δ]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> excretion by the hepatocytes
> inability to get [[direct bilirubin]] into bile

> [!danger] Symptoms
> no [[s/itching/pruritis]]
> asymptomatic

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[direct bilirubin]]
> black liver in dubin johnson not rotor's

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[gilbert's syndrome]] but with [[direct bilirubin]]
