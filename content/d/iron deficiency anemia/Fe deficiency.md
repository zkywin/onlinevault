[[normocytic anemia]] -> [[microcytic anemia]]
> [!tip] In Short
> low [[Fe]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[prematurity]]
> too much raw milk > 24oz/day
> 
> [[colon cancer]] occult bleed
> [[PPI]] [[vC deficiency]] [[duodenum]] problems
>  [[PUD]] 
> [[pregnancy]] [[menorrhagia]]
> [[ancylostoma zoonotic]] [[ancylostoma duodenale]]
> [[plummer-vinson]]
> [[gastrectomy]] → low acid → ↑ Fe2+ → ↓ absorption

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> iron loss
> iron malnutrition
> variable [[Hb]] available to developing [[RBC]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[pica disorder]]
> [[cheilosis]] [[glossitis]]
> [[SIGN koilonychia spoon nails.png]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> screening at 1 yr old
> ↓ [[ferritin]], [[Fe]], 
> ↑ [[transferrin]] ↓ [[transferrin sat]]
> ↑ [[protoporphyrin]]
> ↑ [[RDW]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> iron fortified formula
> breast milk + iron at 4 mo
> solid foods starting at 6 mo
> [[Fe]] pill + [[stool softener]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[α-thalassemia]] [[β-thalassemia]] → ↓[[RDW]]
> [[anemia of chronic disease]]
