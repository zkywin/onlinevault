
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> occluded branch of [[internal carotid]]

> [!info] Etiology
> [[atherosclerosis]]
> [[cardiac myxoma]]
> [[patent foramen ovale]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> loss of blood flow to [[CN II]] head
> edema of [[ganglion cell]] layer

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[macular cherry red spot]] (retinal whitening)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> occular massage + [[carbogen]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[amaurosis fugax]] is a temporary version
> [[CRVO]]
