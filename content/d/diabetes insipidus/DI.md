water wasting
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ⦻ [[ADH]] → [[H2O]] loss

> [!info] Etiology
> [[hypokalemia]] can interfere with [[ADH]] 
> [[central DI]]
> [[nephrogenic DI]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[ADH]] is much lower in [[DI]] than [[primary polydipsia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[polydipsia]] compensatory
> [[hypernatremia]] results if they don't keep drinking water
> [[polyuria]]


> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[Uosm]] low
> [[Uspg]] < 1.006

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[ADH]]

> [!example] Relationships
> opposite of [[SIADH]]
> [[primary polydipsia]] responsive to water restriction
> worsened in pregnancy as [[placenta]] breaks [[ADH]]
