> ⦻ [[caudate]] [[GABA]] neurons
> ↑ [[DA]]

> [!info] Etiology
> juvenile onset in girls
> inherited from their fathers
> 
> CAG [[trinucleotide repeat]] in HD gene
> [[histone acetylation]] fails

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↑ [[DA]]
> ↓ [[GABA]] [[ACh]] [[substance P]]
> neurotoxic HTT protein
> decreased neurotrophic factor transcription

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[chorea]]
> [[athetosis]]
> [[dystonia]]
> delayed [[frontal eye field]]
> [[dementia]]
> personality disorder

> [!question] Objective findings
> MRI [[l/caudate nucleus atrophy]] putamen as well
> gliosis and loss of medium-sized spiny neurons
> CAG repeats on IHC

> [[tetrabenazine]] [[reserpine]] ⊣ [[MAOb]]
> [[haloperidol]]
