[[apnea]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> upper airway obstruction during [[process/sleep]]

> [!info] Etiology
> [[edema]] from [[HTN]]
> [[obesity]], [[micrognathia]], pharyngeal tissue
> tonsil enlargement, adenoids, uvula
> [[OHS]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> worse in [[REM sleep]]
> ↓[[O2]] → ↑ [[NE]] → ↑[[RAAS]] → [[2° HTN]]
> [[pulmonary hypertension]]
> [[2° HTN]]
> [[afib]] [[aflutter]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> loud snoring + nighttime apnea + daytime hypersomnolence + morning headaches
> [[2° enuresis]]
> [[parasomnia]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[friedman scale]]
> [[polysomnography]]
> 5x/hr cessation of breathing > 10 seconds
> [[polycythemia]]
> [[STOPBANG]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[CPAP]]
> AHI < 30: mandibular advancement device,  tongue retaining devices
> stimulation of [[CN XII]]
> AHI > 30 [[septoplasty]] [[turbinate reduction]] [[tonsillectomy]] [[adenoidectomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[d/arrhythmia]], [[HTN]] [[cor pulmonale]] [[CHF]] [[sudden cardiac death]]
> [[choanal atresia]] is present at birth
