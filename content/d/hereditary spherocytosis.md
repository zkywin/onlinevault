[[extravascular hemolysis]] [[autosomal dominant]]
> [!tip] In Short
> round but same size RBC

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> familial [[cytoskeleton]] abnormality
> [[Δ]] spectrin, ankyrin, band 3, or protein 4.2
> preserved [[RBC]] function

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> rigid cell membranes
> by [[poiseuille's law]] increases flow resistance
> [[extravascular hemolysis]]
> size of [[RBC]] drop over time


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[splenomegaly]]
> pigmented [[cholelithiasis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> eosin 5 maleimide binding test
> glycerol osmotic fragility test
> broad [[RDW]]
> very concentrated RBC [[MCHC]]
> [[MCV]] normal (balanced by high [[reticulocyte]])

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[splenectomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[warm AIHA]]
> asymptomatic until [[parvovirus]] infection
> [[gilbert's syndrome]] also stress-induced jaundice
