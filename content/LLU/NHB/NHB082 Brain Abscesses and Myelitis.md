> [!info] Definitions
> brain abscess = focal brain parenchymal infection in its own bubble
> until the abscess ruptures, CSF shows increased WBC, elevated protein, and NORMAL glucose

[[d/acute brain abscess]] and [[d/subdural empyema]] come from the same culprits, but one is in a bubble in the brain and the other is on the side. A different subset of organisms causes [[chronic brain abscess]]. 

[[pork tapeworm]] encysts more in the brain while [[toxoplasmosis]] is more generalized to other nervous tissues.

[[myelitis]] is a serious spinal cord infection with anterior horn destruction most related to [[poliovirus]] and [[west nile virus]] but has a variety of other causes too. It is usually asymmetric but [[transverse myelitis]] can become worse.

Rusty nails will result in infection from [[tetanus]] and cause [[tetany]]. [[rabies]] will come from bites. #done
