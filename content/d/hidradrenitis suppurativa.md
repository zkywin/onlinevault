[[follicular occlusion tetrad]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> inflammation of [[apocrine gland]] [[sweat gland]]
> "folliculopilosebaceous units"

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[obesity]]
> [[smoking]]
> [[PCOS]] [[crohn's]]
> not caused by poor hygeine

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> occlusion of [[hair]] follicle
> rupture into the [[dermis]]
> inflammation of dermis
> [[abscess]] and [[fibrosis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> lesions at thigh, buttocks, axilla
> anogenital in men

> [!question] Objective Findings
> hurley staging

> [!abstract] Treatment
> weight reduction, smoking cessation
> [[Abx]] [[retinoids]]
> intra-lesional [[corticosteroids]]
> [[adalimumab]] [[infliximab]] [[secukinumab]]
> [[skin laser]]
> excision of [[apocrine gland]] tissue

> [!example] Relationships
> [[acne vulgaris]] but it bursts internally
> [[boil]] is still more contained than this
