
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> fluid or electrolyte imbalance upon sudden re-feeding

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> during starvation, intracellular compartment pushes out electrolytes
> during re-feeding, insulin gets cells to rapidly pull electrolytes out of the serum


> [!danger] Symptoms
> edema
> cardiac arrhythmia
> muscle weakness
> [[hyporeflexia]]
> [[rhabdomyolysis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↓phosphorous, potassium, magnesium, glucose

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[anorexia nervosa]]

suddenly resuming diet
massive insulin release to digest food pulls serum phosphate, potassium, and magnesium out
sudden lowering of electrolytes can cause [[d/seizure]], [[d/arrhythmia]], respiratory disteress, confusion
