[[autosomal recessive]] 
> [!tip] In Short
> [[Phe Tyr metabolism]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[phenylalanine hydroxylase]] deficiency
> [[BH4 deficiency]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[Tyr]] deficiency
> [[Phe]] collection
> formation of toxic phenylketones
> phenylacetate, phenyllactate and phenylpyruvate
> maternal PKU affects healthy babies

> [!danger] Symptoms
>  microcephaly [[intellectual disability]]
> [[seizure]] 
> [[eczema]]
> aromatic "mouse-like" body odor
> light skin

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[newborn screen]]
> urine [[Phe]] screened 2-3 days after birth
> light pigmentation of [[substantia nigra]], [[locus coeruleus]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> remove dietary [[Phe]]
> increase dietary [[Tyr]]
> avoid [[aspartame]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[BH4 deficiency]] is dysfunction of enzyme instead of deficiency
> [[classic galactosemia]] right after lactation
