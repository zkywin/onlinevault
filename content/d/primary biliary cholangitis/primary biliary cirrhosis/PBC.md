[[intrahepatic cholestasis]]
> [!tip] In Short
> more insidious (small ducts)
> intrahepatic only

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> middle aged women
> ∝ [[sjogren syndrome]]
> [[systemic scleroderma]]
> [[rheumatoid arthritis]]
> [[hashimoto thyroiditis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[Tc cell]] destroy intralobular bile duct
> [[granuloma]]
> [[bile acid]] in the skin
> [[hypercholesterolemia]]
> risk [[ascending cholangitis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[s/itching/pruritis]]!!!
> [[fatigue]]
> [[xanthelasma]]
> [[hepatomegaly]]
> [[hyperpigmentation]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑↑↑[[alk phos]] > 1.5x
> [[anti-mitochondrion]] 1:40 or higher
> normal [[biliary tree]]
> [[ERCP]] no longer used

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[ursodeoxycholic acid]]
> [[cholestyramine]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[PSC]] involves biliary tree too
> ∝ [[osteoporosis]] possibly via [[vD]]

> [!quote] Experience
> SAC SBC: post cholecystectomy with remaining high [[alk phos]] for years
