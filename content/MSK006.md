### 11 Corticosteroids
%%Describe the mechanism of action, potency and side effects of topical corticosteroids and the importance of their use in the treatment of dermatologic disease%%
[[corticosteroids]]
anti-inflammatory via phospholipase A2 on DNA transcription factors
anti-mitotic to lower some lichenification
immunosuppressive to inhibit proliferation of immune cells
potency depends on structure and vehicle (measured by ability to vasoconstrict skin)

may worsen [[fungi]]
delay wound healing
[[glaucoma]] [[cataracts]] if used around face
[[hypercortisolism]]
