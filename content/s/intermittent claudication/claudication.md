[[PVD]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> reproducible muscle pain on exercise relieved by brief rest

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> by exertion
> [[atherosclerosis]] of large [[femoral artery]] branch
> [[d/leriche syndrome/aortoiliac occlusion]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> temporary flow drop to the limbs

> [!danger] Symptoms
> lower extremity pain
> calf from femoropopliteal distribution

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[cilostazol]] [[dipyridamole]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[raynaud phenomenon]] is hot/cold related
> [[d/neurogenic claudication]]
