
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> narrowing of [[aorta]] past great vessels

> [[turner syndrome]]
> infantile (proximal to [[PDA]])
> adult [[bicuspid aortic valve]] (distal to [[PDA]])

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[cyanosis]] low extremity 
> [[HTN]] of extremities
> ⦻ [[LV]] → [[aorta]]
> [[RV]] → [[PA]] → [[PDA]] → [[aorta]]
> [[PDA]] closure → ↑[[LV]][[EDP]] → [[left heart failure]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[MSM]] heard over the back

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[ankle brachial index]]
> doppler [[echo]]
> [[notched ribs]] from engorged intercostal arteries

> [!abstract] Treatment
> if preductal: [[PGE2]] [[PGI2]] → maintain [[PDA]]
> [[maintained PDA]]
