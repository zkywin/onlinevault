[[fungi]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> oil lover "cradle cap"

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> hot and humid conditions
> [[skin commensals]]
> fungemia in premature infants with central catheter
> severe in [[HIV]], [[parkinson's]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[pityriasis rosea]]
> [[fungal skin infection]] hypopigmented lesions
> dandruff is [[HSR 1]]
> overactive [[sebaceous gland]]
> flaking rash of scalp/eyebrows

> [!danger] Symptoms
> "cradle cap" in infants
> dandruff in adults
> occipital [[lymphadenopathy]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> visible hyphae = infection
> growth on agar with olive oil
> [[sebaceous gland]] not inflamed
> [[KOH]] "spaghetti and meatballs" appearance

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[ketoconazole]]
> [[selenium]] selcin blue plus
> topical antifungal

> [!example] Relationships
> [[seborrheic keratosis]]
> [[parkinson's]]
