[[ACUTE]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> excessive stretch/contraction

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> damage to [[skeletal muscle]] or [[tendon]]
> whiplash
> slowing from a sprint
> 

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> at [[trapezius]] causes [[scapular dyskinesis]]
> [[herniated disc]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> muscle spasms
> [[reduced aROM]]
> normal movement

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> self-resolving
> [[anticonvulsants]]
> [[lidocaine]] patch
> [[NSAID]]
> [[antidepressants]]
> [[opioid]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[sprain]] more severe
> [[herniated disc]]
> [[radiculopathy]]
