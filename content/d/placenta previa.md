"placenta before baby"
> [!tip] In Short
> placenta covers cervical os

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> other parts of uterus not normal
> [[c-section]]
> 
> M multiparity
> M multiple gestations
> A advanced maternal age
> P previous placenta previa
> P prior uterine surgery


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> baby blocked from leaving [[uterus]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> painless bleeding in pregnancy
> normal heart tracing

> [!question] Objective Findings
> found on [[US]]
> only look, don't touch

> [!abstract] Treatment
> majority can self-resolve by [[3rd trimester]]
> deliver the baby via [[c-section]]
> avoid being [[sexually active]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[placental abruption]] has a firm uterus
> [[vasa previa]] less of a big deal
