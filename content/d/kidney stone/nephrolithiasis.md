> body acidity risks [[calcium stones]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[uric acid stone]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[renal colic pain]]
> unilateral flank tenderness
> no flank pain in kids

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[CT]] without contrast
> [[US]] only for very large stones or [[pregnancy]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> hydration if < 4mm
> [[α1 blocker]] if < 10mm
> surgery if stone > 10mm not passed within hours
> 
> use [[thiazide]]
> avoid [[loop]]
