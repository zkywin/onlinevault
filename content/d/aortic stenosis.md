
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology
> #1 [[vascular calcification]]
> at the valve
> above the valve
> [[bicuspid aortic valve]] 50% at age 50
> [[dilated aortic root]]
> [[supravalvular aortic stenosis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> prolonged asymptomatic stage
> 
> [[fibrosis]] of the cusps of the valve
> [[left ventricular hypertrophy]]
> minor [[extravascular hemolysis]] from shearing damage

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[stable angina]] if < 1cm2
> [[syncope]] → risk of falls
> exercise intolerance

> [!question] Objective Findings
> pulsus parvus et tardus
> [[MSM]]
> [[ES]] at [[2nd ICS right]] [[aortic band]]
> ![[Pasted image 20231213162302.png]]
> no [[physiologic s2 splitting]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> don't treat unless symptomatic
> [[CABG]]
> [[balloon valvuloplasty]]

> [!example] Relationships
> can co-occur with [[aortic regurg]]
> [[rheumatic fever]] will always have BOTH [[aortic valve]] and [[mitral valve]] problems and also [[aortic valve commisure fusion]]
