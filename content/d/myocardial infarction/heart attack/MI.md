[[ACS]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> 20+ min [[chest pain]]
> [[STEMI]] [[NSTEMI]]

> [!info] Etiology
> total occlusion #1 [[LAD]] (45%)
> [[RCA]] or [[LCX]]
> 
> [[prinzmetal angina]] > 20 minutes
> [[coronary artery]] [[vasculitis]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> minutes ↓[[contractility]]
> hours [[cardiogenic shock]]
> days [[neutrophil]] [[vfib]]
> week [[macrophage]] [[acute pericarditis]] [[free wall rupture]] [[IV sepal rupture]] [[papillary rupture]]
> month [[granulation tissue]] [[mural thrombus]] [[dressler syndrome]] [[true ventricular aneurysm]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> atypical in women, elderly, [[DM]]
> crushing chest pain to L arm or jaw
> over 20 min
> [[dyspnea]] [[diaphoresis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[S4]] heard acutely
> [[MI histology]]
> ↑[[β2]] → ↑[[Na-K pump]] → [[translocational hypokalemia]]
> [[st segment]] initial ↓, later ↑
> [[t wave inversion]]
> [[EKG]]
> ↑[[troponin I]]
> ↑[[CK-MB]] for secondary MI
> biopsy with [[TTC stain]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[aspirin]] [[heparin]]
> [[nitrate]] [[MRA]]↓ [[preload]]
> [[β1 blockers]] ↓ [[HR]]
> [[ACEi]] [[SGLT2 blocker]] ↓ blood volume
> [[angioplasty]]
> 
> complication
> [[contraction band necrosis]]
> [[reperfusion injury]]

> [!example] Complications
> [[arrhythmia]]

> [[papillary rupture]]
> [[IV sepal rupture]]
> [[ventricular pseudoaneurysm]]
>
