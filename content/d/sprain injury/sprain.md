[[ACUTE]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[trauma]] stretch or tear of [[ligament]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> transient [[subluxation]] damages capsule and ligaments


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[ankle sprain]]
> [[UCL sprain]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> severe pain with reduced range of motion

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[NSAID]]
> [[MRI]] to assess ligaments
> soft collar to limit range of motion

> [!example] Relationships
> more severe than [[strain]]
