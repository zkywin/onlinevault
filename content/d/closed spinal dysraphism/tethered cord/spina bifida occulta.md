[[spina bifida]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> gap in the vertebrae

> [!info] Detailed Description
> failure to close vertebral arches in the back
> meninges and cord covered by skin

> [!danger] Symptoms
> asymptomatic
> overlying skin has a tuft of hair
> [[tethered cord syndrome]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> only one with normal [[AFP]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[spina bifida]]
> [[anencephaly]] [[rachischesis]]
