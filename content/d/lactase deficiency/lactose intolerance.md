dietary [[lactose]] intolerance
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> inability to metabolize at the [[small intestine]]

> [!info] Detailed Description
> 1.1 congenital [[lactase]] deficiency
> 1.2 natural adult absence of [[lactase-persistent allele]]
> 2.0 [[bacterial gastroenteritis]] distal villi inflammation

> [!danger] Symptoms
> explosive diarrhea
> bloating, cramps, flatulence
> osmotic [[diarrhea]]
> [[abdominal sounds]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> healthy mucosa
> ↓ pH stool (fermentation of [[lactose]] by bacteria)
> H+ on [[hydrogen breath test]]
> check blood glucose after giving lactose


> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[lactase]] pills
> avoiding dietary [[lactose]]
