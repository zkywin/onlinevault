renal outer medulla K+ channel

> [!tip] Function (response to [[aldosterone]] and hyperkalemia)
> K+ recycling: secrete K+ back into the lumen allows for more Na+, Ca+, and Mg+ reabsorption

> [!info] Location
> [[henle 1]]
> [[principal cell]]

> [!check] Activators
> [[aldosterone]]
> [[cortisol]] (sometimes)
> intracellular [[K+]]
> lumen negativity
> high [[Na+]]

> [!fail] Inhibitors
> [[AT2]] via WNK signaling
> high [[H+]] in lumen

> [!example] Relationships
> unlike [[BK]] works under normal kalemic conditions
> [[vomiting]] indirectly increases ROMK activity

> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[hypokalemia]]
