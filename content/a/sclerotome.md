[[somite]]
> [!tip] Function
> [[bone]] + [[cartilage]]

> [!info] Location
> [[PAX]]1 causes [[SHH]] sensitivity

> [!question] Axial Development
> primary [[sclerotome]] surrounds the [[notocord]] first
> primaries condense into segments
> center of primary become [[intervertebral disc]]
> primaries separated into secondaries
> secondaries fuse with adjacent segments to make [[vertebrae]]

> [!warning] Structure
> [[intramembranous ossification]]
> [[endochondral ossification]]
> [[neurocranium]]
> [[vertebrae]] = secondary sclerotome
> [[ribs]] = secondary sclerotome extensions
> [[sternum]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[viscerocranium]]
> [[spinal cord]] spinal nerves persist throughout spinal development


> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[achondroplasia]]
> [[acromegaly]]
> [[gigantism]]
> [[spina bifida]]
> [[craniosynostosis]]
> [[klippel-feil syndrome]]
> [[scoliosis]]
> [[thoracic outlet syndrome]]
