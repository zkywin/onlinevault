
> [!tip] In Short
> fasting > 95, 1hr > 140, 2hr > 120
> within 20 wks of gestation

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> hr count starts at first bite
> ↑[[hPL]] is implicated
> [[insulin]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> osmotic diuresis → ↓maternal [[Mg2+]] → ↓[[PTH]] → neonatal [[hypocalcemia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[LGA baby]] → size-date discrepancy

> [!question] Objective Findings
> 50 g 1-hour [[glucola]] at [[2nd trimester]]
> 
> screened using serum testing
> urine testing is NOT effective

> [!abstract] Treatment
> goal fasting 95, 1hr post 140, 2hr post 120

> [!example] Relationships
> [[diabetic embryopathy]]
