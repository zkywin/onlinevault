
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> "the ITCH that rashes"
> [[HSR 1]]
> [[dyshidrotic eczema]]
> [[nummular eczema]]

> [!info] Etiology
> FLG[[Δ]] filaggrin with water loss
> cold/hot weather
> irritants and allergens
> rarely an [[allergic contact dermatitis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[lichen simplex chronicus]]
> → [[impetigo]]
> ∝ [[HSV-1]] conjunctivitis
> dry, penetrable skin layer
> penetration of irritants
> immune response [[Th2 cell]] [[Th1 cell]] [[IgE]]
> [[IL-4]] [[IL-5]] [[IL-13]] [[IL-31]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> very bad [[s/itching/pruritis]]
> hyperpigmented [[papule]], [[vesicle]], [[plaque]]
> [[crust]], [[lichenification]]
> association with [[asthma]] [[d/seasonal allergies/allergic rhinitis]]
> occipital [[lymphadenopathy]]

> [!question] Distribution
> small infants: extensor surfaces, face, scalp, torso
> childhood: flexor surfaces
> adult: chronic hand/face/eyelids
> ranges from spongiosis in kids to psoriaform in adult

> [!abstract] Treatment
> half of children go into remission by age 12
> [[emollient]]
> topical [[corticosteroids]] + [[calcineurin inhibitors]] 
> [[PDE4 inhibitor]]
> [[UV phototherapy]]
> [[dupilimab]] [[tralokinumab]]
> [[JAK inhibitor]]
> [[NSAID]]

> [!example] Relationships
> ∝ [[hyper IgE syndrome]] [[wiskott-aldrich syndrome]]
> [[psoriasis]] is on the back of joints
> eye findings kind of look like [[dermatomyositis]]
> nummular type looks sort of like [[tinea]]
> [[SSSS]]
> [[dermatitis herpetiformis]]
> [[pityriasis alba]]
> [[keratosis pilaris]]
> [[dandruff]]
> [[psoriasis]]
> [[nummular eczema]]
> [[irritant contact dermatitis]]
> [[impetigo]]
