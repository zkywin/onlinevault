low serum [[K+]] due to [[distal tubule]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[Sk]] < 3.5 mEq/L

> [!info] Etiology
> HYPERtensive [[aldosterone]] [[cortisol]] [[eNaC]]
> NORMOtensive acidotic [[RTA]]
> NORMOtensive alkylotic [[Na+]] [[HCO3-]], [[diuretic]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[ileus]] 

> [!info] Urinary Loss (>30 mEq/day)
> hypertensive: [[2° hyperaldosteronism]], [[hypercortisolism]], [[SAME]], [[liddle syndrome]]
> normotensive acidotic [[RTA]]
> normotensive alkylotic with low [[urine Cl-]] (high [[HCO3-]]): [[anorexia nervosa]], [[metabolic alkalosis]]
> normotensive alkylotic with high [[urine Cl-]]: [[diuretic]], low [[Mg2+]], [[gitelman syndrome]], [[bartter syndrome]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> hyperpolarization of [[neuron]] but somehow increases excitability
> weakness
> [[arreflexia]]
> [[respiratory failure]]
> [[rhabdomyolysis]]
> [[QTc prolongation]]
> [[tachyarrhythmia]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[EKG]] low T amplitude, high U amplitude, 
> [[QTc prolongation]]
> [[spurious hypokalemia]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> fix any [[hypomagnesemia]]
> PO/IV [[K+]] expect +0.25 for every 20 mEq added

> [!example] Relationships
> [[translocational hypokalemia]] is sudden uptake into cells
> [[EtOH]] use lowers uptake
> [[DI]], [[hyperglycemia]]
> associated with [[metabolic alkalosis]] and [[HTN]] when brough on by low [[Na+]] reuptake
> may worsen [[digoxin]] by moving it to the heart
