[[gestational tumors]]
> [!tip] In Short
> before 20 weeks
> snowstorm only complete

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> complete mole: empty egg + 1 sperm x2 (46XX)
> partial mole: normal egg + 2 sperm (69XXX)

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> partial mole: some fetal tissue
> complete mole: no fetal tissue (more persistent)
> 
> 15-20% of completes are invasive
> [[choriocarcinoma]] related to complete mole (new [[hCG]] rise within 6 months after [[D&C]])
> 
> fluid filled cysts swollen [[trophoblast]]
> ovarian theca lutein cyst bilateral
> [[hyperthyroidism]]
> [[preeclampsia]] in [[1st trimester]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> ↑[[hCG]] → [[hyperthyroidism]] → [[hyperreflexia]]
> severe nausea weight loss 
> "hyperemesis gravidarum"
> grape-like mass

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[e/p57]] [[CDK]] negative on [[IF]] in complete mole
> [[US]] transvaginal snowstorm pattern
> ↑↑↑[[hCG]] > 100,000
> [[HTN]] with [[proteinuria]] before 20 weeks
> monitor [[hCG]] to check for [[choriocarcinoma]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> suction [[D&C]]
> [[MTX]] [[dactinomycin]] if [[hCG]] levels remain high
> 
> continue 6 mo [[contraceptive]] "surveillance"
> weekly [[hCG]] monitor till undetectable
> monthly monitoring for 6 months

> [!example] Relationships
> [[rhabdomyosarcoma]] also grapelike
> [[preeclampsia]] is third trimester
> [[ovarian cyst]] theca lutein is related
> [[ectopic pregnancy]] below expected [[hCG]]
> complete sort of like [[uniparental disomy]]
> [[OHSS]]
