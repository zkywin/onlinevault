> [!tip] Condensed Description
> DLCO or TLCO is the extent to which oxygen passes from the air sacs of the lungs into the blood
> diffusion ability of [[O2]] in [[lung]]

> [!question] Mechanism
> estimated using [[carbon monoxide]]
> [[O2]] 100 mmHg in humidified lung

> [!danger] Diseases
> normal in [[extrinsic lung restriction]]
> ↓ in [[restrictive lung]] [[emphysema]] because damage to capillary beds

> [!example] Relationships
> increases with exercise
> [[chronic bronchitis]], [[asthma]] normal [[diffusing capacity]]
