[[miscarriage]]
> [!tip] In Short
> over 20 wks

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[missed abortion]]
> [[inevitable abortion]]
> [[threatened abortion]]
> [[complete abortion]]
> [[septic abortion]]
> [[incomplete abortion]]


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> may cause [[coagulopathy]] [[DIC]]

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> do an [[autopsy]] and [[placental evaluation]]
> check for [[syphilis]] if umbilical vessel surrounding necrosis

> [!abstract] Treatment
> 20-23 wks: [[D&C]] or vaginal delivery
> over 24 wks: [[mifepristone]] 24 hrs → [[misoprostol]]
> vaginal delivery regardless of position

> [!example] Relationships
> [[miscarriage]] is < 20 wks
