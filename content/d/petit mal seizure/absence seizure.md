AKA "petit mal" [[seizure]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> last 5-10 seconds
> impaired consciousness, no post-ictal state

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> exclusively in childhood
> in adults, it's actually a [[focal impaired]]

> [!info] Detailed Description
> between ages 6 and 10
> ∝ [[ADHD]] [[anxiety disorder]]
> [[hyperventilation]] trigger

> [!danger] Symptoms
> staring spells 10s daily "zoning out"
> slight eye fluttering

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[EEG]] generalized 3Hz spike and wave activity

> [!abstract] Treatment
> anti-epileptic treatment to help with school
> 90% of patients go seizure free after about 15 years
