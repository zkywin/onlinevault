
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> lymphoid hyperplasia in children
> hard feces fecalith in adults

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> obstruction of opening of [[appendix]] to [[cecum]]
> [[d/appendiceal abscess]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> fever nausea [[vomiting]]
> [[epigastric pain]] → [[RLQ pain]]
> [[mcburney's point]]: area of worst pain

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[CT]] scan to confirm
> Alvarado score
> 
> [[rovsing's sign]]
> psoas sign
> obturator sign

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[appendectomy]]
> if presenting > 5 days later, can wait 6-8 weeks

> [!example] Relationships
> [[peritonitis]]
> mimic by [[yersinia enterocolitica]]
> [[diverticulitis]] is [[LLQ pain]] in elderly
> [[ectopic pregnancy]] in women
