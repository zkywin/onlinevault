
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> weak collagen

> [!info] Etiology
> classic type: mutant COL5A1, COL1A1 [[collagen 5]]
> vascular type: mutant COL3A1 COL1A1 [[collagen 3]]
> benign type: familial hypermobility type 5

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> vascular type: [[berry aneurysm]], ruptured organs
> ruptured [[uterus]] in childbirth

> [!danger] Symptoms
> stretchy skin
> superflexible joints
> easy bruising
> long-thin "cigarette" scars

> [!question] Objective Findings
> beighton scale > 5/9

> [!abstract] Treatment
