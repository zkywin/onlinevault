
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> severe damage of any part of the kidney
> very low [[GFR]]

> [!info] Etiology
> [[DM]] [[HTN]]
> [[nephritic syndrome]] [[nephrotic syndrome]]
> [[ADPKD]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[renal osteodystrophy]]
> [[HTN]], [[hyperkalemia]], [[metabolic acidosis]], [[hypocalcemia]]
> [[uremia]]
> [[atherosclerosis]] → hypoperfusion → [[angiodysplasia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[HTN]] from increased [[EABV]] from increased [[Na+]]
> [[azotemia]]
> [[angiodysplasia]] → [[hematochezia]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[hyponatremia]] [[hypotonia]]
> ↑↑↑ [[Cr]]
> low [[EPO]], low [[vD]], high [[PO4-]]
> [[EGFR gene]] < 15

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[HD]] or [[kidney transplant]] necessary
> shrunken kidney then develops [[renal cyst]] and [[RCC]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[cirrhosis]] has elevated [[PT-INR]]
> not quite the same as [[CKD]] stage 5
> [[HTN]] may persist due to hyperactivity of [[PTH]], [[NE]], [[EPO]]
