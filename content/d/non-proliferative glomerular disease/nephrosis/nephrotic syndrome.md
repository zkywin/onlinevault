[[glomerulopathy]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[GBM]]/[[podocyte]] destruction

> [!info] Etiology
> [[MCN]] #1 in children
> [[FSGS]] #1 in hispanic/african american
> [[membranous glomerulopathy]] #1 in whites
> [[diabetic nephropathy]]
> [[systemic amyloidosis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[proteinuria]] → [[hypoalbuminemia]] → [[edema]]
> ↑[[albumin]] production → [[HLD]] compensatory
> [[AT3]] wasting → [[hypercoagulability]] → [[renal vein thrombosis]]
> ↑[[NFP]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[proteinuria]]
> low [[albumin]]
> [[urine lipid]]
> [[pitting edema]] of feet and eyes
> [[HLD]]
> [[hypercoagulability]] from [[AT3]] loss

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[UPCR]] and [[ACR]]
> [[albumin]] < 2.5 g/dL
> [[lipiduria]]
> [[azotemia]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[nephritic syndrome]] damages [[endothelium]]
