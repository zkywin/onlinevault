[[HSR 4]] "dirt in eyes, can't chew a cracker"
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> gland destruction

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> women in 40s
> autoimmunity to [[lacrimal gland]] and [[salivary gland]]
> 1° "sicca syndrome"
> 2° [[rheumatoid arthritis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> low secretions
> [[PBC]]
> [[neonatal lupus]]
> [[lymphoma]] of [[B cell]]
> [[candida]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[uveitis]] [[dry eye]]
> dry skin
> [[arthralgia]]
> [[raynaud phenomenon]]
> [[atrophic vaginitis]]
> [[xerostomia]]
> red eye, painful
> enlarged [[parotid gland]]
> [[dental caries]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[ANA panel]] [[rheumatoid factor]]
> schirmer paper test for tears (<10mm)
> salivary testing
> [[lymphoid follicle]] in [[salivary gland]]
> [[anti-SSA Ro]]
> [[anti-SSB La]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> good dental care
> [[pilocarpine]]

> [!example] Relationships
> normal aging may overlap
> [[sarcoidosis]] mimics it
> [[GPA]] [[EGPA]] may mimic it
> [[rheumatoid arthritis]] mostly [[keratitis]]
> [[ankylosing spondylitis]] also uveitis
> [[scleritis]]
