> [[cold shock]] ↑[[TPR]]

> [[ischemia]] and [[hypoxia]]
> [[lactic acidosis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑[[HR]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> TWO LARGE bore hole [[IV]]
> [[NS]] 20mL/kg in 5-20 min
> intraosseous if intravenous fails
