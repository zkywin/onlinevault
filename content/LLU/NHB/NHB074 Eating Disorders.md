> [!info] Definitions
> compensatory mechanisms: weight control behavior either 1. purging or 2. non-purging
> binging: out of control eating behavior or amount
> bulemia: cycles of overeating and purging

[[anorexia nervosa]] is an attempt to lose weight, though binging may stem from the horrible hunger. [[binge eating disorder]] involves an uncontrolled appetite - attempts to cover that up cause [[bulimia nervosa]]. Meanwhile having "weird" eating habits [[ARFID]] or [[pica disorder]] are not necessarily as related to mental health problems. They are separate from [[rumination disorder]], which is more associated with intellectual disability. #done
