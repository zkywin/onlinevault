[[arteriosclerosis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> thickened [[vessel intima]]
> at [[arteriole]] and [[muscular artery]]

> [!info] Etiology
> men, postmenopausal women
> [[HTN]]
> [[d/hypercholesterolemia]]
> [[smoking]]
> [[d/diabetes mellitus/DM]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> at [[abdominal aorta]] [[coronary artery]] [[femoral artery]] [[internal carotid]] [[circle of willis]]
> oxidized [[apoB100]] 
> [[foamy macrophages]] 
> [[scavenger receptor A]]
> 
> [[embolus]]: lipid release
> [[thrombosis]]
> [[aneurysm]]
> [[acute limb ischemia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> at >70% stenosis
> at [[renal artery]] [[renal artery stenosis]]
> [[stable angina]]
> [[IMA]] [[intestinal angina]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[cholesterol]] core with [[necrosis]] in [[vessel intima]]
> fibromuscular cap
> calification

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[statins]]
> [[carotid endarterectomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[ischemic stroke]]
