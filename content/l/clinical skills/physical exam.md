physical exam: general, CV, respiratory, GI, neuro

# blood pressure
Place cuff snugly, support arm at heart level
Palpate brachial pulse while inflating cuff, deflate cuff 2-3mmHg/sec
State correct reading

# vital signs
measure respiratory rate for 15 sec., 60 sec. if irregular
palpate radial pulse for at least 15 sec., 60 sec. if irregular
palpate radial pulses simultaneously for symmetry

# hands/arms
Inspect both hands (dorsa, palms, nails)
Inspect and palpate both arms and forearms with clothing removed

# head/neck
Inspect face, scalp and neck, observing for lesions

# eyes
Test visual fields by confrontation
Test visual fields in each eye separately in 4 directions
Inspect external ocular structures (lids, cornea, conjunctivae)
Evaluate extraocular muscle function in six directions
Check for convergence and accommodation
Observe pupillary response to light (direct)
Observe pupillary response to light (consensual)
Observe for Marcus-Gunn pupil bilaterally
Observe for red reflex
Inspect anterior structures with ophthalmoscope
Inspect optic nerve and disc
Trace vessels in four quadrants
Observe macula

# ears
Observe and palpate auricles and postauricular regions bilaterally
Examine ears bilaterally with otoscope
Test auditory acuity by strong finger rub, with eyes closed, each ear separately

# nose
Inspect nasal vaults with nasal speculum on otoscope
Palpate for frontal sinus tenderness
Palpate for maxillary sinus tenderness

# mouth
Inspect lips, gums, tongue, teeth using flashlight
Inspect floor of mouth and base of tongue
Inspect posterior pharynx
Observe elevation of the palate by asking patient to say "ah"

# lymph nodes
Inspect neck
Palpate lymph nodes: Use pads of the 2-3 fingers in small circles
Palpate axillary nodes - central, anterior, posterior, and lateral groups – 3 sweeps with firm pressure down chest wall, and down humerus
Wash hands after palpating axillary nodes
Palpate inguinal lymph nodes

# thyroid
Locate isthmus of thyroid and inspect neck with and without swallowing
Palpate thyroid from back of patient, in correct anatomical location
Palpate thyroid – have patient turn head toward side being examined
Palpate carotids bilaterally, one side at a time
Auscultate carotids bilaterally, with patient’s breath held

# back
Palpate spinous processes of thoracic and lumbar vertebrae.
Perform fist percussion of thoracic, lumbar, sacral vertebrae, and over sacroiliac joints and costovertebral angles bilaterally

# lungs
Ask patient to cross arms to move scapulae and expose lung fields
Percuss bilaterally and symmetrically, posterior and lateral lung fields, upper, middle and lower lobes

# heart
Observe precordium. Stand on patient’s right side
Palpate with patient sitting using ball of hand: 4ICS-L, apex
Auscultate with patient sitting – auscultate minimum 6 sec. each spot
Check for aortic insufficiency-patient leaning forward in expiration 3ICS-L
Examine JVP from right side with patient in recumbent position
Palpate with patient recumbent using ball of hand: 4ICS-L, apex
Auscultate with patient recumbent: bell 4ICS-L, apex, 
Check for mitral stenosis – roll patient to left lateral position, apex bell and diaphgram

# abdomen
Inspect abdomen
Instruct patient to relax abdominal musculature – knees flexed, arms at sides
Auscultate bowel sounds in one spot before manipulation or palpation
Auscultate for bruits: aorta, renal artery, iliac artery
Percuss abdomen in all 4 quadrants, assessing for tenderness and resonance
Palpate progressing from light to deep pressure – entire abdomen
Palpate femoral pulses bilaterally
Auscultate for femoral bruit bilaterally

# lower limbs
Inspect bilaterally with outer clothes removed
Inspect feet including toes and soles
Palpate pulses bilaterally: posterior tibial, dorsalis pedis
Edema press for 5 seconds behind medial malleolus, dorsum of foot and anterior tibia

# cranial nerves
[[CN I]] Sense of smell (not usually done)
[[CN II]] Visual acuity, Visual fields, Ophthalmoscopic exam
[[CN III]] [[CN IV]] [[CN VI]]Pupillary reaction to light, Extraocular movements
[[CN V]] Facial sensation, masseter strength
[[CN VII]] Raise eyebrows, show teeth, puff cheeks, smile
[[CN VIII]] Hearing
[[CN IX]] [[CN X]] Observe palate elevation while vocalizing "ah"
[[CN XI]] Rotate head, shrug shoulder to resistance
[[CN XII]] Midline tongueprotrusion

# motor exam
Upper extremity: shoulder, pronator drift, dorsiflexion, grip, interossei
Lower extremity: hip, knee, foot flex/dorsiflex, gait
Dexterity: foot tap, hand flap

# reflex
biceps, triceps, brachioradialis, patella, achilles

# cerebellar
Rapid alternating movements - hands
Finger to nose bilaterally
Heel-to-shin bilaterally

# sensation
Vibration - 128 tuning fork on malleolus
Proprioception - at least in toe
Pressure sensation on soles of feet bilat. using 5.07/10 g [[monofilament test]]
Light touch and pinprick trunk and 4 limbs (compare sensation symmetrically, proximal and distal)


# gait/balance
Have patient bend forward at waist (observe ROM and curvatures)
Have patient bend backwards and side to side
Ask patient to touch chin to chest, left and right shoulder and bend the head straight back
