[[spinal cord]]
> [!tip] Function
> sensation

> [!info] Location
> somas are in [[DRG]]

> [!warning] Structure
> medial: 
> [[Ia fiber]], [[Ib fiber]]: proprioception
> [[II fiber]], [[Aβ fiber]]: touch
> 
> lateral:
> [[III fiber]], [[Aδ fiber]]: sharp pain/cold
> [[IV fiber]], [[C fiber]]: dull pain/warm

> [!example] Relationships
> embryo via [[BMP]]
> [[ventral horn]] other side of sulcus limitans
> embryonically tied to [[hindbrain]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[arnold-chiari malformation]] Chiari 1 and 2
> [[dandy-walker malformation]]
