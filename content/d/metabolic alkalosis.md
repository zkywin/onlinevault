[[1° acid base disorder]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ↑[[HCO3-]]

> [!info] Etiology
> 1 [[H+]] renal wasting
> 2 [[H+]] GI wasting
> 3 ↑[[HCO3-]] infusion, [[dehydration]]
> 4 [[Na+]][[Cl-]] wasting sm of organic anions

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[vomiting]] causes urine pH increase initially. After stop, pH will decrease considerably

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[urine Cl-]] < 10 implies [[dehydration]] [[hypokalemia]] [[vomiting]], [[diarrhea]], [[diuretic]], [[CF]]
> [[urine Cl-]] > 10 with [[bartter syndrome]], [[gitelman syndrome]]
> [[urine Cl-]] > 10 with [[dehydration]] [[2° hyperaldosteronism]]
> [[anion gap]] is a common finding for [[mixed acid base disorder]]
> urine [[K+]] is usually high

> [!abstract] Treatment
> give [[hypertonic fluids]] if [[urine Cl-]] is low

> [!example] Relationships
> increased [[HCO3-]] and intracellular [[K+]] activity enhances [[ROMK]] causing [[hypokalemia]]
> worsened by [[dehydration]]
