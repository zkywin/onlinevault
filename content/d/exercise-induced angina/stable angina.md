[[CAD]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[chest pain]] with exercise/emotion
> not relieved by [[antacid]]

> [!info] Etiology
> 70% stenosis
> exertion increases [[O2 demand]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[endocardium]] damage
> reversible [[cardiac muscle]] injury

> [!danger] Symptoms
> chest pain < 20 min
> radiates to L arm or jaw
> [[diaphoresis]]
> [[dyspnea]]


> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[st depression]] on exertion

> [!abstract] Treatment
> rest, [[β1 blockers]] if not severe
> [[nitroglycerin]]
> [[cardiac catheterization]] if severe
> [[ranolazine]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[takotsubo cardiomyopathy]]
> [[d/atypical angina]]
