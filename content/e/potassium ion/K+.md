potassium ion
> [!tip] Function
> [[neuron]] excitation and signaling
> vasodilator in response to myocyte [[ATP]] depletion

> [!info] Location
> 2% is [[ECF]] with [[Sk]] = 4.0mEq/L
> 90% is [[ICF]] due to [[Na-K pump]]
> 
> excreted 90% kidney and 10% colon
> 90% reabsorbed a [[proximal tubule]] and [[henle 1]]
> 10% secretion at the [[distal tubule]] by [[aldosterone]]

> [!warning] Channels
> [[Na-K pump]]
> [[ROMK]] 
> [[BK]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> IV or PO, for every 20 mEq expect ↑0.25 serum level

> [!example] Relationships
> lowers [[HTN]] risk
> secretion favored by [[Na+]], [[aldosterone]], [[H+]]
> reuptake favored by [[AT2]] and [[ADH]]
> cellular uptake favored by [[insulin]], [[β2]]
> cellular release favored by [[ADP]], [[α1]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[hypokalemia]]
> [[translocational hypokalemia]]
