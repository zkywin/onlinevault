[[ileus]]
> [!tip] In Short
> acute megacolon is colon only
> post-op ileus usually also small bowel

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[hypokalemia]] → muscle weakness
> 3-7 days after surgery in men > 60 yrs

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> if prolonged > 72 hrs, typically self resolves

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[NG tube]] + rectal tube if < 12 cm
> [[laparotomy]] if cecum > 12cm, ischemic signs

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> [[SBO]]
