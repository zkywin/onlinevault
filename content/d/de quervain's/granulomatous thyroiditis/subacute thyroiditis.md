[[thyroiditis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> young female
> viral illness

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> acute inflammation → follicular rupture
> [[granuloma]] formation
> inflammatory process
> release → transient [[hyperthyroidism]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> tender thyroid gland
> pain may migrate

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[anti-TPO]]
> [[I131]] low uptake
> ↑[[thyroglobulin]]
> ↑[[T4h]] ↓[[TSH]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[NSAID]]
> [[corticosteroids]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[hashimoto thyroiditis]] is painless
