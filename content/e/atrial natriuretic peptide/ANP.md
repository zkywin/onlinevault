[[natriuretic peptide]]
> [!tip] Function
> increases [[NFP]] at [[glomerulus]]
> response to increased [[EABV]]
> lowers [[eNaC]]

> [!info] Location
> secreted by heart right atrium with stretch
> only increases afferent [[glomerulus]]
> uses [[cGMP]]

> [!warning] Structure

> [!example] Relationships
> opposed by [[ADH]] [[RAAS]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
