
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[muscular artery]] [[vasculitis]] at [[coronary artery]]

> [!info] Etiology
> asian children < 4 years
> typically 15-18 months

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> acute phase 10 days
> subacute phase 10 days
> convalescent phase 3-8 wk
> 
> [[coronary artery]] [[aneurysm]]
> [[thrombosis]] [[MI]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> desquamating on skin
> rash on palms and soles
> [[strawberry tongue]]
> fissured lips
> [[fever]], [[conjunctivitis]] 
> [[cervical nodes]] [[lymphadenopathy]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> get an [[US]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[aspirin]] antiplatelet (special case to give for child)
> [[IVIg]]
> [[flu shot]]
> [[🅢 cardiology ✓]] follow up

> [!example] Relationships
> [[reye's syndrome]] exception
> not [[takayasu arteritis]]
> [[scarlet fever]] has sore throat, no conjunctivitis
> [[toxic shock syndrome]] different
