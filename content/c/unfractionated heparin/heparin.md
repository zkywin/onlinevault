> block [[F10]] [[thrombin]]  also [[F9]] [[F11]]

> injections can be used more often

> unfractionated heparin is ok with [[ESRD]]

> [!warning] Metabolism

> [[HIT]]

> ↺ [[protamine]]

> [!example] Interactions

> [[warfarin]] measured with [[PT-INR]]
