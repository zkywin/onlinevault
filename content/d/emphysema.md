[[obstructive lung]] "pink puffer"
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> alveoli direct destruction

> [!info] Etiology
> [[centriacinar emphysema]] [[smoking]] ↑[[elastase]]
> [[panacinar emphysema]] [[α1 antitrypsin deficiency]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> cigarette smoke → ↑[[macrophage]] [[neutrophil]] → [[MMP]] [[free radicals]] →  alveolar destruction
> 
> ↑[[compliance]] ↓[[stiffness]] 
> early compensatory [[respiratory alkalosis]]
> late [[respiratory acidosis]] from [[hypoxemia]]
> [[cor pulmonale]] from alveolar vessel loss and constriction

> [!danger] Symptoms
> pursed lips expiration ↑backpressure
> barrel-chest: high [[FRC]]
> bilateral [[wheezing]]
> weight loss


> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↓[[diffusing capacity]]
> diminished heart and lung sound
> spongy dilated holes of lung
> abnormally clear [[XR]]
> ↑[[FRC]]



> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> opposite of [[idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis]]
> minimal [[sputum]] unlike [[chronic bronchitis]]
> the opposite of having [[idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis]]
