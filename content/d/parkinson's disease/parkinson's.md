[[alpha synuclein]] [[parkinsonism]] 
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[substantia nigra]] pars compacta degeneration >50-70%

> [!info] Etiology
> [[autosomal dominant]] PARK18 risk factor
> [[MPTP poisoning]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ⦻ [[ubiquitin]] [[proteasome]]
> [[alpha synuclein]] toxicity
> ↓ [[DA]] → ↓[[Glu]] in the brain
> ↑ [[ACh]]
> late stage [[dementia]]
> 
> [[locus coeruleus]] sleep disturbances
> [[CN I]] [[anosmia]]
> [[CN X]] [[constipation]]

> [!danger] Symptoms (T.R.A.P.)
> [[MDS-UPDRS]]
> T [[resting tremor]] asymmetrical pill roling
> R rigidity [[cogwheeling]]
> A [[akinesia]]
> P postural instability
> S shuffling/festingating gait, small handwriting
> 
> asymmetrical arm swing
> constipation, [[dandruff]], [[dystonia]], [[REM sleep behavior disorder]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[alpha synuclein]]
> if [[DA]] does not help, may not be parkinson's
> [[Dopamine Transporter DaTScan imaging]]


> [!abstract] Treatment
> tremor: [[trihexyphenidyl]] [[benztropine]] [[amantidine]] for dyskinesia
> [[levodopa]] if > 70
> [[DA blocker]] if < 70
> [[MAOb]] [[rasagiline]] [[selegiline]]
> [[DBS]] 
> [[MRgFUS]]
> 
> [[ropinirole]] 
> [[anticholinergics]]
> Low protein diet


> [!example] Relationships
> [[lewy body dementia]] early onset [[dementia]]
> [[MSA]] has [[autonomic instability]]
> [[tardive dyskinesia]] a side effect
