"crazy toxo cat lady" [[protozoan]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> cat feces oocysts
> ingest bradyzoites encyst in brain
> tachyzoites encyst in neural tissue may reactivate

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> congenital toxoplasmosis
> [[TORCH infection]] (active not latent)
> [[HIV]] [[immunocompromised]] disease

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[hydrocephalus]] retinitis, [[calcification]] especially [[basal ganglia]]
> [[ascites]] [[hepatosplenomegaly]] in fetus

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[IgM]] antibodies in first week
> [[IgG]] peak 6-8 wks, falling over 2 years
> [[tachyzoites]]
> [[bradyzoites]]
> [[calcification]] in brain

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[TPM-SMX]] prophylaxis if serology positive
> [[pyrimethamine]] [[sulfadiazine]] treatment
> 
> [[pregnancy]] [[spiramycin]] if < 18 wks
> if > 18wks [[pyrimethamine]] [[sulfadiazine]] [[leucovorin]]
> given to the newborn at birth as well

> [!example] Relationships
> [[cat scratch disease]]
> [[CMV]] also calcification
> may appear like a [[EBV]] picture
