[[posterior pituitary]]
> [!tip] Function
> concentrate urine (↑[[Uosm]] ↓[[free water]])
> conserve water
> thirst

> [!info] Mechanism
> ↑[[Na-K pump]]
> [[V1]] vasoconstrict → ↑[[NFP]]
> [[V2]] → ↑ [[AQP2]] at [[principal cell]]
> at [[distal tubule]] [[collecting tubule]]
> releases [[vWF]] and [[F8]]

> [!warning] Location
> [[AV3V region]] regulation center
> synthesized at [[paraventricular nucleus]] and [[supraoptic nuclei]]
> released at posterior [[pituitary]]

> [!check] Activators
> [[baroreceptor]] 
> [[osmoreceptors]]

> [!fail] Inhibitors
> [[cortisol]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[Uosm]] < 100 mOsm low [[ADH]] activity
> [[Uosm]] > 300 mOsm high [[ADH]] activity

> [!example] Relationships
> in extreme volume depletion, will work on [[eNaC]]
> [[aldosterone]] also uses [[H-ATPase]], [[Na-K pump]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[SIADH]]
> [[DI]]
