[[autosomal dominant]] [[EMERGENT]] [[porphyria]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> acute [[neuropathy]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> adults 30-40
> ⦻ [[porphobilinogen deaminase]]
> [[CYP450]] inducers ie. [[griseofulvin]], [[phenobarbital]], [[EtOH]], [[fasting]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> triggers for [[heme synthesis]]
> ↑[[porphorobilinogen]] ↑ [[δ-ALA]] accumulation
> neurotoxic effects


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[abdominal pain]] + [[altered]]
> 
> [[neuropathy]]
> bilateral upper extremity [[proximal weakness]]
> [[respiratory failure]]
> [[dark urine]] "wine colored"

> [!question] Objective Findings
> urine [[porphobilinogen]] [[δ-ALA]]
> urine [[urobilinogen]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> avoid triggers
> slow [[heme synthesis]] [[heme]] (hemin) [[glucose]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[lead]]
> [[guillain-barre]] and [[arsenic]] do not have GI symptoms
> [[porphyria cutanea tarda]]
