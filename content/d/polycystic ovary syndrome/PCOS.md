
> [!tip] In Short
> 2/3:  [[hirsuitism]] + [[anovulation]] + polycystic ovaries
> unruptured follicles

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> genetics
> [[obesity]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[LH]] / [[FSH]] > 2
> ↑[[LH]] → ↓[[GnRH]] → ↓[[FSH]] → ↓[[estrogen]] → [[amenorrhea]]
> ↑[[LH]] → [[thecal cell]] → ↑[[androstenedione]] → [[estrone]] → [[fat cell]] → [[testosterone]]
> 
> [[DM2]] [[gastric adenocarcinoma]] [[endometrial carcinoma]] very high risk


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[acne vulgaris]]
> [[hirsuitism]] → ↑muscle mass
> [[androgenic alopecia]]
> [[infertility]]
> [[DM2]]
> [[acanthosis nigricans]]


> [!question] Objective Findings
> "string of pearls"
> follicular cysts
> high [[testosterone]]
> [[LH]] >> [[FSH]]
> [[glucola]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> weight loss
> [[OCP]] [[spironolactone]]
> [[metformin]] [[TZDs]] to prevent [[DM2]]
> [[letrozole]] + [[metformin]] for [[infertility]]

> [!example] Relationships
> not severe enough for [[virilization]]
