[[CAH]]
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> loss at [[adrenal gland]] and [[gonads]]
> loss of 17-hydroxylase and 17,20 lyase
> low [[cortisol]]
> low [[androgen]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> high [[ACTH]]
> high [[aldosterone]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> XY [[ambiguous genitalia]]
> males can look totally female
> females have [[amenorrhea]]
> very mild [[hypokalemia]]
> [[cryptorchidism]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> diagnosed at [[puberty]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> part of [[CYP450]]
> [[ketoconazole]] [[spironolactone]] similar effects
