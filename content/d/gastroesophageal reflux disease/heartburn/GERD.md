> lower esophageal sphincter issue

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[spitting up]] = physiologic form
> female
> [[CREST syndrome]]
> [[EtOH]] [[smoking]] [[hiatal hernia]]
> ingesting cleaners)

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> nocturnal [[asthma]]
> [[collagen]] → [[stricure]]
> [[ulcer]]
> [[barrett's esophagus]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> post-prandial
> episodes last > 1 hour
> cough worse at night
> "nocturnal asthma"

> [!question] Objective Findings
> 24hr [[pH]] monitoring (only if surgery coming up)
> basal zone hyperplasia
> [[lamina propria]] elongation
> [[eosinophil]] [[neutrophil]] in [[mucosa]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[PPI]]
> weight loss
> avoid fatty food, caffeine, chocolate
> [[nissen fundoplication]]
> [[antacid]]
> [[H2 blocker]] 

> [!example] Relationships
> [[EOE]]
> [[diffuse esophageal spasm]] precipitated by stress
> [[d/sandifer syndrome]]
