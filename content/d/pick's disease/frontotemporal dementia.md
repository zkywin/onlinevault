[[autosomal dominant]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[frontal]] [[temporal]] destruction

> [!info] Etiology
> problems with [[ubiquitin]] [[proteasome]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[tau]]
> pick's (more behavioral)
> semantic dementia (more speech related)
> 

> [!danger] Symptoms
> 50% behavioral disturbances ie. [[disinhibition]], empathy loss, compulsive behaviors
> progressive [[language]]
> 20% develop [[ALS]]-like symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> knife-edge gyri: very severe frontal and temporal atrophy
> [[pick bodies]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> least common [[tau]] disease
> [[alzheimer's]] is uniform atrophy
> [[non-pick's frontotemporal lobar degeneration]] same gross view, but different aggregate composition
