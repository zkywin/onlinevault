[[restrictive lung]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> adults > 40 years

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> cyclical lung injury and healing
> [[TGF-β]] → [[fibrosis]]
> [[ARDS]] knockout of [[pneumocyte 2]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> progressive [[dyspnea]]
> [[cough]]
> [[digital clubbing]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> "honeycomb lung" [[CT]]
> [[fibrosis]] subpleural
> [[idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis]] on [[XR]]
> low [[FRC]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[transplant]]
> anti-fibrotic block [[TGF-β]] [[perfenidone]] [[nintedanib]]
