syndrome apparent mineralocorticoid excess
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[cortisol]] works at [[kidney]]

> [!info] Etiology
> [[11b-hydroxysteroid hydrogenase 2]] deficiency
> consumption of [[black licorice]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[ROMK]],  [[eNaC]] at [[principal cell]]
> [[H-ATPase]] at [[AIC]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> childhood [[HTN]]
> [[hypokalemia]]
> [[metabolic alkalosis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[cortisol]] to [[cortisone]] ratio
> low [[aldosterone]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]] (feedback ⊣)
> [[K-sparing diuretic]] [[spironolactone]]

> [!example] Relationships
> seen in [[hypercortisolism]] as well
> [[1° hyperaldosteronism]] has actually high aldosterone
