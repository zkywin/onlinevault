[[GI atresia]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology
> failed "recanalization"
> [[down syndrome]]
> 1-2 days after birth

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> duodenum naturally obstructed during epithelial development
> failed re-opening

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[bilious emesis]] OR nonbilious [[vomiting]]
> no stools

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[double bubble]]
> [[l/ultrasound/US|US]] fluid filled stomach on fetal US

> [!abstract] Treatment
> surgical repair

> [!example] Relationships
> [[annular pancreas]] also double bubble
> [[jejunal atresia]]
