
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> "irregularly irregular rhythm"
> [[PV]]/[[LA]]

> [!info] Etiology
> #1 [[HTN]]
> ∝ [[OSA]]
> [[hyperthyroidism]] (check [[TSH]] in new diagnosis)
> [[left heart failure]] [[CAD]] 
> [[EtOH]] trigger "holiday heart"
> ∝ [[mitral stenosis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[PV]]/[[LA]] stretching/remodeling 
> random action potentials in atria
> slow [[AV node]] protects ↓ conduction to ventricle
> ↓[[EDV]] loss of atrial kick
> [[cardiomyopathy]] → [[CHF]]
> [[mural thrombus]] → [[ischemic stroke]], [[mesenteric ischemia]], [[PE]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[palpitations]]
> [[dyspnea]] 
> [[fatigue]]
> ↑ [[HR]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> no [[p wave]]
> [[qrs complex]] irregularity

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[β1 blockers]] + [[non-DHP CCB]]
> [[defibrillation]] after [[echo]]/[[anticoagulant]] for thrombus
> 
> [[cardiac ablation]] [[PV]]
> [[CHADSVASc score]] [[anti-platelet drugs]]
> [[digoxin]]

> [!example] Relationships
> "valvular afib" combined with [[mitral stenosis]]
> [[aflutter]] [[RA]] only
