[[peptide hormone]]
> [!tip] Function
> increases "free calcium"
> ↑[[Ca2+]] ↓[[PO4-]] (loss to urine)
> [[calcitriol]] [[M-CSF]]

> [!check] Activators
> [[PO4-]]

> [!fail] Inhibitors
> [[Ca2+]] via [[CaSR]]
> [[Mg2+]] via [[CaSR]]
> [[vD]]

> [!info] Location
> ↑ [[distal tubule]] [[Ca-Na exchanger]]
> 
> daily bolus: activate [[osteoblast]]
> continuously: activates [[osteoclast]]
> activate [[1α-hydroxylase]]
> [[proximal tubule]] low [[PO4-]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[PTHrP]]
> help fix [[metabolic acidosis]]
> [[vD]] increase [[PO4-]] but overall [[PTH]] lowers
> [[teriparatide]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> [[hyperparathyroidism]] [[FHH]]
> [[hypoparathyroidism]]
> [[pseudohypoparathyroidism]]
