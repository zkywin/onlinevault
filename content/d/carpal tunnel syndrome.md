
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [[lunate]] dislocation
> [[rheumatoid arthritis]]
> [[systemic amyloidosis]]
> [[hypothyroidism]]
> [[acromegaly]]
> [[DM]]
> [[pregnancy]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> compression of [[median nerve]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> numb digits 1-3
> weakness of [[adductor pollicis brevis]]
> palm sensation remains
> radiating pain up arm
> shaking hand relieves pain
> tingling from holding above head for several mins

> [[tinel sign]]
> [[phalen maneuver]]
> 🜲 [[EMG]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> splint
> topical → oral [[corticosteroids]]
> incise the flexor retinaculum
