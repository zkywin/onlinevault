[[EMERGENT]] 
[[neuropathy]] [[CNS demyelination]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[schwann cells]] [[molecular mimicry]] [[HSR 2]]
> ascending paralysis

> [!info] Etiology 
> 🜲 1°  [[diarrhea]] illness ie. [[campylobacter jejuni]]
> ICE MyCZ [[flu]] [[CMV]] [[EBV]] [[mycoplasma pneumoniae]] [[zika virus]] [[COVID]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[molecular mimicry]] → [[autoantibody]] [[GM1]] [[GM2]]
> ⦻ [[schwann cells]] 
> motor neuron loss
> risk of [[respiratory failure]] in the first two weeks
> [[miller-fisher guillan-barre]]
> AIDP is 60-80% of cases

> [!danger] Symptoms
> ascending paralysis
> bilateral [[facial paralysis]]
> [[↑DC]] damage
> [[arreflexia]]
> [[bradycardia]]

> [!question] Tests
> [[MRI]] often normal
> [[EMG conduction block]]
> [[lymphocytic infiltrate]] + 
> [[CSF cytoalbuminologic dissociation]] [[LP]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> steroids are useless!!!
> [[PLEX]] or [[IVIg]] 
> [[flu vaccine]] can prevent guillan-barre
> [[intubation]] for [[FVC]] < 20, distress

> [!example] Relationships
> [[charcot-marie tooth]] is the slow version
> [[botulism]] has descending paralysis
> [[bell's palsy]] also has facial paralysis
> [[tick-borne paralysis]] has normal [[CSF]]
