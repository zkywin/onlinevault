> [!tip] Condensed Description
> below the bone
> above [[dura mater]]

> [!info] Etiology
> lacerated [[middle meningeal artery]]
> [[dura mater]] dissected from [[skull]] in young
> temporal [[skull fracture]]
> pulling epidural catheter

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> blood collection in area
> lucid interval up to 48 hrs before permanent damage
> expansion causes [[brain herniation]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> lucid interval up to 48 hrs before permanent damage
> may co-occur with scalp hematoma

> [!question] Objective Findings
> lens-shaped lesion [[CT]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[craniotomy]] if significant enough
> [[skull trephination]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[subdural hemorrhage]]
> [[brain hemorrhage]]
