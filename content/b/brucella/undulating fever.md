> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> abortis in cows, suis in pigs, melitensis in goats
> milk, cheese products

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> prevent phagolysosome fusion
> infect [[macrophage]]
> spread to reticuloendothelial cells
> [[osteomyelitis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> fever, chills, anorexia
> undulant fever
> moldy hay smell
> [[splenomegaly]] [[hepatomegaly]] [[lymphadenopathy]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> facultative [[intracellular]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[tetracyclines]]
> [[rifampin]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[Q fever]]
