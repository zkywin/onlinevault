"ASPirating lung hyphae-ridden scarecrow"
[[fungi]]
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> inhaled spores
> ∝ [[asthma]] [[CF]]
> previous [[Tb]] or [[klebsiella]] can free up a cavity in the lung

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> allergy → [[bronchiectasis]]
> aflatoxin → [[HCC]]
> [[immunocompromised]] → invasive aspergillosis
> [[fever]] + [[cough]] + [[hemoptysis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> 3+ months symptoms
> weight loss
> cavitary lesions

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑[[IgE]] [[eosinophil]]
> halo sign on [[CT]] in invasive aspergillosis

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships

> [!danger] Diseases
> acute branching unlike [[fungal encephalitis]]
