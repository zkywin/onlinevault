> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> young boys < 5 years (start)
> band Xp21 [[dystrophin]][[Δ]] frameshift deletion

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[sarcomere]] destabilization → damage
> inflammatory response → [[lymphocytic infiltrate]]
> ⦻ distal muscle regeneration → fat replacement
> [[respiratory failure]] 
> [[dilated cardiomyopathy]]
> death at young age

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[proximal weakness]]
> [[calf psesudohypertrophy]]
> [[gower's sign]]
> pelvic girdle weakness

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑↑[[CK]] (10-40k) 
> ↑[[aldolase A]]
> [[muscle biopsy]] [[dystrophin]] <5% = DMD, 
> [[muscle biopsy]] [[dystrophin]] 20-50% = BMD
> failed [[dystrophin stain]]
