[[EMERGENT]] [[seizure]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Detailed Description
> seizure in children tend to last longer than adults
> mortality 20%
> excess Ca2+ influx NMDA causes neuronal injury
> lasting neurological damage 20%
> [[cortical laminar necrosis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> 30-60 min has mortality 5-10%
> 60+ min has mortality 35-40%

> [!danger] Symptoms
> generalized seizure for more than 5 min
> 

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[anti-NMDA receptor antibodies]] on lumbar puncture
> delusions

> [!abstract] Treatment
> airway, breathing, circulation
> 
> ★ [[benzos]] [[IV]] ([[lorazepam]] 2mg)
> refractory: [[levetiracetam]], [[fosphenytoin]], [[valproate]]
> 
> [[IVIg]], [[corticosteroids]]
> [[phenytoin]]
> [[phenobarbital]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[1° general]] [[focal to general]]
> related to [[ischemic stroke]] [[viral encephalitis]], etc.
> associated with [[d/ovarian teratoma]]
> [[non-convulsive status epilepticus]]
