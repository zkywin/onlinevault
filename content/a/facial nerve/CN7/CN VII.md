[[pons]], [[CN VI]]
> [!tip] Function
> motor: facial expression
> taste: anterior 2/3 tongue
> palsy: lower [[facial paralysis]]

> [!info] Location
> nerve wraps around [[CN VI]]
> [[AICA]] to [[CN VII]] nucleus
> input from [[↓CBT]]

> [!warning] Structure
> [[stapedius]]
> [[a/chorda tympani]]
> [[a/greater petrosal]]
> [[a/orbicularis oculi]]
> double innervation upper face
> single innervation lower face

> [!danger] Pathologies
> ⦻ [[stapedius]] → hyperacusis
> [[millard-gubler syndrome]] 
> [[acoustic schwannoma]] lower motor facial weakness
> [[bell's palsy]] isolated inflammatory damage
> [[ramsay hunt syndrome]]
> [[corticosteroids]] [[acyclovir]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[parasympath]] [[lacrimal gland]] greater petrosal branch
> [[parasympath]] [[submandibular]] [[sublingual]] salivation corda tympani
