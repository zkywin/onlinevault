calcium pyrophosphate dihydrate
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> ANKH gain of function mutation
> associated with [[chondrocyte]] death in [[OA]]
> [[hemochromatosis]]
> [[hyperparathyroidism]]
> [[alkaptonuria]]
> [[wilson's disease]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> pyrophosphate from [[ATP]]
> reduced [[proteoglycan]] and pyrophosphatase
> crystals precipitate

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> rhomboid crystals 
> weak [[birefringence]] 
> [[calcification]] of joint [[cartilage]] [[chondrocalcinosis]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[NSAID]] → [[colchicine]] → [[corticosteroids]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[OA]]
> [[gout]]
> differential for [[rheumatoid arthritis]]
> not correlated with [[calcium stones]]
