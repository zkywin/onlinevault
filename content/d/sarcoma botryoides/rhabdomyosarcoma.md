[[sarcoma]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[skeletal muscle]] tumor

> [!info] Etiology
> common in children
> [[li-fraumeni syndrome]] adds [[anaplasia]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> embryonal type (2-4 years): sarcoma botryoides
> 
> alveolar type: t(2;12) t(1;13) translocation
> alveolar type (12-18 years): head and neck, extremities

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[nasal polyp]] kid
> may cause unilateral [[exophthalmos]]

> [!question] Object[](exophthalmos.md)[desmin]] positive
> [[MyoD]] positive
> myogenin positive

> [!abstract] Treatment
> embryonal type good prognosis
> alveolar type harder to treat
