[[conversion disorder]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> psychogenic behavior

> [!info] Detailed Description
> not neurologic in the slightest

> [!danger] Symptoms
> thrashing, thrusting
> lasts for hours (very long)
> eyes are closed during
> lack of post-ictal

> [!question] Objective Findings
> serum [[e/prolactin]] is normal

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> majority of patients have [[MDD]] or [[anxiety disorder]]
> may co-occur with [[d/epilepsy]]
