
> [!tip] Function
> helps with gastric emptying

> [!info] Location
> every 2-3 hours
> ≈ 16 oz/day
> 10-30 min/feeding

> [!warning] Structure
> [[casein]] 30%
> [[lactose]] 
> whey 70%

> [!example] Relationships
> [[prolactin]] receptor downregulated in [[pregnancy]]
> cow milk has 70% casein, 30% whey (wait till 12 mo)
> ⚠ contraindicated [[PCP]] [[cannabis]] [[cocaine]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> ⦻ if [[HIV]] or [[classic galactosemia]]
> don't on [[PCP]], [[cocaine]], [[cannabis]]
> [[breast milk jaundice]]
> [[lactation failure jaundice]]
