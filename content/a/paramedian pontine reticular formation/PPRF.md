[[CN VI]] interneuron
> [!tip] Function
> eye points toward it
> abduction 
> ⤩ to [[frontal eye field]]

> [!info] Location
> [[pons]]

> [!warning] Structure
> input from opposite [[frontal eye field]]
> direct output to [[CN VI]]
> indirect output to [[CN III]] via [[MLF]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> both eyes look away

> [!example] Relationships
> may accompany [[CN VII]] paralysis
