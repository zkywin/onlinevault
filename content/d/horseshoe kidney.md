fused kidney, horseshoe kidney, pancake kidney
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> connection at lower pole = horseshoe
> connection at both poles = pancake kidney

> [!info] Detailed Description
> trapped below the [[IMA]] unable to ascend

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[hydronephrosis]]
> increased risk [[nephrolithiasis]], [[RCC]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> visible on [[CT]] and [[US]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> associated with [[turner syndrome]], [[patau syndrome]], [[edwards syndrome]], [[down syndrome]]
