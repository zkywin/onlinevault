> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[alveoli]] collapse without fluid filling

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[post-op]] day 2-3
> shallow breathing/weak cough due to pain

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[VQ mismatch]] with [[VQ ratio]] = 0
> [[right to left shunt]]
> [[trachea]] TOWARDS collapse

> [!danger] Symptoms
> decreased breath sounds
> dull percussion
> decreased fremitus

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[post-op]] [[c/incentive spirometer]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[consolidation]] is fluid inside alveoli
