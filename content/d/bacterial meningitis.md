[[EMERGENT]]
[[viral meningitis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> inflamed [[pia mater]] [[arachnoid mater]]

> [!info] Etiology
> neonate: [[GBS]], [[e. coli]] [[listeria]]
> children: [[strep pneumoniae]] [[URI]]
> adolescents [[meningococcus]]
> adults: [[strep pneumoniae]] [[TBI]]
> 
> unvaccinated infants: [[H flu]]
> [[coxsackie B virus]] [[candida]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[hydrocephalus]], [[brain herniation]] death
> [[fibrosis]] [[seizure]] [[deafness]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> A [[altered]]
> F [[focal deficits]]
> I [[immunocompromised]]
> S [[seizure]]
> H hx. cns dz
> 
> [[fever]]
> [[headache]]
> nuchal rigidity, [[kernig sign]], [[bradzinski sign]]

> [!question] Tests
> [[cloudy meninges]]
> [[LP]] elevated opening pressure
> [[neutrophil]] and protein, low [[glucose]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[blood culture]] → [[LP]] → [[Abx]]
> 
> <1mo, [[ampicillin]] + [[gentamicin]] OR [[cefotaxime]]
> 1+mo [[vancomycin]] + [[ceftriaxone]] ± [[ampicillin]]
> [[dexamethasone]] reduces [[sensorineural deafness]] in kids

> [!example] Relationships
> [[viral meningitis]] is less dangerous
> [[fungal meningiti]] has low glucose


> [!quote] Experience
> history months of tonsil inflammation
> amoxicillin precipitated worsening neuro symptoms
> bilateral eye pain, neck pain, ataxic gait
> no focal neurological deficits, no papilledema
