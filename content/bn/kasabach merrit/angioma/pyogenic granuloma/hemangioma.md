[[benign neoplasm]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[blood vessel]] [[vessel tumor]]
> often liver or skin

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> strawberry capillary type: infantile head and neck
> cherry capillary type: elderly "[[pyogenic granuloma]]"

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> capillary type
> [[cavernous hemangioma]]: "kasabach merrit" [[liver]]
> arterio-venous type: alternate large and small
> trapping of [[plt]] → [[thrombocytopenia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms


> [!question] Objective Findings
> blanched when pushed

> [!abstract] Treatment
> strawberry type typically regress during childhood
> [[β1 blockers]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[purpura]] do not blanch when pressed
> [[angiosarcoma]]
> "angioma" includes [[hemangioma]] and [[lymphangioma]]
> [[port-wine stain]]
