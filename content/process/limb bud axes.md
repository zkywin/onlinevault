[[FGF]]-secreing AER (apical ectodermal ridge) establishes cutting edge of the limb development "proximodistal axis"

[[SHH]]-secreting ZPA (zone of polarizing activity) establishes "anteroposterior AKA radioulnar axis" and spares the thumb

[[WnT]]7a-secreting Dorsal Ectoderm establishes the "dorsoventral axis" and "dorsalizes" converts default flexors to extensors

the progress zone is full of stem cells to support growth
