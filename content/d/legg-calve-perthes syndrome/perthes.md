[[avascular necrosis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> idiopathic avascular necrosis of the femoral head without a preceding fracture

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> younger than 10 (3-12 years old)

> [!warning] Pathogenesis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> limited internal rotation
> antalgic gait = limp

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[XR]] frog legs → [[MRI]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> limit weight bearing + [[PT]]
> casting and bracing if refractory
> if 6+ femoral [[osteotomy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[SCFE]] is older than 10
> [[sickle cell anemia]] also avascular necrosis
