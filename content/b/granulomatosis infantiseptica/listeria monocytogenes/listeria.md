> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> unpasteurized dairy, cold deli meats
> impaired immunity from [[prednisone]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> cold growth on refrigerated foods
> actin polymerization motility directly cell to cell
> [[lysteriolysin O]]
> [[bacterial meningitis]] in immunocompromised, children, or elderly

> [!danger] Symptoms
> granulomatosis infantiseptica → abscesses all over baby

> [!question] Objective Findings
> intracellular bacteria not easily seen on CSF gram stain
