### 01 Somite
%%Describe somite formation: its origin, its four subdivisions, and its subsequent contributions to adult structures%% 
[[somite]] originates from paraxial mesoderm that extruded through the [[primitive streak]]. The four divisions are [[myotome]] [[sclerotome]] [[syndetome]] [[dermatome]]

[[intramembranous ossification]]: [[viscerocranium]]

### 03 Axial Skeleton
%%Describe the segmental origin of the axial skeletal (including primary and secondary sclerotomes); compare the origins of vertebra, annulus fibrosus, nucleus pulposus, ribs, & sternum%%
primary [[sclerotome]] surrounds the [[notocord]] first
primaries condense into segments
center of primary become [[intervertebral disc]]
primaries separated into secondaries
secondaries fuse with adjacent segments
ribs are an extension of secondary sclerotome
[[nucleus pulposis]] is a remainder of [[notocord]]

[[intramembranous ossification]] [[acromegaly]] [[neural tube defects]]
[[endochondral ossification]] [[achondroplasia]]

### 04 Muscle
%%Identify the origin and development of skeletal, smooth & cardiac muscle  %%
skeletal from [[myotome]]: [[hypomere]] ventral ramus [[epimere]] dorsal ramus
smooth from [[lateral plate mesoderm]]
cardiac from [[visceral mesoderm]]
iris from [[ectoderm]]
%%Clarify the role of connective tissue in guiding skeletal muscle development%%
spindled myocytes migrate from [[myotome]]
bundles are formed from local [[connective tissue]]
facial connective tissue is [[neural crest]]
body connective tissue is [[lateral plate mesoderm]]
[[torticollis]]

### 05 Limbs
%%Describe limb development along the three coordinate axes%%
[[somatic mesoderm]] + [[hypomere]] + [[ectoderm]] skin
proximal-distal axis
[[limb bud axes]]
anterior-posterior axis AKA radial-ulnar
dorsal-ventral axis
%%a. Define the organizing centers & molecular basis of patterning along each axis: %%
%%i. Apical ectodermal ridge (AER) secretes FGFs - Proximodistal axis  %%
[[FGF]]-secreing AER (apical ectodermal ridge) establishes cutting edge of the limb development "proximodistal axis"
%%ii. Zone of polarizaing activity (ZPA) secretes SHH - Anterioposterior axis  %%
[[SHH]]-secreting ZPA (zone of polarizing activity) establishes "anteroposterior AKA radioulnar axis" and spares the thumb
%%iii. Dorsal ectoderm secretes WNT7A - Dorsoventral axis  %%
[[WnT]]7a-secreting Dorsal Ectoderm establishes the "dorsoventral axis" and "dorsalizes" converts default flexors to extensors
%%b. Recognize how malformations can occur by axis disruption %%
[[syndactyly]] simple = soft, complex = bony
[[polydactyly]] can be a single extra digit or full mirroring
[[amelia]] 
[[sirenomelia]]
[[club foot]]
[[hip dysplasia]]
%%c. Explain limb rotation and the basis for the dermatome distribution%%
upper limb rotates slightly
lower limb starts "criss cross" rotates to face down
dermatomes result from maintained spinal innervation to skin
