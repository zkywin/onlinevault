[[skin cancer]] [[cancer]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> #1 skin cancer
> [[stratum basale]], upper lip
> superficial vs. nodular type

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> inactivating PTCH1 gene related to [[SHH]], [[p53]]
> UV-B sunlight
> [[albinism]]
> [[xeroderma pigmentosum]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> often at upper lip
> 0.1% metastasis

> [!danger] Symptoms
> elevated nodule with central ulceration
> [[telangiectasia]] vessels
> superficial type looks like a scaling [[plaque]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> nodules of basal cells
> peripheral palisading
> peri-tumoral mucin production

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[mohs surgery]]
> surgical excision
> good prognosis

> [!example] Relationships
> [[SCC]] more often metastasizes, is lower lip
> 34x [[melanoma]] incidence
