hypoxia-inducing factor
> [!tip] Function
> shifts metabolism to [[lactate fermentation]] instead of [[ETC]]
> induces [[EPO]]

> [!info] Mechanism
> hypoxia makes [[HIF]] alpha harder to degrade by [[PHD]] and it eventually dimerizes to HIF-2 and activates nuclear receptor
> increase [[glycolysis]] enzymes, decrease [[PDC]] and [[ETC]] enzymes
> prevent [[lactate]] buildup with [[NHE1]] and [[monocarboxylic acid transporter 4]]
> increases pH with [[ectoenzyme carbonic anhydrase IX]]

> [!warning] Structure

> [!example] Relationships
> [[PHD]] inhibitors are used for [[anemia]]
> increases [[PDGF]] and [[VEGF]] in [[RCC]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
