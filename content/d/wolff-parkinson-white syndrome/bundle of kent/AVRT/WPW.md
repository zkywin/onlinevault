[[EMERGENT]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology
> anatomic pathway past [[AV node]]
> causes either [[AVNRT]] or [[afib]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> bundle of kent bypasses [[AV node]]
> ventricular depolarization before [[AV node]] signal
> orthodromic AVRT: loop back to [[AV node]]
> antidromic AVRT: reverse loop toward [[AV node]]
> [[afib]] can cause a [[wide complex]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> asymptomatic

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↓[[pr interval]]
> delta wave (sloped "early ventricular excitation")
> ↑[[qrs complex]] (slow conduction?)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[procainamide]] [[ibutilide]] if stable
> [[defibrillation]] if unstable
> 
> DO NOT SLOW [[AV node]] (will cause other paths)
> ⦻ [[digoxin]] [[β1 blockers]] [[non-DHP CCB]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[AVNRT]] = AV node only
> AVRT = atria and ventricles
