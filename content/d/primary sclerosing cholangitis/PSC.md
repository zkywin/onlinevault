[[intrahepatic cholestasis]] + [[extrahepatic cholestasis]]
> [!tip] In Short
> more jaundice, dramatic
> in and out

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> 90% have [[UC]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[fibrosis]]/strictures of [[biliary tree]]
> strictures within and outside of liver
> future [[ascending cholangitis]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[RUQ pain]] [[fatigue]]
> [[UC]] with subsequent [[cholestasis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[p-ANCA]]
> high [[IgM]]
> onion skin fibrosis
> [[ERCP]] "beading" only for treatment

> [!abstract] Treatment
> endoscope stenting
> screen for [[cholangiocarcinoma]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[PBC]] is intrahepatic only and has more [[Ab]]
