[[ICP]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> brain swelling
> "high to low, brains blow"

> [!info] Etiology
> cytotoxic [[ischemic stroke]]
> vasogenic [[BBB]]
> interstitial [[hydrocephalus]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> cytotoxic: from [[ischemic stroke]]
> vasogenic from [[acute brain abscess]] or 
> interstitial from [[hydrocephalus]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> risk of herniation
> breathing control irregularity
> [[HTN]]
> [[bradycardia]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> flattened gyral convolutions
> brain weight over 1250g

> [!abstract] Treatment
> therapeutic [[hyperventilation]] → ↓[[PaCO2]] → vasoconstriction → lower blood volume
