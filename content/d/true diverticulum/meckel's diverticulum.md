[[vitelline defect]]
"rule of 2"
> [!tip] In Short
> persistent [[vitelline duct]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> 2%, 2M:F, 2 ft from ileocecal valve, 2 inches
> most common GI abnormality
> discovered young

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[heterotopia]] gastric tissue or pancreatic
> sometimes acid causes bleeding
> all layers of [[mucosa]] [[submucosa]] [[muscularis externa]]
> [[intussusception]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> asymptomatic
> bulge at the [[ileum]]
> painless red dark formed stool

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[technetium scintigraphy]]


> [!abstract] Treatment


> [!example] Relationships
> [[diverticulosis]] does not include [[muscularis externa]]
> [[FPIAP]] on differential
