[[PVD]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[femoral artery]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> "fontaine classification" (not commonly used)
> [[critical limb ischemia]]
> strong predictor of [[CAD]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> hair loss on extremities
> 
> stage 1: asymptomatic
> 2: pain on exertion
> 3: pain at rest
> 4: [[necrosis]] gangrene
> 
> [[internal iliac artery]]: [[ED]]
> [[femoral vein]]: calf pain
> 

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[ankle brachial index]] 0.4-0.9 (<0.9)
> then get [[angiogram]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> graded exercise → [[cilostazol]] → [[revascularization]]
> 
> [[aspirin]] + [[statins]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[coarctation of the aorta]]
