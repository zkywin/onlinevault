pigment nephropathy
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> rupture of [[skeletal muscle]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[opioid]] → ischemic compression
> severe [[dehydration]]
> [[seizure]]
> [[statins]] + [[cyclosporine]] [[gemfibrozil]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[NO]] constricts afferent [[glomerulus]] arterioles

> [!danger] Symptoms
> combined signs [[prerenal AKI]] and [[ATN]]
> [[hypokalemia]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> elevated [[CK]]
> [[dark urine]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> aggressive [[rehydration]]

> [!example] Relationships
> may be confused for [[hematuria]]
