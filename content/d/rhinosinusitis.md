
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> acute < 4wk
> chronic > 12wk

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[URI]], [[smoking]], [[DM]]
> mostly viral, [[smoking]], [[strep pneumoniae]], [[H flu]]
> bacterial: [[H flu]], [[strep pneumoniae]], [[moraxella]]

> septal deviation
> [[maxillary sinus]] or [[frontal sinus]] → middle meatus
> [[ethmoid sinus]] in children


> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> bacterial type lasts > 10 days
> mucus retention cysts
> [[cavernous sinus]] infection
> [[acute brain abscess]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> facial pain, tenderness to palpation

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> oral [[amoxicillin]]
> [[clindamycin]] if penicillin allergy
> [[ampicillin]] if inpatient
> [[supportive care]]
