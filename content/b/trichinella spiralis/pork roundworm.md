[[roundworm]]
> [!tip] In Short

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> undercooked pork
> undercooked bears/wildcats/etc.
> between animal through rats

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> encysted larvae in the meat
> intestinal stage
> tissues stage larval migration through muscle
> calcified cysts viable 30+ years

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[vomiting]]
> periorbital edema
> severe [[myalgia]]


> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[muscle biopsy]]
