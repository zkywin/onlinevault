[[sarcoma]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> HHV8 [[endothelium]] [[vessel tumor]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> european ashkenazi jew form: foot of men >50
> classic form: older medditerranean men
> african form: younger [[HIV]]
> iatrogenic form: [[transplant]] [[immunocompromised]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> poorly formed vessels
> extravasation of [[RBC]] on skin
> lymph node [[mets]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> purple [[patch]] to [[plaque]] to [[nodule]]
> raised [[purpura]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> does not blanch on pressure

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[ART]]

> [!example] Relationships
> lower grade than [[angiosarcoma]]
> may look like a [[purpura]]
