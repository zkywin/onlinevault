
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> intermittent sinus tachycardia

> [!info] Etiology
> [[AVNRT]] is the most common type

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[reentry circuit]] just above [[AV node]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> sudden [[palpitations]]
> lightheadedness
> diaphoresis

> [!question] Objective Findings
> HR > 150
> [[retrograde p wave]] or absent

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[vagal maneuver]] → ↓[[CN X]] → ↑[[parasympath]] → slow [[AV node]]
> [[adenosine]]
> [[cardiac ablation]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[tachycardia]] is < 150bpm
