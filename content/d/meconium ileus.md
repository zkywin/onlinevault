
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> "poop problem"
> blockage of baby's first stool (normally first 36 hrs)

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> 90% ∝ [[CF]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ≈ [[constipation]] "inspissated meconium"

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[bilious emesis]]
> failure to pass meconium in 48 hrs

> [!question] Objective Findings 
> gastrografin enema → microcolon (unused)
> dilated [[small intestine]]

> [!abstract] Treatment


> [!example] Relationships
> [[hirschsprung disease]]
> opposite =  [[meconium stained amniotic fluid]]
