[[neural tube]] defect
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> absent [[brain]] causes absent [[neurocranium]]

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[vB9 deficiency]]
> combo of multiple defects in the anterior [[neural tube]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> acrania/cranioschisis no [[neurocranium]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> "frog like appearance"
> protrusion of the eyes
> only meninges, glia, and vessels "area cerebrovasculosa"

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> allow spontaneous vaginal delivery

> [!example] Relationships
> [[polyhydramnios]] baby doesn't swallow amniotic fluid
> [[spina bifida]] [[spina bifida occulta]] are the same but at the other end
