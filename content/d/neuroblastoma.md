
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[neural crest]]

> [!info] Etiology
> mutations hard to characterize

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> "dancing eyes, dancing feet"
> arises at the [[sympathetic chain]]
> compression → [[horner syndrome]]
> epidural invasion → [[cord compression]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> abdominal mass that can cross midline
> [[opsoclonus myoclonus]]
> orbital metastases → [[raccoon eyes]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[VMA]] [[HVA]] in urine
> [[calcification]] of tumor
> bombesin, neuron specific enolase
> 
> ganglioneuroma all schwannian background
> ganglioneuroblastoma majority schwannian
> neuroblastoma [[neuropil]] or no stroma at all
> serum bombesin, neuron specific enolase

> [!abstract] Treatment
> observation/resection
> younger has better prognosis
> MYCN oncogene amplification poor prognosis
> 1p 11q heterozygosity loss poor prognosis

> [!example] Relationships
> [[wilms tumor]] at kidney, no calcification
> [[APS-1]]
