> [!info] Definitions
> AED: anti-epileptic drugs
> focal seizure AEDs are all the same and target Na+
> generalized seizure meds may differ based on the type and target Ca+

Conventional medications
[[phenytoin]] and [[carbamazepine]] both decrease Na+ current. [[c/phenobarbital]] and [[benzos]] both increase GABA Cl- current. [[ethosuximide]] decreases Ca+ current. Lastly, [[valproate]] does a combination of all of these.

Newer anticonvulsants
[[oxcarbazepine]] and [[lacosamide]] decrease Na+ current. [[lamotrigine]] and [[topirimate]]  block [[Glu receptor]] in addition to Na+. [[levetiracetam]] works on a vesicle glycoprotein, and [[gabapentin]] blocks Ca+ current.

Of these, [[valproate]], [[lamotrigine]], [[topirimate]], and [[levetiracetam]] work on both [[focal aware]] and [[1° general]]. [[ethosuximide]] only works for [[1° general]].

[[status epilepticus]] treated with IV formulations sequentially [[benzos]], [[c/fosphenytoin]], [[phenobarbital]].
