IPE
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> episodes of [[edema]] related to menstrual cycle

> [!info] Detailed Description
> early on is later part of cycle, becomes entire cycle
> high [[insulin]] causes [[Na-K pump]] activity, causing [[edema]]
> likely precapillary dilation and increased [[hydrostatic]] as well as [[NE]] [[RAAS]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> worsened [[edema]] with standing
> starts with [[pitting edema]] that later is non-pitting
> worsened high [[insulin]] secondary to [[anorexia nervosa]] or [[bulimia nervosa]]
> worsened by [[thiazide]] and [[loop]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> restrict [[Na+]] and carbohydrate intake
> [[ACEi]] [[ARB]]
