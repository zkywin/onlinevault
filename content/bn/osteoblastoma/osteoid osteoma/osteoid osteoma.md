[[benign neoplasm]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[osteoblast]] proliferation (osteoid)
> thin rim of reactive bone (osteoma)

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> young males
> osteoid osteoma [[long bone]] [[metaphysis]]
> osteoblastoma [[vertebrae]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> osteoid osteoma <2cm 
> osteoblastoma >2cm

> [!danger] Symptoms
> nocturnal bone pain

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[osteoblast]] proliferation within [[cortical bone]]
> bony mass with radiolucent core

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[aspirin]] resolves [[bone pain]] if < 2cm
> [[aspirin]]-refractory if osteoblastoma >2cm
> ablation or excision

> [!example] Relationships
> [[osteoma]] at face
> [[s/growing pains]]
> [[ewing sarcoma]] more in the bone
