[[cancer]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[dendritic cell]] tumor 

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> young [[children]] under 15

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> invasion of bone, skin, CNS ([[DI]])
> 
> punched-out bone erosion
> pathologic [[fracture]] in adolescent
> CNS [[pituitary]] involvement

> [!danger] Symptoms
> pain/swelling of [[skull]], [[ribs]]
> [[maculopapular rash]]
> [[hypernatremia]] [[polyuria]] [[polydipsia]]
> [[eczema]]
> lytic bone lesion
> [[diaper rash]]
> recurrent [[otitis media]] in jaw mass

> [!question] Objective Findings
> normal [[Ca2+]] levels
> [[CD1]] [[S100]] [[CD207]] [[macrophage]]
> ↑↑[[eosinophil]], [[neutrophil]], [[lymphocyte]], [[plasma cell]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> stop [[smoking]]

> [!example] Relationships
> lytic lesions look like [[multiple myeloma]]
