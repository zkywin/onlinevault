hypotonic, isovolemic hyponatremia
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> serum [[Na+]] < 136
> [[Sosm]] < 280 mOsm

> [!info] Etiology
> HYPERtonic* [[translocational hyponatremia]]: [[hyperglycemia]] 
> HYPOtonic [[hypovolemic hyponatremia]]: [[diuretic]], [[secretory diarrhea]]
> HYPOtonic [[hypervolemic hyponatremia]]: low [[EABV]]/[[RAAS]] and [[GFR]]
> HYPOtonic isovolemic: high [[free water]] [[SIADH]]
> 
> *HYPERtonic implies translocational, no[](SIADH.md)hyponatremia

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> impaired H2O excretion [[SIADH]], [[primary polydipsia]], [[1° adrenal insufficiency]], [[exercise induced hyponatremia]]
> small increase in [[EAB[](SIADH.md)ns hyponatremia via lower [[RAAS]] and higher [[ANP]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[confusion]], lethargy, [[vomiting]]
> 
> when acute, [[cerebral edema]], [[brain herniation]]
> when chronic, cells adapt by exporting [[Na+]] [[K+]] [[amino acid]] into [[interstitium]]
> no heart issues

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[Una+]] and [[Uosm]] in renal problems

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[water restriction]]
> if severe, [[3% NaCl solution]]
> 
> rapid correction →  [[central pontine myelinolysis]] 

> [!example] Relationships
> [[hypokalemia]] [[hyperkalemia]] cause electrolyte disturbances
> [[isotonic hyponatremia]] artifact caused by high lipids in serum
> [[translocational hyponatremia]] artifact is just [[hyperglycemia]]
> [[cerebral salt wasting]]
