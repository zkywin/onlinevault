[[normocytic anemia]] -> [[microcytic anemia]]
> [!tip] In Short
> poor use of iron

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> mild anemia of any chronic inflammation

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> lower [[RBC]] survival
> [[hepcidin]] lowers [[EPO]] and [[ferroportin]]
> ↑ [[ferritin]] storage in [[macrophage]]
> low [[Fe]] availability 

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> ↑ [[hepcidin]]
> ↑ [[ferritin]] ↓ [[transferrin]] ↓ [[transferrin sat]]
> ↓ [[reticulocyte]]
> ↑ [[protoporphyrin]]
> [[Hb]] > 10g/dL (not too low)

> [!abstract] Treatment
> treat the underlying disease
> usually incidentally found
> [[Fe]] does not help

> [!example] Relationships
> [[occult blood]] → [[Fe deficiency]] is more microcytic
