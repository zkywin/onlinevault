Projection fibers from the cortex to subcortical areas include the [[a/corona radiata]] and all parts of the internal capsule. [[a/anterior internal capsule]] is about coordination, [[genu]] is on the face, and [[posterior internal capsule]] takes care of body sensation and motor.

Association fibers like [[arcuate fasciculus]] connect diffent areas of cortex.
