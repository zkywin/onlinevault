trochlear nerve
> [!tip] Function
> look down and out

> [!example] Relationships
> [[superior oblique]]

> [!info] Location
> exits the [[pons]] out the back

> [!warning] Structure
> the only cranial nerve to cross! (not clinically significant)
> [[CN III]] [[CN VI]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> affected eye deviates upward
> to tell if [[CN IV]] accompanies [[CN VI]] palsy, when eye is abducted, no incyclotorsion
> most damages are to the nerve at the back of the [[pons]] not the nucleus at the [[midbrain]]
