[[seizure]] includes a variety of responses to cortical interference. the types are based on different results of [[EEG]] measurement.

[[focal aware]] are like prodromes, [[focal impaired]] makes a person goofy, and [[focal to general]] is a full tonic-clonic seizure. Shaking is also seen in [[syncope]] but those cases last only seconds. Meanwhile [[PNES]] is more psychogenic than neurologic.

[[1° general]] is more common in children than adults, and should be separated from other pediatric disorders such as [[absence seizure]], [[benign childhood epilepsy with centrotemporal spikes]] and [[juvenile myoclonic epilepsy]]. Especially in children, [[status epilepticus]] can last for a long time and risk lasting damage.

Continuous versions include [[non-convulsive status epilepticus]] which is hard to pin down and chronic [[focal status epilepticus]]. #done
