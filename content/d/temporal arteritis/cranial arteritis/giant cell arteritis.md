[[EMERGENT]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[internal carotid]] + [[ophthalmic artery]]

> [!info] Etiology
> 50+ years females 

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> severity ∝ [[IL-6]]
> damage to [[elastic artery]] [[common carotid]] and branches
> segmental buildup
> [[giant cell]]
> [[amaurosis fugax]] → [[AION]]
> [[aortic dissection]]
> [[polymyalgia rheumatica]] in 50%
> 10-20% risk of [[thoracic aortic aneurysm]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[headache]]
> [[jaw claudication]] [[claudication]]
> [[AION]] blindness

> [!question] Objective Findings
> high [[ESR]]
> biopsy of a very LONG vessel to catch the vasculitis
> [[fibrosis]] between [[vessel intima]] and [[vessel media]]
> "halo sign" special vasculitis [[US]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[corticosteroids]] or [[MTX]]
> [[toculizumab]]
> take [[angiogram]]

> [!example] Relationships
> unlike [[polyarteritis nodosa]] vasculitis, giant cell is cell-mediated
