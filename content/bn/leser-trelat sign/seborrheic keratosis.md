[[benign neoplasm]]
[[paraneoplastic]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> rubbery oily nodule

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> common in middle age and older
> [[gastric adenocarcinoma]] "leser-trelat sign" sudden multiple

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[keratinocyte]] proliferation

> [!danger] Symptoms
> waxy to warty texture
> "stuck on" appearance
> [[plaque]] on face

> [!question] Objective Findings
> pseudocyst with [[keratin]] "horn cysts"

> [!abstract] Treatment
> check for a GI malignancy

> [!example] Relationships
> [[acanthosis nigricans]]
> [[actinic keratosis]] more closely related to [[SCC]]
