
> [!tip] Function

> [!info] Location
> use lowest [[FiO2]] to achieve [[SpO2]] > 90%
> lower [[PaCO2]] using [[Vt]] or [[RR]]

> [!warning] Structure
> AC/Volume Control: same [[Vt]] every time
> Pressure Control: patient sets rate; assisted
> 
> [[CPAP]]: patient initiates, ventilator constant pressure

> [!example] Relationships
> [[PEEP]]
> when weaning, ↑[[RR]] ↓[[Vt]]
> [[peak inspiratory pressure|peak inspiratory pressure]] = [[resistive pressure]] + [[plateau pressure]]

> [!danger] Pathologies
> worsens [[tension pneumothorax]]
