> [!tip] Condensed Description
> a sequence of [[ischemic stroke]] disconnects the cortex

> [!info] Etiology
> moderate [[hypoxic brain injury]]
> [[vasculitis]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> often co-occurs with [[alzheimer's]]
> residual deficits after [[ischemic stroke]] are a big risk factor
> [[lacunar stroke]] [[pseudobulbar palsy]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> notable focal neurological deficits
> [[urinary incontinence]]
> [[gait]] problems
> neuropsych problems

> [!question] Objective Findings
> MRI [[white matter intensities]]

> [!abstract] Treatment

> [!example] Relationships
> pure [[alzheimer's]] does not have evidence of strokes
> [[MDD]] after stroke has more sadness less apathy
