
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> ankle deformation
> foot faces inward

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> [[oligohydramnios]] [[potter sequence]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> positional clubfoot: only in certain fetal positions
> congenital clubfoot: talus inherent defect

> [!danger] Symptoms
> bone deformation, cannot be fixed without surgery

> [!question] Objective Findings

> [!abstract] Treatment
> serial molding casts like braces

> [!example] Relationships
> metatarsus adductus is a byproduct of in-utero position, is correctable
> [[bent toe]] is separate
