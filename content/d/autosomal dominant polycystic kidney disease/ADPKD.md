chromosome 4
[[autosomal dominant]] adult PKD
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[renal cyst]] everywhere throughout the kidney

> [!info] Etiology
> presents in adults age 30-40
> mutation APKD1, APKD2 or [[de novo]]
> proliferation of cells anywhere on [[nephron]]
> a few cysts compress other [[nephron]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> cyst expansion → local ischemia → ↑[[renin]]
> [[berry aneurysm]] at brain! (poor vascular integrity)
> [[mitral prolapse]]
> hepatic cyst liver
> [[2° HTN]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> recurrent flank pain
> asymptomatic in childhood
> bilateral upper abdominal masses
> [[UTI]], [[HTN]]
> [[ESRD]] after cysts enlarge

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[US abdomen]]
> many spherical [[renal cyst]] with [[calcification]]
> increased [[renin]]
> venous [[US]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[tolvaptan]]


> [!example] Relationships
> [[secondary amyloidosis]] has [[CKD]] [[splenomegaly]]
> [[multicystic dysplastic kidney]] [[renal dysplasia]]
> [[pheochromocytoma]] is intermittent [[HTN]]
