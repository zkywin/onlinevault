
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[REM sleep]] intrusion into wakefulness

> [!info] Detailed Description
> HLA-DQB1.0602 in 90% of cases

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> lateral [[hypothalamus]] autoimmunity
> ↓[[orexin]] type 1
> wake → [[REM sleep]] without [[NREM sleep]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> daytime somnolence
> cataplexy (triggered by emotion)
> sleep paralysis
> hallucinations

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[multiple sleep latency test]]
> low [[CSF]] levels of hypocretin 1

> [!abstract] Treatment
> ★ [[modafinil]] (non-amphetamine)
> [[amphetamines]] 
> [[TCA]] [[SSRI]]
> nighttime [[sodium oxybate]] [[ptiolisant]]

> [!example] Relationships
> fighting [[strep pneumoniae]] or [[b/H1N1]] may trigger it
