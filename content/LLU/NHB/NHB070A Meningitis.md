

Elevated leukocytes and protein with normal glucose implies [[viral meningitis]], which usually goes away on its own. [[coxsackie B virus]] and [[echovirus]] are fecal-oral in children, [[mumps]] is respiratory in unvaccinated children, [[west nile virus]] is from mosquitos, and [[lymphocytic choriomeningitis virus]] is related to rodent excrement. [[HSV-1]] is in adults and can follow bouts of [[HSV-1]] or [[d/genital herpes]]

Elevated leukocytes with low glucose level implies [[bacterial meningitis]]. Notably, of the two [[chocolate agar]] culturable causes [[meningococcus]] is seen in college age adults and [[H flu]] in infants. [[listeria]] is capable of cold growth. [[strep pneumoniae]] lacks bells and whistles and is much more opportunistic.

The same CSF findings apply to fungal meningitis such as [[cryptococcal meningitis]], which happens in AIDS and cancer patients. #done
