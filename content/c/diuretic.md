
> [!info] Mechanism
> 1 natriuresis (increase [[Na+]] excretion)
> 2 diuresis (increase [[UOP]])

> [!danger] Uses
> lower [[BP]] 10-15 mmHg
> [[HTN]], [[edema]], [[CHF]], [[pulmonary edema]]
> [[CKD]]

> [!abstract] Formulations
> [[carbonic anhydrase inhibitors]]
> [[osmotic diuretic]]
> [[loop]]
> [[thiazide]]
> [[K-sparing diuretic]]
> [[ADH antagonist]]

> [!warning] Metabolism
> most are secreted at [[proximal tubule]] and require good [[GFR]]
> [[spironolactone]] need not enter lumen
> [[osmotic diuretic]] filters directly


> [!failure] Adverse Effects
> all cause [[hypokalemia]]
> [[loop]] [[hypernatremia]] water loss dilute urine
> [[thiazide]] [[hyponatremia]] salt loss
> [[metabolic alkalosis]]
> [[metabolic acidosis]]

> [!example] Interactions
> decreased [[Na+]] reuptake (>40mEq in urine)
> increased [[K+]] secretion [[hypokalemia]]
> low [[Mg2+]]
