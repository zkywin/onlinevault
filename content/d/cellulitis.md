
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> deep [[dermis]] and subcutaneous fat infection

> [!info] Etiology
> caused by break in the skin barrier, insect/animal bites
> [[MSSA]] purulent type
> [[GAS]] non purulent type
> [[vibrio vulnificus]] saltwater
> [[aeromonas hydrophila]] freshwater
> [[pasteurella multocida]] cats
> [[actinomyces israelii]] jaw

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[necrotizing fasciitis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> fluctuance: tense/boggy skin, likely pus underneath

> [!abstract] Treatment
> skin culture → [[Abx]]
> antibiotics for strep

> [!example] Relationships
> [[erysipelas]]
> [[rheumatic fever]]
> [[d/orbital cellulitis]]
> [[intertrigo]]
> [[d/finger pad abscess/felon]]
