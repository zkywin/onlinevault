[[d/diabetes mellitus/DM]] [[HHS]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[insulin]] resistance

> [!info] Etiology
> family history risk
> adults [[obesity]] "apple shape viscerally fat"
> [[CF]] [[prader-willi syndrome]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> ↓ [[insulin]] sensitivity
> [[hyperglycemia]]
> [[pancreas]] eventually fails to compensate
> release of free [[fatty acid]]
> serine threonine kinases inhibit [[insulin receptor]]
> [[localized amyloidosis]] of [[amylin]]
> late ⦻ [[β cell]]


> [!danger] Symptoms
> [[polyuria]]
> [[polydipsia]]
> [[acanthosis nigricans]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> [[glucola]] screening if > 35
> [[amylin]] in [[pancreatic islet]] confused with [[amyloid]]
> [[A1C]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[metformin]]
> [[sulfonureas]] [[meglitinides]]
> [[TZDs]] [[pramlintide]]
> [[glucosidase inhibitors]] [[SGLT2 blocker]]
> [[GLP-1s]] [[DPP4 inhibitors]]
> weight loss

> [!example] Relationships
> [[insulin]] presence prevents [[DKA]]
