
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> cartilage under [[patella]] wears down

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> women runners
> [[trauma]]
> weak [[quadriceps femoris]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> atrophy of [[quadriceps femoris]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> pain at front of knee
> worse with flexion

> [!question] Objective Findings
> move patella from side to side
> patellofemoral compression test

> [!abstract] Treatment
> stretch/strengthen [[quadriceps femoris]] [[gluteus medius]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[chondromalacia patellae]] presents similarly
