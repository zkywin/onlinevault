Devic's disease
demyelinating disorder
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> antibodies against [[aquaporin 4]] on astrocytes

> [!info] Detailed Description
> seen in japanese descent patients
> preferential spinal cord and optic nerve involvement

> [!danger] Symptoms
> vomiting from [[area postrema]] involvement
> necrotic lesions [[transverse myelitis]]
> bilateral [[optic neuritis]]  but WITHOUT brain involvement
> [[respiratory failure]]

> [!question] Tests
> [[AQP4 Antibodies]]
> CSF pleocytosis
> MRI lesion all up to the [[a/area postrema]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[c/rituximab]]

> [!example] Relationships
> affects cervical cord, while [[MS]] is thoracic cord
