[[amyloid]]
chromosome 21
> [!tip] Condensed Description

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> differing cleavage of [[APP]] can affect cognitive function

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> Rise in insoluble, nonfunctional Amyloid beta 42
> Drop in soluble, active, Aβ42 can occur up to 25 years prior to symptom onset

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> eosinophilic hyaline material
> [[congo red]] 7-10mm non-branching fibrils
> comprise extracellular [[senile plaques]]

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[alzheimer's]] lowering amyloid beta seems to have little effect

> [!example] Relationships
> higher expression in [[down syndrome]]
