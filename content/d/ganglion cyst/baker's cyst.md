[[bursitis]]
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> herniation of [[synovium]] of knee posteriorly

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> arthropathies
> knee trauma

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[connective tissue]] outpouching "pseudocyst"
> rupture →  sudden pain, crescent-shaped ecchymosis

> [!danger] Symptoms

> [!question] Objective Findings
> paucicellular mucin contents

> [!abstract] Treatment
> observation
> aspiration only if compression of nerves
> if serious, excision

> [!example] Relationships
> [[baker's cyst]]
> may present like a [[DVT]]
