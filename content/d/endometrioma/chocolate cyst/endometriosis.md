2° [[dysmenorrhea]]
> [!tip] In Short
> [[sex hormones]] sensitive
> ectopic [[endometrium]] tissue

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> retrograde flow of [[menstrual cycle]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> can cause uterus shrinking
> 
> [[ovary]] [[fallopian tubes]]
> uterosacral ligaments
> rectovaginal septum
> [[peritoneum]] [[bladder]]
> [[ovarian epithelial tumors]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> pain throughout the cycle
> risk of [[infertility]] due to inflammation
> [[menorrhagia]]
> [[dysmenorrhea]]
> [[dyspareunia]]
> [[dyschezia]]
> [[dysuria]] if in bladder

> [!question] Objective Findings
> 🟊 [[laparotomy]]
> negative [[sliding sign]]
> 
> nodules at posterior fornix
> normal [[uterus]] size
> "chocolate cysts" at the ovary

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[laparascopy]] diagnostic and therapeutic
> [[TAH]] [[BSO]]
> [[elagolix]]
> [[NSAID]] [[OCP]] (especially [[progestin]])
> [[leuprolide]] [[danazol]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[adenomyosis]]
