[[menstrual irregularity]]
> [!tip] In Short
> failure of [[menstruation]]
> 1°: never periods > 13 or > 15 with characteristics (anatomic/genetic)
> 2°: cessation of periods >3 mo prev regular or > 6 mo prev irregular

> [!info] Etiology/Risk Factors
> primary [[mullerian agenesis]] [[turner syndrome]] [[imperforate hymen]]
> secondary: 
> 🟊 [[pregnancy]] [[hypothyroidism]] [[prolactinoma]]
> [[hyperthyroidism]] 
>  [[hypercortisolism]]
> [[hypothalamic amenorrhea]]
> [[anorexia nervosa]]/[[malnutrition]] low [[GnRH]]
> [[menopause]]
> [[PCOS]]
> [[asherman syndrome]]
> [[cervical stenosis]]
> shortly after [[OCP]] discontinuation

> [!question] Objective Findings
