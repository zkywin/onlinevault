[[autosomal recessive]] chromosome 7
> [!tip] Condensed Description
> [[CFTR]] absence

> [!info] Etiology
> [[Phe]]508 deletion[[Δ]]

> [!warning] Pathogenesis
> [[pancreatic insufficiency]]
> misfolded protein in [[R-ER]]
> low [[Cl-]] secretion
> high [[Na+]] absorption
> dehydrated lumen
> skin-related [[hyponatremia]] [[RAAS]]
> [[hypokalemia]] [[metabolic alkalosis]]
> recurrent [[MSSA]], [[pseudomonas]]
> ↓[[glucagon]] + [[DM]] → [[hypoglycemia]]

> [!danger] Symptoms
> salty sweat (failed Cl- reabsorption)
> thick, dehydrated, GI mucus (failed Cl- secretion)
> [[bronchiectasis]]
> pancreatic insufficiency
> [[meconium ileus]]
> [[infertility]] in men (atresia of vas deferens)
> subfertility in women
> [[fat malabsorption]]
> [[cirrhosis]]

> [!question] Objective Findings
> salty sweat (test aided by [[pilocarpine]])
> [[immunoreactive trypsinogen]]
> [[nasal TEPD]] negative
> [[digital clubbing]]
> nasal polyp
> Cl > 60 mEq/L

> [!abstract] Treatment
> [[pancrelipase]]
> [[azithromycin]]
> nebulized [[dornase alfa]]
> nebulized [[5% NaCl solution]]
> [[albuterol]]
> [[chest physiotherapy]]

> [!example] Relationships
> [[eNaC]] sodium follows suit with [[ATP-gated chloride channel]]
